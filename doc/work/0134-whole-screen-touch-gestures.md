# 0134: The touch overlay without gamepad buttons

Status: todo

## Goal

Asked on 2026-09-30, in two steps: "I want the whole screen to react to taps and tap-and-hold for placing and mining blocks, respectively, right now it seems like its only the right hand side that has this behavior? With this i think we can remove the L2 and R2 overlay buttons completely? Along with the L1 and R1", then "lets drop the rest of the gamepad buttons, instead of A to jump lets have a tap in the rightmost 20 % of the screen be jump (tapping the rest 80 % places blocks or uses tools as usual). Inventory screen reachable from a button next to the hotbar with a nice backpack icon". Today (0118) the tap scheme runs on the free look half only, the left half is the floating stick's, and the overlay copies GameNative's buttons: LT, RT (place, mine), LB, RB (hotbar previous, next), A (jump, interact), B (sneak), X (inventory), Y (rotate), L3 (sprint), Back (bound to the map) and Start (pause).

## Change

- Every finger that lands on free screen (not on a HUD button, not on a hotbar slot, not inside a static stick's base) is undecided at first (`Pending`), on both halves. It becomes a drag once it moves past `TOUCH_TAP_SLOP`: the stick when it landed on the stick's side and the stick is free (centred where it landed, taking the whole drag so far, as the look drag does today), else the look drag. It becomes a hold once it rests `TOUCH_HOLD_SECONDS` inside the slop (Mine, aimed at the finger), and a lift before either is a tap. A tap in the rightmost 20 percent of the screen's width is Jump (`SOUTH` through the bindings, which Interact shares; the jump zone presses it with no aim, so a target under the finger there is not interacted with); a tap elsewhere is Interact when the target takes it and Place or Use_Item otherwise, as today. The jump zone's width is a key of the `look` element (`jump_zone_share`, 0.2, 0 for none), validated like the others. A static stick (0120) keeps taking only the touches inside its base; the rest of its half is free screen.
- The `crosshair` scheme (`settings.touch_interaction`) keeps the gestures and changes only the aim: the hold and the tap act at the view's centre. No scheme needs a button.
- HUD touch buttons (`hud.odin`, drawn while the overlay drives the world, on the touch pointer): beside the hotbar a backpack button opens the inventory (`Open_Inventory`) and a pause button opens the pause menu (`Pause`); while the selected hotbar slot holds a building that rotates, a rotate button (`Rotate_Building`) shows next to them. They are overlay elements in effect: a finger landing on one is that button's for its life (role `Button`), it claims the pointer like the pills do, and the overlay presses the bound gamepad control, so the actions stay bindings. The backpack icon is a new `data/ui/icons/backpack.png` (check whether `inventory.png` already is one; if not, draw a placeholder by script as the other icons were, 0071), the pause and rotate icons likewise. `test_no_touch_overlay_element_covers_a_hotbar_slot` covers the HUD buttons.
- `data/touch_overlay.sjson`: every button leaves the Default layout (LT, RT, LB, RB, A, B, X, Y, L3, Back, Start); the stick and the look element stay. The controls stay known (`touch_overlay_control_from_name`), so a user layout with buttons still loads and the editor can still add and rebind buttons. The rule that a user layout needs a START button (0121, `touch_overlay_start_problem`) goes, since the HUD's pause button is always there on touch; the loader and the editor stop checking it, and the doc says why.
- Sneak has no touch control after this; a user layout can add a B button. Sprint stays the stick's rim. Hotbar cycling stays the hotbar's taps. Drop stays the hotbar's long press.
- Docs: `doc/input.md` (Fingers, Tap to interact, the layout description, the layouts and editor rules, the table rows for the buttons on touch), `doc/ui.md` (the HUD buttons), `DESIGN.md` Input paragraph (the overlay no longer copies GameNative's button layout), `doc/log/2026-09-30.md` with the assumptions: the stick's slop start, the hold on the stick's side (a thumb resting a quarter second before pushing mines once under it; to be judged on the phone), the crosshair scheme's aim, the jump zone's share, sneak unbound on touch, the START rule dropped.

## Verify

- `./build.sh check`, `./build.sh check-android`, `./build.sh test`. Tests (`touch_overlay_test.odin`, the frame driven tests of 0118 to 0120 as the pattern): a tap on the left half places; a hold on the left half mines and aims at the finger; a finger on the left half that moves past the slop drives the stick with the whole drag so far and never taps; a second finger on the left half while the stick is held becomes the look drag; a tap in the rightmost 20 percent presses SOUTH with no aim; a tap at 79 percent places; a tap outside a static stick's base on its half places; in `crosshair` a hold aims at the view's centre; the Default layout has no button; a user layout with an LT button and without START still loads; the backpack, pause and rotate buttons press their controls and claim the pointer; the rotate button shows only while the selected slot holds a rotatable building; the HUD buttons cover no hotbar slot.
- The user, on the phone: tap and hold anywhere to place and mine, jump with a tap at the right edge, walk with the stick, sprint over the rim, open the inventory and the pause menu from the hotbar's buttons, rotate a belt with the rotate button.
