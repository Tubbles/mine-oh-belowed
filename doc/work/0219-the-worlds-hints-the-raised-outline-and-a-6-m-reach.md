# 0219: The world's hints, the raised footprint outline and a 6 m reach

Status: verified (2026-10-04, fix round done on item/0219; worktree `.claude/worktrees/0219` on `item/0219` from `main`, the specification approved the same day with the decisions below; the user's playtest notes after 0215: "I don't see some bindings when playing with mouse and keyboard, eg. Q is missing. Also make the 'footprint ghost' extend a little upwards into the air, since now it clips into the terrain and is tricky to see. could we also extend the build distance by 50%"; folds 0217)

## Goal

Three playtest fixes in the HUD and the field placement. The glyph bar tells a keyboard player what Q, R and the mouse buttons do with an item held: today the world's bar shows Inventory and Pause plus at most one context hint (`hud.odin`, the cases after `tools_radial`), never the tools radial, Rotate or Place, so a keyboard player never learns Q. The footprint outline (0215) rises off the ground so a slope cannot hide it. The reach grows by half, from 4 m to 6 m.

## Change

- **The world's glyph bar.** The hints, in this order, each shown only when its action does something now: the aimed thing's action as today (Interact's Turn, Open or Close; Mine's Pick up or Fell; the schematic's hints); then the held item's actions: Place (the `Use_Item` glyph, "Place") with a placeable tool held (`Field_Held_Tool` Material, Foundation, Belt_Run, Pipe_Run, Machine, Torch), Tools (a new `Glyph_Button.Tools` on the `Pipette` action, "Tools": Q on the keyboard, D-pad Up on the pad) with any item held, Turn (the Rotate glyph) with a tool `Rotate_Building` turns on the field (the design stage reads which); then Sprint as today, Inventory and Pause.
- **The fit (0217 folded).** The bar is laid out in the room between the hotbar's right edge and the safe area's right edge, never over a hotbar slot: hints that do not fit wrap into a second row above the first, both right aligned, and beyond two rows the bar drops from the end. The world's bar therefore sheds Pause and then Inventory first (constant, learnt in the first minute), the opposite of the menus' rule that keeps Back (`ui.md`, The glyph bar), and the placement editor's four stay whole on two rows at the smallest audit size. The UI audit gets a case per bar at every size and the test asserts no glyph box intersects a hotbar slot.
- **The raised outline.** The outline's four edges become low walls, `PLACEMENT_OUTLINE_HEIGHT_CELLS` one cell tall (0.5 m at the shipped pitch), see through below an opaque top rim, and the front arrow is drawn at the walls' top; the design stage checks the height against the bare ground flatness limit over the largest footprint (250 mm over 5 m) and the editor's own ghost, and says the margin. The foundation ghost per cell (0215) is unchanged.
- **The reach.** `tool_reach_millimetres` in `data/game.sjson` goes from 4000 to 6000; its bound (1 to `MAXIMUM_FIELD_PLAYER_LENGTH_MILLIMETRES`) holds. The aim's raycast steps a quarter sample, so the longer ray costs a few more steps a tick; the bury rule and the centre rule are unchanged. Tests and docs that state the 4 m reach follow (the design stage greps `tool_reach` and "4 m").

## Controls

No binding changes. The glyph bar shows existing bindings only: the first binding of each action on the active device, as `glyph` reads it (0151). This is the keybinding pass: nothing gains or changes a meaning.

## Specification (design, 2026-10-04)

Nothing here changes a binding, a save layout, the record or the lockstep input. The widths below are the test's widths: the audit's `Ui_State` has no fonts, so text is measured by `approximate_text_width` (0.37 of the size a character, the size times the text scale), the same as in every hint test.

### Answers to the item's open choices

- **Which tools show Turn.** On the field `Rotate_Building` becomes `Next_Brush` (`simulation_field.odin`, the action table at line 116), and `update_field_held_tool` turns `placement_rotation` only for `Field_Held_Tool.Machine`; for every other tool it cycles the brush. In the placement editor's outline mode the press still reaches the world and turns the outline (`placement_editor_world_frame` removes Rotate only while anchored). So Turn shows for `.Machine` alone. Place shows for every tool but `.Hand` (Material, Foundation, Belt_Run, Pipe_Run, Machine, Torch), as the item lists.
- **Where the held hints apply.** Only in a field session. `Field_Player.tool` is zero (`.Material`) in a block session, where it would show a false Place, and Pipette does nothing anywhere today (log 2026-10-04), so the radial's only use is the field's placement editor. A new `Hud_Context.field_session` carries it.
- **The aimed machine's Open.** Where the Inventory binding opens the aimed panel (`inventory_hint_key` returns `hint_open`), that hint is part of the aimed thing's hints (kept, in today's place after Turn or Pick up), and the trailing constant Inventory is left out, since it is the same binding. Elsewhere the trailing hint reads Inventory and can be shed.
- **The wrap rule.** Rows are filled greedily from the start of the list: the first row (the bottom one) takes hints in order while their width fits, the rest go to the second row above it, both right aligned. If more than two rows would be needed, the last hint is dropped and the rows are laid again, so the world's bar sheds Pause, then Inventory, then Sprint, then held hints from the end.
- **The room beside the hotbar is too narrow at the large UI scales, so the bar has a second place.** The room is the safe right edge minus (the last slot's right edge plus `3 * UI_GAP`, the touch row's gap). Measured in UI units: 482 at 1920 by 1080 scale 1, 338 at scale 1.2, 194 at scale 1.5, 396, 266 and 136 at 1280 by 800 scales 1, 1.2 and 1.5, 325 on the Deck preset, 347 at 2880 by 1920, 482 at the three split screen sizes but 1346 at 1920 by 540. At 1280 by 800 scale 1.5 with the largest text (1.6), one keyboard hint ("Right mouse" Place, 289) is twice the room, and even the gamepad's Cancel (152) does not fit. So the item's "the editor's four stay whole on two rows at the smallest audit size" cannot hold beside the hotbar. The rule: the bar stands **beside** the hotbar when its kept hints (the editor's four; on the world the aimed and held hints, at least the first hint) fit there in two rows without a drop. Otherwise it stands **above** the hotbar: right aligned to the safe right edge, as wide as from the hotbar's left edge to the safe right edge (876 at the smallest size, so it never reaches over the craft queue's column on the left), its bottom row's bottom `UI_GAP` above the held item's name row (`safe bottom - UI_SLOT_SIZE * HUD_SELECTED_SLOT_SCALE - UI_GAP - UI_ROW_HEIGHT - UI_GAP`), the same two rows and the same drop. The editor's four there: Move 124, Turn 118, Place 289, Cancel 289 in one row is 892, more than 876, so they wrap to Move, Turn, Place and Cancel above. The world's furnace bar there: Place, Tools, Turn and Inventory in one row (816), Pause in the second.
- **The outline's height.** One cell, 500 mm at the shipped pitch. A bare ground placement's frame stands at the aimed hit, which is the footprint's centre cell, and the footprint's four corners and centre must read within `bare_ground_flatness_millimetres` (250) of each other, so on any accepted bare placement the ground at a corner rises at most 250 mm over the outline's floor, over the largest footprint (the 5 m stone furnace) too. The wall top stands 510 mm over the floor (the 0.02 cell lift plus the cell), so at least 260 mm of wall and the whole rim show at the worst corner: the margin is 260 mm, half the wall. Between the five sampled points the surface can rise more; the margin covers those bumps. A placement refused Too steep can bury a wall, but its outline is red. On a foundation the outline stands on the frame's top and nothing buries it. The anchored ghost replaces the outline (`draw_placement_editor_ghost` switches on the kind), so the walls never overlap the model ghost. The chevron goes from 0.04 to 1.0 cells over the floor.
