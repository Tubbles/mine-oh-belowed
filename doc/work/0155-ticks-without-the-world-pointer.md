# 0155: Tick procedures without the world pointer

Status: implemented

## Goal

Queue entry 3 of the architecture audits (`doc/audit/simulation.md`, refactor 7). After 0154 the kind ticks reach the records explicitly; what they still need from `^World` is the block store (reads at 26 sites, writes at 4) and the entity pools. For the engine cut, the game's tick takes its own state and a block interface, never the chunk store.

## Change

- One tick context struct (the entities, the game records, the content, the tick, the block query and the block write) that `tick_entities` builds once and the kind ticks take instead of `^World`; the block query and write are the two procedures the plugin boundary will carry.
- `tick_entities` takes the context; the player tick, which moves through blocks, keeps `^World` for now and is named in the item's notes as the next cut.
- `doc/architecture.md` (the simulation) states the shape; `doc/code_map.md` records lowered (simulation -> world falls by the block reads that go through the context).

## Verify

- `./build.sh check`, `./build.sh check-android`, `./build.sh check-windows`, `./build.sh test`, `python3 tools/check_docs.py`, `python3 tools/code_graph.py --check doc/code_map.md`; the factory benchmark's two sizes in the suite still pass with the same state hash as before the change (the implementer records the hashes).

## Implementation notes

- `Entity_Tick_Context` (`simulation_world.odin`): `entities`, `records`, `content`, `tick_rate`, `settings` (a copy of `World_Settings`: the drills read the seed and `veins_infinite`, the furnaces, assemblers and the electric demand read `byproducts_lenient`), the vein registry's fields `veins`, `vein_indices`, `column_veins`, `outcrop_cells` and `spent_outcrops` (still on `World`; the drills draw from and exhaust veins, the electric demand and the core sample drill look them up, the outcrop step reads them), and the block interface `block_data` with `query_block: Block_Query` (`proc(data, position) -> (block, loaded)`, an unloaded cell reads air) and `write_block: Block_Write` (`proc(data, position, block) -> bool`). `world_tick_context` builds it; `world_query_block` and `world_write_block` are the world's implementations (`world_cell`, `world_set_block`). The goal's "26 reads, 4 writes" were the simulation cluster's counts; the entity tick itself had fewer.
- Block sites moved (before: 4 loaded checks through `not_in world.chunks`, 7 block reads through `world_get_block` or `world_water_level`, 1 write through `world_set_block`; after: 8 `tick_get_block` calls and 1 `tick_set_block`): `belt_end_drop_cell` (loaded check and solid read, `belt_movement.odin`), `cell_holds_loose_items` (1 read), `loose_item_can_fall` (loaded check and 2 water reads, `loose_item.odin`), `flowing_water_level_sum` (the hydro turbine's water, `power_machine.odin`), `dominant_band_block` (loaded check and read, `prospecting.odin`), `apply_spent_outcrops` (read and write, moved from `world_vein.odin` to `simulation_world.odin` beside `tick_vein` as the tick's outcrop step, so the world cluster does not name the context; it serves the drills' exhausted veins and the cells `register_outcrop_cells` queues on chunk load), `place_pending_crates` (loaded check, `schematic.odin`). `fluid_machine.odin`'s water and support checks and `cell_is_solid_or_entity` serve placement and the player, not the tick, and keep `^World`.
- `^World` parameters reached from `tick_entities`: 37 game procedures before (the kind ticks, the loose item and belt end helpers, the electric demand chain, the drill and vein draw chain, the core sample chain, `tick_lamps`, `sync_entity_lights`, `apply_spent_outcrops`, `place_pending_crates`), none after; the world is reached only through the two procedure values. Kept as `^World` wrappers for callers outside the tick: `spill_stack` (player, felling, placement; builds a context with no records), `registered_vein` and `deep_vein_at_column` (over the pure `vein_of_id` and `deep_vein_in_columns`), `flowing_water_level` (placement, over `block_flowing_water_level`).
- Lamp lights: `tick_lamps` sets `lit` only. `tick_entities_on_world` (what `simulation_tick` and the tests call) runs `tick_entities` and then `sync_entity_lights`, which is now after the outcrop and crate step instead of before it. `set_entity_light` reads and writes only light values and queues, and `world_set_block` only records the change, so the order does not change the result; the hashes below agree.
- Still on `^World`: `tick_player` (the next cut: its movement and collision read blocks many times per tick), `update_magnetometer`, `apply_item_use` through the state, `apply_launch_requests` and `tick_world` (the world's own tick: block changes, water, light, leaf decay).
- Determinism: `simulation_state_hash` after the factory benchmark's ticks (2 minutes warm up and 1 measured, as `test_factory_benchmark`), by a throwaway test run at HEAD from a `git archive` copy and in the working tree after the change: size 1 `6712730d3d5fc666` before and after, size 4 `d5bfd4fe996c48e4` before and after. The throwaway test is deleted.
- Tests call `tick_entities_on_world` in place of `tick_entities`, and a kind tick directly with `world_tick_context(&world, &records, content, TEST_TICK_RATE)` (nil records where the procedure reads none); no world builder changed.
- `code_map.md`: world -> simulation stays 221 (the vein helpers on the world side take the registry's fields, not the context). simulation -> world is an allowed edge and has no record; by `tools/code_graph.py --json` it fell from 626 to 612 references (the kind files lost 48, `simulation_world.odin`'s context, builder and block procedures added 34).
