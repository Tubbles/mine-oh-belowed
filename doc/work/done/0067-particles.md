# 0067 Particles and feedback

Status: implemented
Milestone: M11

## Goal

Actions have no visible consequence beyond the block change. Debris, smoke, steam, sparks and exhaust make machines and mining read at a glance.

## Deliverables

- A small particle system on the renderer: mining debris in the block's colour, break puffs, furnace and boiler smoke, steam from engines, sparks from the alloy furnace, flare flame, rocket exhaust and launch smoke, the capsule descending under a parachute.
- Emitters driven by entity state at render time; nothing in the simulation.
- Tests: emitter selection per state as pure procedures.

## Verify

- Builds and tests pass.
- User: Screenshots of a working furnace line and a launch.

## Notes

Implementation pointers (main agent, 2026-09-28), decisions taken so the item is unambiguous. Lands after 0065 water; read the Implemented paragraphs of 0063 (`src/render_weather.odin`, the billboard and depth pattern) and 0065 (the draw order) first.

- Pool, in a new `src/particles.odin` (pure, no raylib): `Particle_System` with a fixed pool of 2048 `Particle {position, velocity, age_seconds, life_seconds, size, color, kind}` and a next index that wraps, so the oldest is replaced when full; `advance_particles(system, seconds)` moves each live particle by its velocity, applies its kind's gravity and drag (debris falls and bounces off nothing, smoke rises and slows, steam rises fast and fades, sparks fall with a spark's gravity, exhaust rises little and spreads) and ages it out; `spawn_particles(system, emitter, seconds, random)` spawns `rate` per second (fractional accumulation per emitter is not kept: use a hash of the frame count and the emitter's cell as the random source, so no state per emitter) with the kind's speed spread and lifetime. Kinds: `Debris`, `Puff`, `Smoke`, `Steam`, `Spark`, `Flame`, `Exhaust`. This is render state in `Frame_State` (`src/loop.odin`), advanced with `frame_seconds` capped at a tenth of a second, never touched by the simulation, and discarded on a session change.
- Emitters, in a new `src/render_particles.odin`, pure selection tested per state, `emitters_for_frame(world, machines, models, players, mining_memory) -> []Emitter` in the temp allocator, with an `Emitter {position, kind, rate, color, spread}`: a furnace in `Burning` smokes from the centre of its top (`machine_model_top` for the height), a boiler in `Producing` smokes, a steam engine in `Producing` puffs steam from its top, a combustion generator in `Producing` gives thin exhaust, a flare stack that is relieving (`flare_stack_is_relieving`) shows a flame at its top, a crafting machine whose `recipe_maker` is `Alloy_Furnace` in `Working` throws sparks, a launch pad in `Launching` gives exhaust and smoke from the pad centre under the rocket (rate scaled up during the first quarter of the ascent), and a player mining a block emits debris in the block's colour (the top face colour from `Block_Texture_Definition`, which the atlas fallback already uses) at the target cell at a rate from the mining fraction. Fluid machine roles come from the machine data the fluid code already reads; do not hardcode ids, find how `advance_boiler`, `advance_flare_stack` and the engine are told apart and use the same field.
- Break puffs and the capsule descent need a memory of the last frame, kept in `Frame_State` as `Particle_Memory`: the last frame's mining target cell and fraction (a fraction above 0.5 followed by that cell no longer holding the block gives a puff burst of 24 particles in the block's colour at the cell), the last frame's shipment count (`len(world.shipments)` growing starts a descent: a capsule with a parachute, drawn as two billboards from the item and block atlases or two coloured quads, descending from 60 blocks above the landing pad's capsule cell to it over 8 seconds along a straight line with a slight sway, then a puff burst on landing; a world loaded mid delivery shows nothing, which is fine), and the launch: no memory needed, the pad state drives it.
- Drawing (`draw_particles` in `src/render_particles.odin`): camera facing quads through `rl.DrawBillboardRec` of a 16 by 16 soft disc texture generated once (`src/render_sky.odin` has a disc generator to share or copy), the colour and alpha from the particle (fading over the last third of its life), after the water pass and before the weather particles in `draw_session_world`, depth test on and depth writes off with the batch flushed around it like the weather. Debris draws as tiny cubes (`rl.DrawCubeV`) instead, since a spinning speck reads better than a disc.
- Settings: none new; the weather setting stays the weather's. 0074 will fold particles into reduced motion.
- Tests (`src/particles_test.odin`, `src/render_particles_test.odin`): the pool wraps and replaces the oldest, advance applies each kind's gravity and drag and retires particles at their life, spawning is deterministic for the same frame and emitter, the emitter selection per state for every machine listed and none for an idle one, the debris colour from the block texture, the puff detector fires once, the descent path starts above the capsule cell and ends on it in 8 seconds. No raylib in tests.

Per frame the particles walk the 2048 slots three times (advance, debris, discs) and the furnace, fluid machine, assembler and launch pad pools once; each live particle is one `DrawBillboardRec` quad (debris a `DrawCubeV`) in the batch, flushed at the start and end of the pass and once at the switch from cubes to discs. A furnace smokes 6 particles a second living 3.5 seconds on average, about 21 live, so a line of 20 working furnaces draws about 420 quads, plus about 35 per producing boiler and 17 per steam engine. A launch draws about 850 during the first quarter of the ascent (exhaust 360 a second for 1.2 seconds, smoke 120 a second for 3.5 seconds) and under 300 afterwards; the pool caps it at 2048.
