# 0062 Loose items on the ground

Status: implemented
Milestone: M11

## Goal

A mined block whose items do not fit refuses to break, a belt that ends at a ledge holds its items at the edge, and picking up a chest whose contents do not fit does nothing. Couch requests (2026-09-27): items must be able to lie on the ground. Belts that stop at a ledge drop their items down, items dropped onto the ground spill out, and destroying a chest yields its contents.

## Deliverables

- An item entity: a stack lying at a cell (a small spinning model or the item icon), falling with gravity to the ground or water surface, merging with a stack of the same item within the cell, a despawn timer from data (`game.sjson`, generous), saved with the world through the codec.
- Spilling: a mined block whose drop does not fit spills the drop at the block; picking up an entity whose contents do not fit takes what fits and spills the rest around it (chests, furnaces, machines and their held items alike), so a pickup never refuses; the inventory's Drop action puts the held stack on the ground in front of the player; items spilled onto a belt land on it.
- Belt ends: a line whose end is a dead end over a drop (no block in front at the belt's height and air below) lets items fall off the end into the cell below, onto the ground or a belt there; a dead end against a wall or on level ground still holds items as today.
- Pickup: walking over loose items picks them up when they fit, hotbar first for items already there; a full inventory leaves them lying.
- Drills keep handing their unit to the entity in their drop cell and never spill: a refused unit (gravel into a fuel slot) stalls the drill on purpose, an early game mechanic the user keeps (2026-09-27); sorting is the answer.
- Tests: spill on a full inventory, pickup on walk over, merge, despawn, belt end over a ledge drops and a level dead end holds, chest pickup with a full inventory spills the rest, save round trip.

## Verify

- Builds and tests pass.
- User: fill the inventory and mine, see the items; run a belt off a ledge and watch the ore fall; pick up a full chest with a full inventory and see the rest on the ground.

## Notes

Implementation pointers (main agent, 2026-09-27), decisions taken so the item is unambiguous:

- Store: loose items are not machines, so not an `Entity_Pool` (pools are checked against machine ids, `pool_is_consistent`) and never in `Entities.cells`, so they block no placement. A `Loose_Items` store in `Entities` (new `src/loose_item.odin`): `Loose_Item {item, count, cell: World_Coordinate, fall_ticks: u32, age_ticks: u32}` in a dynamic array, ticked in `tick_entities` (`src/entity.odin`) after the belts. Falling is integer: a stack falls one cell per `LOOSE_ITEM_FALL_TICKS` (6) while the cell below is air (`world_get_block`, `block_is_solid`, `src/world_block.odin`); a water block (`water_level > 0`) or an unloaded chunk holds it where it is. Stacks of one item in one cell merge up to the item's stack size; a cell may hold several stacks. Despawn after `loose_item_despawn_minutes` from `data/game.sjson` (`Game_Config` in `src/data_load.odin`, default 15, 0 never); the world settings carry it like the tick rate.
- Save: written and read with the entity pools in `src/save_state.odin` (`write_list`, `read_list`, the self describing schema). Existing saves must keep loading: a save without the table loads with no loose items. Read the codec's rules in `doc/architecture.md` and `src/save_binary.odin` before deciding whether that needs a format version step.
- Spilling: one procedure `spill_stack(world, cell, stack)` puts a stack at the cell, or at the first air cell above when the cell is solid. `mine_block` (`src/player_interaction.odin`) breaks the block even when the drops do not fit: what fits goes in, the rest spills at the block's cell, and the Inventory_Full event still fires once (`refuse_mining` goes). `pick_up_entity` (`src/entity_placement.odin`) takes what fits and spills the rest at the entity's origin after the removal, so it never returns false for a full inventory. A machine placed on cells holding loose items lifts them to the cell above its top.
- Belt ends: in `src/belt_movement.odin` the dead end hold (`lane_front_at_dead_end`) stays for a level dead end. When the cell in front of the line's last belt at the belt's height is air and the cell below that is air too, the front item leaves the lane as a loose item in the cell in front and falls. A loose item that lands in a cell holding a belt goes onto it (`belt_insert_item`, lane from `belt_near_lane` with the point it fell from) and waits on top while the belt has no room.
- Pickup: in the player tick (`tick_player`, `src/player.odin`) loose items within the player's cell or the cell below the feet and within one block of the position go into the inventory when they fit (`inventory_add`, which already fills the hotbar first for items it holds); a full inventory leaves them. Obtained statistics and discovery follow from `inventory_add` as for mined items; check `record_obtained`.
- Drop: a Drop button in the inventory panel (`src/ui_inventory.odin`, next to the existing buttons) drops the cursor's held stack, or the focused stack with nothing held, one block in front of the player at feet height, and a menu action `Menu_Drop` bound to a free control from `data/bindings.sjson` does the same (name the control in the docs). Dropping is a world action (`record_world_action`).
- Rendering: new `src/render_loose_items.odin`, drawn from `draw_session_world` (`src/loop.odin`) after the belts: a cube of 0.3 blocks coloured like belt items (`item_cube_color`, `src/render_belts.odin`), turning slowly and bobbing with the render time (see `motion_phase` in `src/model_motion.odin`), its height interpolated from `fall_ticks`.
- Drills: unchanged, they never spill (the Deliverables say why).
- Tests: `src/loose_item_test.odin` for merging, falling, water and unloaded chunks, despawn, spill above a solid cell, pickup and a full inventory; `src/belt_test.odin` for the ledge drop and the level hold; `src/player_interaction_test.odin` for the mining spill; `src/entity_placement_test.odin` for the pickup spill and placement lifting items; the save round trip in `src/save_test.odin` and the old save case; the UI audit with the Drop button.
