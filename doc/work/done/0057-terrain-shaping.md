# 0057 Terrain with shape: ranges, plateaus, valleys, beaches

Status: implemented
Milestone: M11

## Goal

The terrain is a gently noisy plain. Continents and ranges, plateaus and cliffs, valleys with gentle banks and beaches make a landscape worth crossing while the flat landing site rule still finds a home.

## Deliverables

- A layered height function: low frequency continents and ranges, domain warping for ridges and valleys, plateaus with cliffs, river valleys with gradual banks and sand beaches, still one pure function of seed and column.
- The spawn search keeps finding flat sites for every seed (extend the generation tests), and cave and vein placement follow the new heights.
- A `generator_version` in `world.sjson`: a loaded world whose version is older is marked in the load list ("terrain changed since this world was made") and loads anyway, since unmodified chunks regenerate with the new terrain.
- Tests: height ranges and slopes per biome, spawn found for the seven test seeds, determinism.

## Verify

- Builds and tests pass.
- User: Fly around a new world; screenshots from the pad.

## Notes

Implementation pointers (main agent, 2026-09-27), decisions taken so the item is unambiguous:

- Where: `terrain_height` in `src/generation_terrain.odin` stays the one pure function of `Purpose_Seeds` and a column, called per column by the chunk grid, by the caves (`src/generation_caves.odin`) and by the spawn search on a grid, so it stays cheap: a handful of `noise_2d_at` samples per column. New purposes join `Purpose_Seeds` (`src/generation_seed.odin`): range height, plateau mask, warp x, warp z. The streaming test logs "generation N ms per chunk" (`test_report_generation_and_meshing_time`); report the number before and after, and keep it within one and a half times the current one (about 16 ms on the agent's machine).
- Layers, all in blocks: (1) continental swell as today but shaped, so there are broad lowlands near sea level and raised masses (a smoothstep remap of the continental noise rather than a straight multiply). (2) Ranges: ridged noise (one minus the absolute value, squared for sharp crests) at a wavelength of about 256, masked by the raised masses, up to about 40 blocks. (3) Domain warping: the range and hill samples take a position offset by two low frequency noises (wavelength about 200, up to about 30 blocks), which bends ridges and valleys. (4) Plateaus: where a plateau mask exceeds a threshold, the height is pulled to a level per plateau (quantised to steps of about 12 blocks) with a cliff over a narrow smoothstep band, and the detail noise is damped on top. (5) Valleys and beaches: `carve_river` keeps its bed, and a second wider band lowers the banks gradually by up to about 6 blocks; beaches come from a new `beach` biome in `data/biomes.sjson` for columns 0 to 1 above sea level (sand top and filler, no trees), listed before the plains. A `mountains` biome for columns 48 and more above sea level (stone top, gravel filler if a gravel block exists, else stone; no trees, boulders) covers the new heights; the existing hills biome keeps its range below it. `TERRAIN_MAXIMUM_HEIGHT` rises to `SEA_LEVEL + 96`; check what depends on it (`GENERATION_CEILING`, `chunk_needs_caves`) still holds.
- Spawn: the flat site rules in `src/generation_spawn.odin` stay; lowlands must remain common enough that the ring search finds a site on every test seed. Tests use `TEST_SEEDS` plus seeds up to seven in total (extend the constant or a local list in the spawn test) and assert a site is found for each.
- Generator version: `GENERATOR_VERSION :: 2` in `src/generation_terrain.odin` (1 is the terrain before this item). `World_File` (`src/save_world.odin`) gains `generator_version`, written by `make_world_file`; a file without the key reads as 1. The load list (`src/save_list.odin`, `Save_Entry`) gains `terrain_changed` when the file's version is older, and the title's load screen (`src/ui_title.odin`) marks the row "(terrain changed since this world was made)" (string `save_terrain_changed`); the world loads as usual. The pinned landing pad (0049) keeps the pad where it was.
- Tests (`src/generation_test.odin`, `src/generation_spawn_test.odin`, `src/save_test.odin`): heights within the clamp; determinism (the existing tests cover it, extend where seeds are added); over a 512 by 512 sample per test seed the land fraction lies between 0.55 and 0.85, at least 80 percent of neighbouring columns differ by at most 1 block and at least 0.5 percent differ by 3 or more (cliffs exist), and some column reaches the river bed; the beach and mountains biomes are selected for the heights they cover; spawn found for the seven seeds; `generator_version` round trips through `world.sjson`, a file without it reads 1, and the load list flags an older version as terrain changed and loadable.
