# 0094 One tab strip over inventory, recipes and research; a shorter pause menu

Status: implemented
Milestone: M11

## Goal

Couch report (2026-09-28): R1 from the inventory opens Recipes, but R1 again switches the recipe categories instead of going on to Research, so the bumpers do not read as one tab strip. Recipes and Research can leave the pause menu since the inventory reaches them.

## Deliverables

- The inventory, the recipe browser and the technology screen become three tabs of one screen strip: L1 and R1 always step between them, from any of the three, wrapping; the strip stays visible on all three (`inventory_tabs` in `src/ui_inventory.odin`, `src/ui_recipes.odin`, `src/ui_technologies.odin`, the screen stack in `src/ui_core.odin`: opening the inventory pushes one screen whose tab is the state).
- Recipe categories: the category tabs in the recipe browser become a focusable row (up from the list reaches it, left and right change the category, Confirm not needed), and the bumpers no longer touch them. The technology screen's own tabs, if any, follow the same rule.
- The pause menu (`src/ui_screens.odin`) loses Recipes and Technologies; Journal, Power, Statistics, Settings, Developer and the rest stay. The strings for the removed buttons go.
- Initial focus (couch, 2026-09-28): opening the inventory screen or any machine panel puts the focus on the hotbar slot that is selected in the world, not the top left backpack slot; the focus fallback in `src/ui_core.odin` takes a preferred widget id the screen names on its first frame (`ui_prefer_focus`), and the inventory and machine screens name the selected hotbar slot's id. The pointer is unaffected.
- Item info always on (couch, 2026-09-28): the item info panel that the Info action opened on a focused or hovered stack shows by itself, in every slot view (the inventory, the machine panels, the recipe browser's lists), as a tooltip after `UI_TOOLTIP_DELAY` on the focused or hovered stack and gone when the focus moves; the Info action and its glyph hint leave the slot views (the binding stays for screens that use Info for something else; the subagent lists them). Never a way to disable it.
- Tests: the info tooltip appears on a focused stack after the delay and follows the focus; the initial focus on the selected hotbar slot for the inventory and a machine panel; bumper steps across the three tabs from each, the category row's focus and left and right, the audit for the three screens and the pause menu.
- Docs: `doc/ui.md`, `doc/input.md`, `doc/log/2026-09-28.md`, this item.

## Verify

- Builds and tests pass.
- User: R1 twice from the inventory lands on Research; in Recipes, up reaches the categories and left and right change them.

## Notes

Implementation pointers, decisions taken so the item is unambiguous (main agent, 2026-09-28):

- The strip: `.Inventory`, `.Recipes` and `.Technologies` stay three screens in the stack (`Screen` in `src/ui_core.odin`); `inventory_tabs` (`src/ui_inventory.odin`) is drawn by all three (`recipe_screen`, `technology_screen` too) with its selection written from the top screen every frame (as `recipe_category_tabs` writes `state.selections` from the browser's filter), so the old reset to 0 goes. A bumper or a click on another tab replaces the top screen (new `replace_top_screen` in `src/ui_core.odin`: pop, then push), so Back from any tab returns to what was under the strip (the world, or a lab's panel that opened Technologies). The recipe browser opened as a picker from a machine panel (`browser.selecting_for != NO_ENTITY`) draws no strip and keeps Back to the panel. Open_Inventory (keyboard E, which is also Tab_Next) closes any of the three strip screens the way `closes_inventory` in `handle_screen_keys` closes the inventory today, and the strip drops the tab inputs on that press as `inventory_tabs` already does. The glyph bars of the recipe and technology screens show `{.Tab_Next, text("hint_tabs")}` for the strip instead of `hint_categories`, which goes from `data/strings/en.sjson` if nothing else uses it.
- Recipe categories: `ui_tabs` (`src/ui_widgets.odin`) gains `mode: Ui_Tabs_Mode = .Bumpers` (`Ui_Tabs_Mode :: enum {Bumpers, Focus}`). In `.Focus` mode the strip is one focusable widget: `ui_interact` with `.Adjusts_Horizontally`, so left and right step the selection like a slider and up and down leave the row, the bumpers are ignored, a click still picks a tab, the focus outline draws around the row and the selected tab keeps its accent underline. `recipe_category_tabs` uses `.Focus`; dropping the tag filter on a category change stays; the list's initial focus (`src/ui_recipes.odin`, the focused recipe or the first row) stays. The technology screen has no tabs of its own (checked), nothing to do there.
- Pause menu (`pause_screen` in `src/ui_screens.odin`): the Recipes and Technologies buttons go, `button_count` becomes 8 (9 with developer), the strings `pause_recipes` and `pause_technologies` go, the comment over `handle_screen_keys` and the docs stop saying the two open from the pause menu. Journal, Power, Statistics, Save, Settings, Developer and the quits stay.
- Initial focus: `Ui_State` gains `preferred_focus: Ui_Id`, cleared in `ui_begin` next to `focused_tooltip`; `ui_prefer_focus(state, id)` sets it; the fallback in `ui_resolve` (`focus_index < 0`) takes the preference when it is among the frame's widgets, else the first widget as today. `ui_begin` clears `state.focus` while no screen is open (`state.screens.count == 0`): the world has no focusable widgets, so a focus remembered from the last visit is stale, and every screen opened from the world starts at its preference or its first widget; a screen pushed over another keeps the focus as today (Settings over Pause and back lands on the Settings button). `inventory_screen` and `machine_screen` (`src/ui_machine.odin`) prefer the player's selected hotbar slot: the id `ui_slot_grid` gives it (`ui_id(state, "slot", player.selected_hotbar_slot)` under the grid's pushed label, see `player_slot_region`); a helper on the grid that returns a slot's id is fine. The pointer is unaffected.
- Item info always on: `Ui_Widget_Flags` gains `.Tooltip_Shows_Itself`, which `ui_item_slot` sets on every slot. `Ui_State` gains `focus_rest_seconds`, advanced in `ui_begin` by the frame seconds while the focus id is the one of the previous frame and zeroed when it changed. `append_tooltip` draws the focused widget's tooltip when `tooltip_open` (the Info toggle, as today) or when the focused widget has the flag and `focus_rest_seconds >= UI_TOOLTIP_DELAY` (`UI_TOOLTIP_DELAY :: 0.4`, a constant in `src/ui_widgets.odin`). An empty slot has no tooltip text and shows nothing. The pointer resting on a slot is the same path, since a moved pointer focuses the widget under it. `inventory_glyph_bar` drops the Info hint; the Info action itself stays bound and keeps toggling the panel on the settings screen, the title's load list and the keyboard (shift); on a slot view the toggle is harmless. List the slot views that get the tooltip (every `ui_item_slot` caller) in the Implemented paragraph.

## Implemented

`inventory_tabs` (`src/ui_inventory.odin`) is drawn by `inventory_screen`, `recipe_screen` (not in the picker) and `technology_screen`; its selection follows `top_screen` through `inventory_tab_index` and a step calls `replace_top_screen` (`src/ui_core.odin`) with `inventory_tab_screens[tab]`. `handle_screen_keys` closes `.Recipes` and `.Technologies` on Open_Inventory too. `ui_tabs` (`src/ui_widgets.odin`) takes `mode: Ui_Tabs_Mode` (`.Bumpers`, `.Focus`), stepping through `step_tab_selection`; `recipe_category_tabs` passes `.Focus`. `pause_screen` has 8 rows (9 with Developer). `ui_prefer_focus` sets `Ui_State.preferred_focus`, which `fallback_focus` takes in `ui_resolve`; `inventory_screen` and `machine_screen` prefer `selected_hotbar_slot_id`; `run_screens` clears the focus while no screen is open (not `ui_begin`, see the log). `advance_focus_rest` counts `focus_rest_seconds` on `focus_rest_id` in `ui_begin`, and `tooltip_shows` decides for `append_tooltip`. The slot views that got the tooltip, every `ui_item_slot` caller: the inventory's backpack grid and hotbar and the same two in every machine panel (`player_slot_region` through `ui_slot_grid`), a machine's slots (`machine_slot`, `src/ui_machine.odin`), a chest's grid, an inserter's filter slot and "In hand" slot, and a splitter's filter slot. The recipe browser's lists are recipe rows, not item slots, and have no item tooltip; nothing changed there. The Info hint left `inventory_glyph_bar` (inventory and machine panels); Info stays on the settings screen, the title's load list and the on-screen keyboard. Tests: `test_preferred_focus_wins_the_fallback`, `test_item_tooltip_shows_itself_after_the_delay`, `test_focus_tabs_step_on_left_and_right` (`src/ui_core_test.odin`), `test_bumpers_step_the_inventory_tab_strip`, `test_open_inventory_closes_the_strip`, `test_a_frame_without_a_screen_clears_the_focus` (`src/ui_inventory_test.odin`), `test_inventory_strip_pause_menu_and_initial_focus` (`src/ui_audit_test.odin`), and the bounds audit over the three screens and the pause menu.
