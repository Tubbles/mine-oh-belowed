# 0294: The water's look: sheet, radial plane, waves

Status: landed (2026-11-06, e6a9316)

Milestone: the water tech demo (0291); the water stream after 0293, needed by both models.

## Goal

Water is drawn where it is and only where it is: a body as its radial plane at every level of detail (no staircase), thin or fast water as a sheet hugging the terrain with a minimum visible thickness while it moves and its true thickness at rest, no water showing through a one-sample wall, no film climbing a drained basin's walls; and a surface wave layer, a presentation-only float height field over the surface samples, excited by water arriving from a fall, a stream mouth into standing water, a dig, a pump intake, never by routine levelling, so a stream entering a lake sends ripples out and a settled lake is bit-identical frame to frame. The browser demos' rules (`/home/user/water-demos/notes/bodies-waves.md` sections 6 to 8, `teleport-waves.md` revisions 3 to 6, `surface-mesher.md`).

## Controls

None.

## Change

- `world_field_water.odin` `field_water_density` and the gather sites (`copy_field_grid_part` in `world_field_mesh.odin`, `coarse_field_water_density` in `world_field_lod.odin`): per corner the maximum of the level plane (a body's level, or the sample's slab level) and the sheet (terrain density plus the sample's thickness: the true fill share at rest, a 0.2-sample floor while moving, the full column while falling), stamped onto the sample and its 26 neighbours through face-connected open space only, with the wall guard (a stamp onto a solid sample is clipped so the crossing stays inside the wall on every edge to an air sample the wet sample does not reach). Moving means fill changed, flux ran or the sample falls within the last 6 ticks (a `water_moved` stamp per sample, derived, not saved).
- Remesh: a body's chunks remesh when its displayed level moves a thirty-second of a sample or ownership changes; a chunk with active wave samples remeshes while they are active; a settled chunk never.
- `render_field_waves.odin` (new, presentation): per surface sample a height offset and a velocity (f32, frame side, never in the simulation state or the hash), the height-field wave equation on the active set (c, damping, clamp 0.15 sample, CFL), excitations from the simulation's events of the tick (a per-tick event list the water step fills: impacts, stream mouths, digs, intakes), the offset added at gather time for the water grid; a `waves` setting in `data/game.sjson` presentation keys.
- Docs: `doc/presentation.md` (The water's look), `doc/architecture.md`, the log.

## Verify

- The checks and the suite.
- Tests: the trough stream of 0293's test has mesh within a sample of every moving wet sample (reading the chunk mesh); a settled lake's water mesh is byte-identical over 4 consecutive meshes; a one-sample wall between a full body and air yields no water vertex beyond the wall; a drained basin (pumped dry) has no water vertex more than 0.1 sample above the ground; the sea's surface residual at latitude 0, 45 and 90 at LOD steps 1, 2, 4 under 0.05 sample; waves change no fill byte and no hash.

## Specification (design, 2026-11-05)

Designed against 0292's and 0293's specifications and worktrees, neither landed: 0293's owner plane `Field_Chunk.water_owner` (`WATER_OWNER_NONE`, `WATER_OWNER_SEA` = 1, `WATER_OWNER_RELEASED`, `water_owner_body`), its bodies with `level` (fill units from the centre), `write_water_body_fills`, `write_field_water_quiet`, the front as the awake unowned samples, the two-pass flux (`plan_field_water_sample`, pass two's `set_field_water`), `settle_field_water_front`'s absorption, `claim_sea`, the flood's claim, `displace_water_body_volume` (0295's stub); 0292's `field_water_rests_on_full`, `dry_field_water_film` and the test helpers (`water_pressure_test_tuning`, `field_water_level_at`). `water_moved` is this item's. Against 0297's specification (not landed): the field shader's lighting and the water glint are 0297's; this item touches no shader (the glint constants stay 0297's shader constants, revisited only if the couch asks). Under the teleport model everything here applies with no bodies: only the sea (owner `WATER_OWNER_SEA` from the generation's mark, 0293) takes a plane.

Units: fill 0..254 a sample; a level in fill units from the centre (`i64`); the mesher's density in `DENSITY_STEPS_PER_SAMPLE` (128) steps a sample, clamped to `MAXIMUM_DENSITY` (127); the sheet's thickness in those steps. All simulation arithmetic integer; the wave layer is `f32`, frame side only.

### 1. The mesher's input (`world_field_water_look.odin`, new, world cluster after `world_field_water.odin`)

`field_water_density` (the slab value) stays as it is; the look composes it. The gather writes, per grid sample `s` (chunk `c`, index `i`, coordinate `p`, span `span`, terrain density `d`, step `step`):

- `field_water_look_base :: proc(world: ^Field_World, chunk: ^Field_Chunk, index: int, sample: Sample_Coordinate, step: i32) -> (value: i8, plane: bool)`: `chunk == nil` or `d > 0`: `-MAXIMUM_DENSITY, false`. Owner `o := chunk.water_owner[index]` not `WATER_OWNER_NONE`: the level `L` = `world.water_planet.sea_level` for the sea, else `world.water_bodies[water_owner_body(world, o)].level` (RELEASED maps to the pending body, as 0293); `value = i8(clamp(floor_divide_i64((L - span.middle) * DENSITY_STEPS_PER_SAMPLE, FIELD_WATER_FULL * i64(step)), -MAXIMUM_DENSITY, MAXIMUM_DENSITY))`, `plane = true` (the study's C: a signed distance, so every crossing edge has both ends inside the clamp band at every step; a body's dry air above its level is owned too, 0293, and takes the negative plane). Unowned (front water or plain air): `field_water_density(d, fill, sample), false` (the slab level, as today; at a coarse step `coarse_field_water_density` follows in `generate_field_grid` as today).
- Classification, frame-independent, derived from simulation facts: `Field_Water_Sheet_Mode :: enum u8 {Resting, Moving, Falling}`. `field_water_sheet_mode :: proc(world: ^Field_World, chunk: ^Field_Chunk, index: int, sample: Sample_Coordinate, span: Field_Water_Span, tuning: Field_Water_Tuning) -> Field_Water_Sheet_Mode`: `chunk.water_moved[index] == 0` or the sample owned: `Resting`. Else `down := field_radial_down_neighbour(world, chunk, sample)` (the face neighbour along the sample's radial axis towards the centre: the axis with the largest `|sample[axis]|`, the lowest index on a tie, offset `-sign`; the loaded chunk and index, or none): `Falling` when `down` is loaded, `density <= 0`, `water < FIELD_WATER_FULL` and `field_water_level(down.span, down.fill) + FIELD_WATER_FALL_GAP <= field_water_bottom(span)` (`FIELD_WATER_FALL_GAP :: 51`, 0.2 sample: the study's revision 6, a settled staircase is not a fall); else `Resting` when `down` holds any fill, or no face neighbour is ground (the top of a body or free water: the study's revision 6); else `Moving`.
- Thickness and clip in density steps (`FIELD_WATER_SHEET_FLOOR_STEPS :: 26`, 0.2 sample; `FIELD_WATER_SHEET_FALL_LIFT_STEPS :: 64`, half a sample), for a wet sample `w` with fill `f`, level `L_w` (its body's level when owned, else `field_water_level(span_w, f)`), stamped onto a neighbour `n` with middle `m_n` and terrain density `d_n`: share `t0 = i32(f) * 128 / 254`; `plane_w(n) = floor_divide_i64((L_w - m_n) * 128, 254)`; Resting: `t = t0`, `clip = plane_w(n)`; Moving: `t = max(t0, 26)`, `clip = plane_w(n) + 26`; Falling: `t = max(t0, 26)`, `clip = floor_divide_i64((field_water_bottom(span_w) + span_w.height - m_n) * 128, 254) + 26 + 64` (the column, the study's revision 4 clip). `stamp = clamp(min(i32(d_n) + t, clip), -127, 127)`; applied as `value(n) = max(value(n), stamp)`. The sample itself is one of its 27 targets, so a moving thin sample draws at least 0.2 sample thick, a resting film at its true share (a 3 percent film: the ground below it takes the level plane, the air above stays negative: a 0.03 sheet; the study's drained basin).
- Reach: a target `n = w + o` that is ground is always stamped (guarded, below). An air target with `|o|₁ = 1` always; `|o|₁ = 2` when either of the two intermediates (`w` plus one nonzero component of `o`) is not ground; `|o|₁ = 3` when one of the six two-step paths runs through non-ground samples. A missing chunk's sample counts as air. Resting owned `w` skips every owned non-ground target (its stamp is at most the plane the target already holds), so a lake's interior costs nothing: only its bottom, its shore and its unowned neighbours are stamped.
- The wall guard, for a ground target `n` (`d_n > 0`): for each of `n`'s six face neighbours `k` that is not ground (a missing one is air), not inside `w`'s 3×3×3, and whose base value `v_k <= 0`: `limit = (-i32(v_k)) * 3 * i32(d_n) / (2 * i32(d_n) - 5 * i32(d_k))` (the water crossing on the edge `n`→`k` at 60 percent of the way to the terrain crossing, `tW = 0.6 · d_n/(d_n − d_k)`, `v_n <= −v_k · tW/(1 − tW)`; with ±127 the limit is 54, the crossing at 0.30 of the edge against the wall face at 0.5); `stamp = min(stamp, limit)` over all such `k`; a `k` with water of its own (`v_k > 0`) limits nothing. When the guard lowers a Moving or Falling `w`'s stamp onto `n` to `g < stamp`, `w`'s own value is raised: with `D = i32(d_n) - i32(d_w)`, `num = -128 * i32(d_w) - t * D`, `den = 128 * D - num`; if `num > 0`: `value(w) = max(value(w), -g * num / den)` (the crossing on the edge `w`→`n` stays where the unguarded sheet put it, the study's revision 5 fix 2; a Resting `w` keeps its level value). The base values of `k` beyond the grid are computed on the fly.
- `stamp_field_water_sheet :: proc(grid: ^Field_Grid, world: ^Field_World, tuning: Field_Water_Tuning)`: a scratch pass after the 27 part copies in `gather_field_grid`, full resolution only. It walks local `-2 ..= FIELD_GRID_CELLS + 1` on every axis reading the wet samples (`density <= 0`, `water > 0`) straight from the loaded chunks (the chunk and its 26 neighbours; a wet sample two beyond the grid stamps the shell sample the neighbouring node meets this node at, so both compute the shared vertex from the same values), computes the mode once per wet sample, and stamps into `grid.water` within `-1 ..= FIELD_GRID_CELLS`. The guard reads `grid.water`'s base values (a copy taken before the first stamp, `base: [FIELD_GRID_SAMPLE_COUNT]i8` in the temp allocator) so the order of stamps never matters. Cost: a span per target (the integer square root `field_water_span` already does) and the reach's 1 to 6 density reads; a 500-sample stream column about 13 500 stamps, under 0.5 ms; a settled lake chunk only its boundary samples (above).
- The gather sites: `copy_field_grid_part :: proc(grid: ^Field_Grid, world: ^Field_World, chunk: ^Field_Chunk, offset: [3]i32, waves: Field_Wave_Heights)` writes `grid.water[index], grid.water_exact[index] = field_water_look_base(...)`, then adds the wave offset (section 3) to a non-ground sample; `gather_field_grid :: proc(world, coordinate, tuning: Field_Water_Tuning, waves: Field_Wave_Heights = nil, allocator := context.allocator)` calls `stamp_field_water_sheet` after the copies. `gather_coarse_field_grid` writes the base through `field_water_look_base(..., grid.step)` with `grid.water_exact[index] = plane`; `generate_field_grid` keeps a loaded sample's value as it is when `water_exact` is set and runs `coarse_field_water_density` otherwise. `Field_Grid.water_exact: [FIELD_GRID_SAMPLE_COUNT]bool` (comment: the water value is already a distance in the grid's spacing, a body's plane, 0294). Coarse grids take no sheet and no waves: the floor is 0.2 sample, under a coarse cell's resolution at 64 m and beyond; a fall there meshes from its slab values (a falling column's samples hold fill above a half, which the slab draws). The streaming passes `session.field_content.water` (the tuning, for `minimum_fill`) and `session.field_waves.heights` to `gather_field_grid` from `schedule_finest_field_mesh` (`update_field_streaming` gains `tuning` and `waves` parameters; the planet preview and the tests pass the tuning and nil).

`Field_Chunk.water_moved: [FIELD_CHUNK_SAMPLE_COUNT]u8` after `water_flow` (`world_field.odin`; comment: ticks of moving left, derived, never saved or hashed, zero on a fresh or decoded chunk). `set_field_water` sets `chunk.water_moved[index] = FIELD_WATER_MOVED_TICKS` (6) when the fill changes (not `write_field_water_quiet`: a body's bytes following its level are not movement). `expire_field_water_moved :: proc(world, chunk, index, sample)`: `if water_moved[index] > 0 { water_moved[index] -= 1; if it reached 0: mark_field_chunks_around_sample_dirty, world.edited_chunks[chunk.coordinate] = {} }` (the mesh returns to the resting sheet once); called at the top of `step_field_water_sample` (teleport) and of `plan_field_water_sample` (bodies) for every awake sample of the pass. A changed sample stays awake at least `still_ticks` (20) ticks, so the countdown always reaches zero awake; `sleep_field_water_sample` and the absorption into a body set it to 0 all the same. The codec, `field_chunk_equals`, `field_chunk_terrain_and_water_equal` and `field_chunk_state_hash` leave it out (named in their head comments).

### 2. The remesh rule

- A body's chunks: `Water_Body.meshed_level: i64` (derived, not saved, `min(i64)` on a fresh or loaded body; the sea's set to its level at `make_sea_water_body` and never marked again). In `write_water_body_fills`, before the band test and regardless of `force`: `if body.meshed_level == min(i64) || abs(body.level - body.meshed_level) >= FIELD_WATER_LOOK_LEVEL_STEP` (`:: 8`, a thirty-second of a sample): every coordinate in `body.chunks` gets `chunk.dirty = true` and `world.edited_chunks[coordinate] = {}`; `meshed_level = level`. The bytes still follow a band at a time (0293), the plane follows the level: the mark is the only thing the mesh needs.
- Ownership changes: `note_water_owner_change :: proc(world, chunk, sample)` = `mark_field_chunks_around_sample_dirty` + `edited_chunks`, called where 0293 sets an owner: the flood's claim (step 6), `finish_water_body_flood`'s release sweep (RELEASED to 0), `settle_field_water_front`'s absorption and `claim_sea`. The freeze and thaw change no owner.
- A front chunk: as today, `set_field_water` marks per change, the expiry once more.
- A settled chunk: no `set_field_water`, no owner change, `meshed_level` within the step, no active wave: never marked. `test_a_settled_lakes_water_mesh_repeats_byte_for_byte` holds it.

### 3. The wave layer (`render_field_waves.odin`, new, presentation)

Records. In the world cluster (`world_field_water_look.odin`, the mesher's input): `Field_Wave_Chunk :: struct { height, velocity: [FIELD_CHUNK_SAMPLE_COUNT]f32, active: Field_Sample_Bits, active_count: int }` and `Field_Wave_Heights :: map[Field_Chunk_Coordinate]^Field_Wave_Chunk` (nil: no waves). In the presentation: `Field_Wave_Tuning :: struct { on: bool, speed_squared, damping, clamp, velocity_clamp, kick: f32 }` (`velocity_clamp = 4 * clamp`), `make_field_wave_tuning(config: Water_Look_Config)`; `Field_Wave_Cell :: struct { coordinate: Field_Chunk_Coordinate, index: int }`; `Field_Waves :: struct { tuning: Field_Wave_Tuning, heights: Field_Wave_Heights, active: [dynamic]Field_Wave_Cell, emptied: [dynamic]Field_Chunk_Coordinate, last_tick: u64 }`, `destroy_field_waves`. `Session.field_waves: Field_Waves` (`session.odin`), made in `start_field_world` beside `start_field_streaming` from `config.water_look`, destroyed with the streaming. Never in the simulation state, the save or the hash.

Events. `Field_Water_Event_Kind :: enum u8 {Impact, Stream_Mouth, Dig, Pump}`, `Field_Water_Event :: struct { kind: Field_Water_Event_Kind, sample: Sample_Coordinate, amount: i32 }`, `Field_World.water_events: [dynamic]Field_Water_Event` (not saved, not hashed; freed in `destroy_field_world`), `MAXIMUM_FIELD_WATER_EVENTS :: 1024`, `push_field_water_event(world, kind, sample, amount)` appends while under the cap and drops beyond it. `step_field_water` clears the list first thing, both models. Appended: Impact, water arriving from a neighbour farther from the centre onto standing water: bodies, in `plan_field_water_sample` when the receiver is owned (a body's or the sea's) and `sender.span.middle > receiver.span.middle`; teleport, in `spread_field_water` when the neighbour is nearer and `field_water_neighbour_fill(neighbour) > tuning.minimum_fill || field_water_rests_on_full(neighbour.span, field_water_neighbours(world, neighbour.chunk, neighbour.sample))`; the sample is the receiver's, the amount the fill moved. Stream_Mouth: the same two sites when the sender is not farther (a lateral arrival into standing water). Dig: `follow_field_terrain_with_water`, a sample turned air with a face neighbour holding fill (amount `FIELD_WATER_FULL`) or a wet sample turned ground (the displaced fill), at the sample. Pump: `displace_water_body_volume(world, id, amount, intake: Sample_Coordinate)` gains the intake (0293's test call passes the lake's floor sample) and pushes one event with the fill taken. The weir, the teleport pull, the band's levelling and `write_water_body_fills` push nothing (the study's revision 6: the bobbing surface).

The step, `advance_field_waves :: proc(waves: ^Field_Waves, world: ^Field_World, tick: u64)`, called at the top of `stream_field_session` (`loop_field_session.odin`) before `update_field_streaming`, so the gather of this frame reads this frame's heights: off (`!on`): free every chunk entry, clear the lists, return. `steps := min(tick - last_tick, MAXIMUM_FIELD_WAVE_STEPS_PER_FRAME)` (`:: 4`), `last_tick = tick`; `steps == 0` (paused, or no tick since): return before anything else, so a frame without a tick neither excites twice nor moves (the cadence follows the world, never the frame or cheat speed). Then for each event of `world.water_events`, `excite_field_waves` (below); then `steps` integrations; then the marks: every chunk with `active_count > 0` gets `chunk.dirty = true` (the finest node only; coarse grids take no waves, so not `edited_chunks`); every coordinate in `emptied` once more, then its entry freed when `active_count == 0`; an entry whose chunk is no longer in `world.chunks` is freed.

One integration, order independent: pass one, for every active cell `i` in list order, `v_i = (v_i + speed_squared * Σ_j (h_j - h_i)) * damping` over the four tangential face neighbours `j` (the face neighbours other than the radial axis's two, `field_radial_axis(sample)`), reading `h` as it stood at the step's start (`h` is written in pass two only): a `j` that is not a surface sample contributes 0 (reflects); a surface `j` not active contributes `-h_i` (its `h` is 0) and is appended to the active set (its chunk's entry made under the cap; refused, it reflects); `|v_i| <= velocity_clamp`. Pass two: `h_i = (h_i + v_i) * damping`, `|h_i| <= clamp`; `|h_i| < FIELD_WAVE_REST && |v_i| < FIELD_WAVE_REST` (`:: 0.003`): `h_i, v_i = 0, 0` exactly, the cell leaves the list (swap remove, `active_count -= 1`, its chunk into `emptied`). Damping on `h` takes a non-zero-mean kick to zero (the study: no mean removal needed with it). A surface sample: loaded, `density <= 0`, `water > 0`, the radial-up face neighbour loaded with `water < FIELD_WATER_FULL` (`field_wave_surface_sample(world, sample) -> bool`). Constants: `c²dt²/dx²` = `speed_squared` 0.25 (CFL 0.5), damping 0.98, clamp 0.15 sample, the study's section 9.

Excitation, `excite_field_waves(waves, world, event)`: the surface sample along the radial through `event.sample` (itself, then up to 3 steps radial-up, then 3 radial-down; none: dropped); `k = min(velocity_clamp, kick * f32(amount) / FIELD_WATER_FULL)`; Impact: `v[s] -= k`, each tangential surface neighbour `v += k / 4` (zero mean); Stream_Mouth: the same with `k / 2`; Dig: `h[s] -= min(clamp, kick)`; Pump: `v[s] -= k / 8` (the steady dimple). Each touched cell activated. `MAXIMUM_FIELD_WAVE_CHUNKS :: 64` entries (256 KiB each, 16 MiB): an event or a spread into a chunk beyond the cap is dropped.

The gather reads it: `copy_field_grid_part` with `waves != nil` and the sample not ground: `h := field_wave_offset(waves, coordinate, index, world, sample)` = the sample's own `height` if its chunk has an entry, else the radial-up neighbour's, else the radial-down neighbour's (the study: the whole band shifts together), else 0; `value = clamp(i32(value) + i32(math.round(h * DENSITY_STEPS_PER_SAMPLE)), -127, 127)` (plane and slab alike, before the sheet pass). The gather runs on the main thread at submission (`schedule_finest_field_mesh`), so the job's `Field_Grid` carries the offsets baked in as `i8`; the worker reads no table, the simulation and the hash never see it. The sheet's clip ignores the offset (a shore under a ripple is clipped to the still level: under 0.15 sample at the shore only).

Rest: once the active set is empty every `h` and `v` is exactly 0, nothing is marked, and the gather adds 0: a settled chunk's mesh is the bytes' function again.

### 8. Decisions on the open points

- The sheet at a chunk border: the stamp pass reads wet samples two beyond the grid from the loaded neighbours, so neighbouring nodes compute their shared vertices from the same values; a missing neighbour reads as air and stamps nothing, and the node remeshes when it arrives (`field_world_insert_chunk` and the set's insert mark the chunk and its neighbours dirty today).
- Cost per remesh: the base pass is today's copy plus a span and an owner read per sample (about 39 000 integer square roots, which `field_water_density` already paid); the sheet is wet samples times 27 minus the skipped lake interiors; a solid target's guard reads six neighbours. A settled lake chunk stamps its shore and bottom only; a stream column under half a millisecond. The study's numbers (0.65 ms worst for the scatter, the full surface-nets pass itself 0.7 ms) carry over.
- The wave table is capped at 64 chunks; an excitation beyond it is dropped. The events list is cleared each tick, so a frame that ran several ticks sees the last tick's events only (a lost splash under catch-up, never a wrong one).
- Coarse levels of detail take the plane and the slab, no sheet and no waves (section 1): the floor and the clamp are under a coarse cell.
- Moving and falling are derived per sample from `water_moved` and the radial-down neighbour, with the study's 0.2-sample gap and its "stands on water is resting" rule, so a settled staircase on the sphere never draws as a column.
- Damping replaces the study's mean removal; the clamp and the velocity clamp bound every event's energy.
- A new file `world_field_water_look.odin` holds the look instead of growing `world_field_water.odin` (the item's Change names `field_water_density` and the gather sites; the sheet and the guard are 200 lines of their own).
