# 0311: Ground cover: tufts, flowers, ferns and stones

Status: landed (2026-11-07, 863dd02)

Milestone: the world generation series 0306 to 0313, after 0307 and 0310. Biomes (0308) are skipped for now (user, 2026-11-07), so the cover reads the climate (0307) and the slope directly.

## Goal

The ground near the eye is not bare: grass tufts, flowers, ferns, pebbles and boulders by climate and slope, placed by hash, never tiling (DESIGN.md, No perceivable repetition), fading out with distance, cleared by digging and by building.

## Specification (design, 2026-11-07)

Designed against the specifications of 0324 (`uncratered_relief` is the height map's top, so a carved face, a grotto's floor and the air under an arch read as cut; `generate_field_grid` gives shaded carved air sky 0), 0312 (lakes as generated water in the grids) and 0310 (the instanced tile draw: `instanceTransform` at locations 9 to 12 with a divisor of 1, a VAO and a VBO per tile, `mvp` as rmodels.c's instanced path sets it, the bottom row of the instance matrix carrying data; and `field_trees`' desktop and Android rows), all landing before 0311, and 0313's light on `main`. The worktree is made from 0310's tip. 0282's doodads (pebbles where stone meets the cover, ore specks on the veins' outcrops) are kinds of this pass; 0282 keeps the blend at material boundaries and the cut face's look (question 4). Biomes are skipped: the cover reads the climate, the slope and the surface. The figures come from a probe on `main` at 8 km with `DEFAULT_WORLD_SEED` (`tmp/0311/tree/src/probe_0311_cover_test.odin`, a prototype of the placement below on generated level 0 grids; logs `tmp/0311/climate.log`, `tmp/0311/counts.log`, `tmp/0311/speed.log`).

### Decisions

1. **The level 0 mesh job places the cover**, on the triangles it just made: the job already holds the surface, the climate and the natural share per vertex (the look), the materials, the light and the water, so every instance sits on the drawn mesh, arrives with it under the node's one revision, and an edit clears it through the remesh the edit already causes. Only level 0: every point within `field_view.level_distances_metres[0]` (64 m) lies in a level 0 node, and every kind's reach ends `COVER_LEVEL_MARGIN_METRES` (8) inside it, so no cover pops at a level change.
2. **Placement by hash per triangle**, keyed by the grid edge of its quad in global sample coordinates and its half, so the same ground gives the same instances on every remesh and nothing repeats on a lattice; the count is `floor(area × density + a hashed share)`; the density per kind is its rate times its tents over the temperature, the moisture and the slope, the shares of the surfaces it grows on (grass, sand, snow, rock, stone, the three ores, split as `field.fs` splits the topsoil), a boundary term (0282) and a patch noise of its own. The look's per vertex climate is reused, so the cover adds only the patch noises per vertex.
3. **Digging clears** through the remesh: a vertex further than `COVER_CUT_MILLIMETRES` (200) from the generated surface (the look's measure, now kept per vertex) thins the cover, none past twice it. Natural ground measured at most 141 mm off at 1000 mm on the hilly cold spot (p99 56 mm), 26 mm on the plains, 6 mm at 500 mm, so a node meshed from its chunks and the same node from a generated grid give the same cover. A generated grid is natural throughout; the sky light keeps it out of carved air (below half the full sky, none).
4. **Building clears** through the same remesh: `Frame_Table.revision` counts every occupancy and pose change; the streaming snapshots the frames when it moves, marks the level 0 nodes under a changed frame for a remesh, and each level 0 job carries copies of the frames over its node, whose occupied cells drop every instance whose base, middle or top lies in one. The terrain under a frame stays unchanged (0174).
5. **Distance**: each instance has a hashed rank; it is drawn out to `far - (far - near) × rank` and shrinks to nothing over the last fifth of that, so the density falls linearly from the kind's near to its far and nothing pops. Instances are sorted by rank per kind and node, so a node draws only the prefix its nearest point can show.
6. **Presentation only**: no kind collides, nothing reaches the simulation, the hash or the save; floats are fine. Boulders stay under 0.65 m and are walked through (question 3).
7. **Stand-in shapes generated in code** (blades, flowers, ferns, pebbles, crystals), a few triangles each, no textures and no discard (cheap on the phone's tiler); question 1 asks about lab items.

Procedures (one line comment each, pure):
- `make_field_cover_table :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, planet: Planet, row: Field_Cover_Row) -> Field_Cover_Table`: `on` when the planet has a climate, kinds, and `row.density_percent > 0`; the kinds converted; `seed = hash_combine(generation.ground_look_seed, COVER_TRIANGLE_SEED)`.
- `cover_tent :: proc(value: f32, range: [2]f32, fade: f32) -> f32`: 1 inside, linear to 0 over the fade outside.
- `cover_ground_shares :: proc(weights: [FIELD_TEXTURED_MATERIAL_COUNT]f32, snow, sand, steepness: f32) -> [Cover_Surface]f32`: the weights normalised; `rock := topsoil × smoothstep(rock start, rock full, steepness)`; snow `(topsoil - rock) × snow`; sand `(topsoil - rock - snow) × sand × (1 - smoothstep(sand start, sand end, steepness))`; grass the rest of the topsoil; stone the stone, deep stone and bedrock; the ores their own (the natural topsoil is all grass, sand, snow or rock, as `field.fs` shows it with a climate).
- `cover_kind_density :: proc(kind: Field_Cover_Kind, ground: Cover_Ground, patch: f32) -> f32`: per m²: `rate × tent(T) × tent(M) × tent(slope) × on × (1 - boundary + boundary × 4 × own × (1 - own)) × patch × cut`, `on` the shares weighed by the kind's surfaces and `own` the shares of the surfaces it grows on at all.
- `cover_patch_share :: proc(kind: Field_Cover_Kind, noise: f32) -> f32`: 1 without a patch, else `clamp((noise - threshold) / edge, 0, 1)` (noise -1 to 1).
- `cover_cut_share :: proc(offset_millimetres: f32) -> f32`: 1 within `COVER_CUT_MILLIMETRES`, 0 past twice it, linear between.
- `cover_unit :: proc(hash: u64, slot: uint) -> f32`: the 16 bits at `16 × slot` over 65536.
- `cover_instance :: proc(kind: Field_Cover_Kind, point, up, normal: [3]f64, slope_tangent: f32, light: [2]u8, color: [3]u8, hashes: [4]u64) -> matrix[4, 4]f32`: four hashes an instance, slots by `cover_unit`: `hashes[0]` the rank (0), the barycentric units (1, 2, used by the caller) and the yaw (3); `hashes[1]` the width and the height, each the mean of two units (triangular); `hashes[2]` the depth (stones: the mean of slots 0 and 1, independent of the width; foliage: the width times 0.85 to 1.15 by slot 0) and the lean (foliage only: up to `COVER_TILT_DEGREES` by slot 2 towards the bearing of slot 3); `hashes[3]` the shade (slot 0, 216 to 255) and the colour pick (slot 1 times `color_count`); stones' up halfway to the ground's normal; columns right × width, up × height, forward × depth and the base `point - up × (sink × height + 0.5 × max(width, depth) × min(slope_tangent, 1))` in world metres; the bottom row `rank, field_pack_bytes({sky, block, shade}), field_pack_bytes(color), 1`. `cover_instance_base(instance)` and `cover_instance_top(instance)` read it back.
- `place_field_cover :: proc(surface: Field_Surface, grid: ^Field_Grid, look: Field_Ground_Look, table: Field_Cover_Table, spacing_millimetres: int, frames: []Cover_Frame, allocator := context.allocator) -> Field_Cover_Data`: nothing unless `table.on`, `look.on` and `grid.step == 1`. Per vertex used by a triangle before `skirt_index_start`: the world position in metres (f64), the patch share per kind whose tents are not 0 there (`value_noise(hash_combine(ground_look_seed, COVER_PATCH_SEED + kind), on_sphere, wavelength)`), wet (any of its cell's eight samples, the cell `clamp(floor_divide(position, FIELD_MESH_POSITION_UNITS), -1, FIELD_GRID_CELLS - 1)` per axis, with `grid.water > 0`), dark (`light[.Sky] < COVER_LEAST_SKY_LIGHT`), the cut share from `look.offset_millimetres`. Per triangle `t`: none when a corner is wet, dark or has a cut share of 0; the ground from the corners' means (celsius, moisture, snow, sand and weights from the look and the vertex; the slope of the mean of the corners' normals against the radial at the centroid); the area; the key `hash_combine` of `table.seed`, the quad's sample plus `grid.origin` and its axis (`surface.quad_edges[t / 2]`) and `t % 2`; per kind: `seed := hash_combine(key, kind)`, `count := int(area × density + cover_unit(seed, 0))`, per instance `i`: `hashes[n] = hash_combine(seed, 4i + 1 + n)`, the rank `cover_unit(hashes[0], 0)` first (the instance skipped at or above `table.density`), the point at the barycentric units 1 and 2 (folded when their sum passes 1), the light the corners' mean, the colour by the kind's source (the corners' mean grass colour, their mean strata tint `vertex.color`, or the picked colour), `cover_instance`, kept unless `cover_instance_cleared`. Then per kind: sorted by rank (the bottom row's first element), cut to `table.limit`, `rank_counts`.
- `cover_instance_cleared :: proc(frames: []Cover_Frame, instance: matrix[4, 4]f32) -> bool`: the base, the middle and the top in world position units; for each frame whose box holds the point, `world_to_frame_cell` and a binary search of its cells.
- `snapshot_cover_frames :: proc(table: ^Frame_Table, allocator := context.allocator) -> [dynamic]Cover_Frame`: every frame of `table.frames` with occupied cells (`table.occupants`, the block frame left out), in id order, its cells sorted, its box from `frame_cell_centre` of its cells plus half a pitch diagonal.
- `cover_frames_meeting :: proc(frames: []Cover_Frame, minimum, maximum: [3]f64, allocator := context.allocator) -> []Cover_Frame`: deep copies of those whose box meets the given one.

### The draw: `src/render_field_cover.odin` (new, presentation)

- `Cover_Shape_Vertex :: struct { position, normal: [3]f32, color: [4]u8 }` (28 bytes). `cover_shape_vertices :: proc(shape: Cover_Shape, parts: int, seed: u64, allocator := context.allocator) -> [dynamic]Cover_Shape_Vertex`, non indexed triangles in a unit box (x and z -0.5 to 0.5, y 0 to 1, the base at the origin), the colour's alpha 255 where the vertex takes the instance's colour and 0 where it keeps its own, `cover_part_vertices :: proc(shape: Cover_Shape) -> int` the vertices per part (0 for stones):
  - blades: per part a blade from a hashed base radius up to 0.15 leaning out to a tip radius up to 0.5 at a hashed height of 0.65 to 1, base half width 0.05, a bend at mid height of half width 0.035, three triangles (9 vertices), grey 0.5 at the base, 0.75 at the bend, 1 at the tip.
  - flower: per part a stem (one triangle, half width 0.02, to 0.75 to 1 high, its own green [80, 112, 56], alpha 0) and a head of five triangles round its top (radius 0.14, tilted 20 degrees out; 1, the centre 0.8), 18 vertices.
  - fern: per part a frond of four triangles along a midline rising to 0.85 at 0.35 out and drooping to 0.5 to 0.6 at 0.5, half widths 0, 0.09, 0.07, 0 (12 vertices), grey 0.55 to 1 to 0.85.

  - crystal: an octahedron with its tip at 1 and its girdle at 0.4, ±15 % by hash, flat normals (24 vertices), 0.75 to 1.

- `init_field_cover_renderer :: proc(renderer: ^Field_Renderer, data_directory: string, planet: Planet, row: Field_Cover_Row, first_level_metres: f32)`: sets `renderer.cover`, called by `start_field_presentation` and the planet preview after the field renderer loaded (`field_cover_row(config.field_cover)`, `level_distances_metres[0]`); the tile means are `renderer.material_colors` (stone) and a new `Field_Renderer.grass_color` (the grass tile's mean, which `init_field_renderer` already computes for the globe); the shader `shaders/cover.vs` and `cover.fs` (a failed load logs `error: the cover shaders did not load, the field draws without its cover` and leaves `on` false); each kind's shape up as a VBO; `color_scale` the first colour / 128 times the grass tile's mean times 2 (grass), the stone tile's (ground), 1 (colors); near and far from `cover_reach`. `destroy_field_renderer` frees it.
- `cover_reach :: proc(kind: Planet_Cover_Kind, row: Field_Cover_Row, first_level_metres: f32) -> (near, far: f32)`: both times the row's distance share, far at most `first_level_metres - COVER_LEVEL_MARGIN_METRES` (`COVER_LEVEL_MARGIN_METRES :: 8`) and at least 0, near at most far.
- `cover_draw_count :: proc(draw: Field_Cover_Node_Draw, near, far, distance: f32) -> i32`: 0 at or past far; `share := (far - distance) / (far - near)` (1 for a near at far); all at a share of 1 or more, else `rank_counts[ceil(share × COVER_RANK_STEPS) - 1]`.
- `upload_field_cover :: proc(cover: Field_Cover_Renderer, data: Field_Cover_Data) -> [MAXIMUM_COVER_KINDS]Field_Cover_Node_Draw` and `unload_field_cover_node`: from `apply_field_mesh` (which gains the data and frees nothing of it; `upload_streamed_field_meshes` destroys it after) and `unload_field_node_render`.
- The instancing is 0310's: 0310's tile VAO set up moves into `src/render_instancing.odin` as `upload_instanced_buffer :: proc(shape_buffer: u32, layout: []Instanced_Attribute, stride: i32, instances: []matrix[4, 4]f32) -> (vertex_array, instance_buffer: u32)` (the shape's attributes, then the instances at 9 to 12 with a divisor of 1), `unload_instanced_buffer` and `instanced_mvp :: proc() -> matrix[4, 4]f32`, and the trees and the cover call them. The cover's layout: position at 0, normal at 2 and colour at 3 (unsigned bytes, normalised).
- `draw_field_cover :: proc(renderer: ^Field_Renderer, camera: rl.Camera3D, visible: []Field_Node_Render)`: in `draw_field` after the terrain loop and before `draw_field_water` (the visible list is the terrain's, so the cover is culled with its node and lies under the water pass). `rlgl.DrawRenderBatchActive`, the shader with `mvp` and `camera_position`; kind major: the kind's uniforms (`cover_near`, `cover_far`, `part_vertices`, `foliage`, `color_scale`), back face culling off for foliage and on for stones; per visible node with instances of the kind, `cover_draw_count` at the distance from the camera to the node's box (its origin and its 32 samples), its vertex array and `rlgl.DrawVertexArrayInstanced(0, vertex_count, count)`; `drawn_count` summed. `set_field_frame_lights` and `set_field_point_lights` also set the cover shader's open sky, spot and point light uniforms when it is on. The planet preview's line adds `cover %d`.

### Shaders: `data/shaders/cover.vs`, `cover.fs`

- `cover.vs`: `in vec3 vertexPosition; in vec3 vertexNormal; in vec4 vertexColor; in mat4 instanceTransform;`. The bottom row read and rebuilt to `0, 0, 0, 1` (as 0310's tree.vs): `rank`, the packed sky, block and shade bytes, the packed colour. `end = cover_far - (cover_far - cover_near) * rank`, `size = clamp((end - distance(camera_position, base)) / (cover_shrink_share * end), 0, 1)` (`const float cover_shrink_share = 0.2;`); a vertex of an instance at size 0 goes outside clip space. Foliage only (times `foliage`, no branch): each part leans by an integer hash of `floatBitsToUint(rank)` and the part `uint(float(gl_VertexID) / part_vertices)`, up to `const float part_lean = 0.25;` of the unit width at the top, scaled by the vertex's height, so no two tufts share a silhouette. The world position is the instance matrix times the local position times `size`, so it shrinks into its base. The normal is the instance's up for foliage (lit as the ground it stands on, both faces alike) and `normalize(transpose(inverse(mat3(m))) * vertexNormal)` for stones. Out: the world position, the normal, the albedo (`mix(vertexColor.rgb, vertexColor.rgb * colour * color_scale, vertexColor.a)` times the shade), the sky and the block light (0 to 1).
- `cover.fs`: the terrain's light: `outdoor = sky * outdoor_light(normal, normalize(fragment_world_position))`, `max(vec3(block), outdoor)`, plus `point_light_sum` and `spot_light`, then `apply_haze(min(lit, vec3(1.0)), fragment_world_position, camera_position, sky)`, opaque, no discard. `outdoor_light`, `apply_haze`, `point_light_sum` (the terrain's, with its clip line), `spot_light`, their uniforms and the constants they read copied verbatim from `field.fs`; arrays `[8u]`, every integer literal `u`, no reserved word.

### Hand-back check

- Memory a frame draws from: the cover's buffers are made and freed in `apply_field_mesh` and `unload_field_node` during `upload_streamed_field_meshes` in `prepare_field_frame`, before any viewport, as the meshes' are.
- A start-up load that can fail: the cover's shaders failing turn the cover off with one line; the field draws.
- A number parsed from text is range checked: every key of `cover` and `field_cover`.
- A new participant in a shared budget: the cover runs inside level 0 mesh jobs (the job's added time measured by `test_report_the_cover_cost`), its remeshes go through the 8 submissions and 8 uploads a frame, and a frame change marks only the nodes under the changed frames.
- A list that grows without bound: kinds at 12, colours at 8, a kind's instances in a node at the row's limit.
- Tests never touch the state directory: all in memory.
- A changed save layout: none (`Frame_Table.revision` is not saved).
