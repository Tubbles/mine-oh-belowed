# 0115 Touch overlay: a virtual gamepad drawn by the game

Status: implemented
Milestone: M11

## Goal

The Android app (0114) has no way to move without a physical gamepad. The user wants the game to draw its own overlay (2026-09-29): "a modern overlay with things like left stick and right stick recentering on the drag start location anywhere on the left side/right side of screen, given its not a button there", the GameNative button layout kept ("Keep the diamond positions, it's comfortable as it is"), and the Bedrock lessons: a floating stick, drag anywhere to look, sprint by pushing the stick past its rim at the top, direct touch in menus. The overlay is a virtual gamepad: it fills the same `Raw_Gamepad` the raylib backend reads and the look delta the mouse fills, so `data/bindings.sjson` maps its buttons to actions like any gamepad's, the Input diagnostics page shows it, and nothing downstream learns a new device. Later items: tap to interact with a break progress ring, hotbar taps, an in-game layout editor, haptics.

The GameNative layout (screenshot of 2026-09-29, a 2424 by 1080 screen), in pixels of a 1080 high screen, which the data file below keeps as the reference: LT a rectangle in the top left corner 363 by 170, RT its mirror in the top right; LB 170 by 100 below LT at y 188, RB its mirror; the face diamond on the right with centres Y (2112, 287), X (1967, 431), B (2252, 431), A (2112, 575) and radius 73, measured from the left edge, so from the right edge Y is 312 in; L3 a circle of radius 60 at (121, 790), R3 its mirror; Back and Start as pills 163 by 79 centred at (1113, 983) and (1304, 983), which is 99 left and 92 right of the screen's centre line. The d-pad on the left (centre (260, 430), arms 145 out) is left out: the left half is the stick's zone, and the d-pad's world actions are on LB, RB and the hotbar.

Facts checked in the code (2026-09-30, after 0114):

- The raylib backend builds its frame in `read_raylib_input_frame(previous_pressed, bindings)` (`input_raylib.odin`) from `read_raylib_raw_input()`: `gamepad_button_actions(raw.gamepad, bindings)` (`bindings.odin`) walks `Raw_Gamepad.button_down` by the backend's button index and adds `bindings.gamepad_buttons[index]`, the triggers are `axis_values[LEFT_TRIGGER]` and `[RIGHT_TRIGGER]` over `TRIGGER_PRESS_THRESHOLD` (`bindings.odin` near line 455), the sticks come from `gamepad_stick(raw.gamepad, .LEFT_X, .LEFT_Y)`, and `look_delta` is `raw.mouse.delta`. `raylib_gamepad_button(button: sdl.GamepadButton)` (`bindings.odin`) turns the control names of `bindings.sjson` into raylib button indices, so the overlay file can spell controls the same way. `Raw_Gamepad` has `connected`, `index`, `name: cstring`, `axis_count`, `axis_values`, `button_count`, `button_down` (`input_actions.odin`).
- The pointer on Android is `raylib_pointer_position` (`input_raylib.odin`, 0114): the first touch while down, the last position after. raylib's `MAX_TOUCH_POINTS` is 8 (`rcore.c`); the touch procedures are `GetTouchPointCount`, `GetTouchPointId(index)` and `GetTouchPosition(index)`.
- The frame reads input through `read_input_frame` (`loop.odin`), draws the HUD with `draw_hud(&state.ui, screen_context)` and then `run_screens`; a screen is open when `state.ui.screens.count > 0`. Settings rows are made like `hold_mode_choice(state, settings_row(content), "settings_sneak_hold", &settings.sneak_hold)` in `ui_screens.odin` (the Accessibility tab), settings live in `settings.odin` with their defaults and the configuration tests in `configuration_test.odin`. Command line flags are the struct fields with `args:"name=..."` tags in `main.odin`.
- Content files load in `load_game_data` (`data_reload.odin`, `content.developer_kits = load_developer_kits(...) or_return` is the pattern, `developer.odin`) into `Game_Content` (`loop.odin`); a top level data file gets its category in `top_level_data_file_category` (`data_watch.odin`), `.Content` reloads it with the rest. The gamepad control names are SDL's (`SOUTH`, `EAST`, `LEFT_TRIGGER`, `LEFT_STICK`, `BACK`, `START`), see `data/bindings.sjson`.

## Change

- `data/touch_overlay.sjson`: `reference_height = 1080` and `elements`, each with `kind` (`button`, `stick`, `look`), and for buttons `control` (a raylib gamepad button name as `bindings.sjson` spells them: `LEFT_TRIGGER`, `RIGHT_TRIGGER`, `LEFT_SHOULDER`, `RIGHT_SHOULDER`, `NORTH`, `WEST`, `EAST`, `SOUTH`, `LEFT_STICK`, `BACK`, `START`), `shape` (`rectangle` or `circle`), `anchor` (`top_left`, `top_right`, `bottom_left`, `bottom_right`, `bottom_center`), `position` (the element's centre from the anchor, x towards the screen's middle, y towards its middle), `size` (width and height, or the diameter twice) and `label`; the stick element has `side = "left"`, `radius = 130`, `dead_zone = 0.15` and `sprint_rim = 1.25` (the drag distance over the radius that sprints when the direction is within 45 degrees of straight up); the look element has `side = "right"` and `sensitivity = 1.0` (multiplied into the drag's pixels as mouse pixels). The shipped file holds the GameNative layout above without the d-pad and R3. Loaded with the other content (`load_game_data`, reloaded by the data watch), validated: unknown kinds, controls, anchors and shapes are errors that name the element.
- `src/touch_overlay.odin`: the pure layout, `overlay_layout(elements, screen_size) -> []Placed_Element`, scaling every length by `screen_height / reference_height` and anchoring, so a wider screen keeps the corners where the thumbs are; hit testing per shape; the touch tracker `Touch_Overlay_State` with one slot per raylib touch point id (`rl.GetTouchPointCount`, `rl.GetTouchPointId`, `rl.GetTouchPosition`, up to raylib's `MAX_TOUCH_POINTS`): a touch that begins on a button latches that button until it ends (the button stays pressed while the finger slides off it, a game gamepad's feel), a touch that begins on the free left half becomes the stick with its origin at the start point (the knob follows the finger, the vector over `radius` is the stick value with the dead zone, clamped to the unit disc, `sprint_rim` presses `LEFT_STICK`, the Sprint control, while it holds), a touch that begins on the free right half becomes the look drag (its movement since the last frame, in render pixels times `sensitivity`, adds into `look_delta` like the mouse, so the look settings apply), and several fingers do all of it at once. Its output is `Raw_Gamepad` (`connected`, `name = "Touch overlay"`, `button_down` by raylib button index, the left stick axes) and a look delta.
- The raylib backend (`input_raylib.odin`): while the overlay is on, `read_raylib_input_frame` merges the overlay's gamepad into the frame after the physical one (a physical gamepad still works; buttons and axes are or'ed and summed then clamped) and adds its look delta; a raw mouse from touch stays as 0114 made it, so menus take direct touch. `Raw_Input.backend` stays `.Raylib`; the diagnostics Input page shows the overlay's gamepad as it shows any.
- When it is on: `settings.touch_overlay` (`auto`, `on`, `off`; `auto` is on for the Android build and off elsewhere) in the Accessibility tab, and the command line flag `--touch-overlay` forcing it on for the run (doc/build.md's flag list), so the couch and the Deck test it: there the mouse is touch point 0 while its left button is down, so a drag with the button held moves the stick or looks, and a click presses a button. The overlay is drawn only while no screen is open (the world context); with a screen open touch is the pointer (0114) and the overlay's buttons are not read, except Start and Back, which stay so the pause menu can be closed the way it was opened.
- Drawing (`draw_touch_overlay`, after the HUD in the frame): outlines and labels in the UI font at a low alpha (0.35), a pressed button filled, the stick's base ring at the drag origin with the knob at the finger, nothing for the look zone. The overlay never flickers with the frame rate and nothing pulses on a period (DESIGN.md's no repetition rule; a pressed state changes only with the finger).
- Tests: the layout scaling and anchoring at two screen sizes (1080 and 720 high, 16:9 and 20:9); hit tests per shape; touch sequences into gamepad state: a press on A sets SOUTH, a slide off keeps it until release, a left drag gives the expected axes and the rim sprint, a right drag gives the look delta, a finger on each at once; the data file loads and validates, and a bad control name is reported with the element's label.
- Documentation: `doc/input.md` (the overlay section: what it does, the layout, the desktop flag, the setting), `doc/ui.md` (the Accessibility row), `doc/build.md` (the flag), `doc/content.md` or wherever data files are listed (`touch_overlay.sjson`), `CLAUDE.md` if a rule falls out, `DESIGN.md` (touch as a first class input beside the gamepad and the trackpad pointer), a dated entry in `doc/log/2026-09-29.md` (tags `#input #android #decision`).

Deviations from the plan:

- The overlay merges into the SDL3 backend too (`read_sdl3_input_frame`), not only the raylib one: the couch and Deck check in Verify runs `--touch-overlay` on their default backend, which is SDL3. The overlay builds its gamepad in the backend's numbering (`touch_overlay_raw_gamepad`): raylib button indices with the triggers as `LEFT_TRIGGER_2` and `RIGHT_TRIGGER_2`, or SDL button indices with the triggers as axes. Both backends number the first four axes as the sticks and the next two as the triggers, so the merge sums and clamps the stick axes and takes the larger trigger value (a summed resting raylib trigger would read -2).
- `data/bindings.sjson`: Sneak on `EAST` and Sprint on `LEFT_STICK` lost `backend = "sdl3"`. On the Android build (raylib backend) the overlay's B would not sneak and the rim sprint would do nothing otherwise. A physical gamepad on the raylib backend now sneaks on B and sprints on the stick click like on SDL3. `test_binding_rows`, `test_configuration_dump` and the reference raylib table in `bindings_test.odin` follow.
- While the overlay drives the world (on, in a world, no screen open) the pointer's delta is replaced by the overlay's look delta and the mouse buttons read nothing, since the first touch holds raylib's left mouse button (Mine) and moves the mouse delta (look); the raw mouse stays as 0114 made it. On the desktop the cursor stays enabled in the world while the overlay is on, since a disabled cursor has no screen position to hit test.
- The overlay is active only in a world (a session exists): on the title screens touch is the pointer alone, and Start and Back are not read there.
- The file is held to the configuration's strict keys (`parse_configuration_layer` and `assign_configuration_value`, as `bindings.sjson`), so a misspelt key is an error too. Controls are the SDL gamepad button names that have a raylib equivalent plus `LEFT_TRIGGER` and `RIGHT_TRIGGER`; a paddle is refused. At most one stick and one look, on different sides. A circle's hit test is the ellipse inside its size.
- Details the plan left open: LB and RB sit at the screen edge (`x` centre 85); `bottom_center` positions are signed offsets from the centre line (Back -99, Start 92); a second finger on the stick's half while the stick is held is ignored; a finger keeps the role it landed with, so one that landed in a menu stays the pointer's after the menu closes; the knob is 0.4 of the stick's radius and is drawn under the finger, unclamped; a finger whose element a data reload removed or replaced with another kind reads nothing (`touch_slot_reads`).
- Circles needed two draw commands the UI did not have (`Circle`, `Ring`, drawn with `rl.DrawCircleV` and `rl.DrawRing`). The new Accessibility row made the UI audit report the tab's last row squeezed at UI scale 1.5 until `ACCESSIBILITY_SETTINGS_ROW_COUNT` went from 5 to 6.
- The log entry is `doc/log/2026-09-30.md` (the day it was implemented; the logs are write once) rather than `2026-09-29.md`.

After review (2026-09-30), changed in the same working tree:

- The rim sprint latches: the first crossing of `sprint_rim` within 45 degrees of straight up holds `LEFT_STICK` until the finger lifts (`Touch_Slot.sprint_latched`, `stick_sprint_latched`), so easing back inside the rim, or a wobble across it or its 45 degree edge, makes no new press. In toggle mode the one edge switches sprinting on and it ends when movement stops; in hold mode it sprints until the lift. `test_rim_sprint_lasts_the_drag_in_both_sprint_settings` runs both settings through the shipped raylib bindings, `apply_hold_settings` and `update_sprinting`.
- With a screen open the overlay draws and reads Start and Back alone (`control_serves_screens`, `placed_for_screen`; `draw_touch_overlay` now runs after `run_screens` so the pills show over the screen). The first version read them without drawing them, so a tap on the bottom centre of the inventory closed it; reading nothing over screens, tried next, left the inventory, machine panels, map, recipes, journal, power, statistics and technologies without a way to close by touch, since their close is a glyph hint handled in `handle_screen_keys` and raylib consumes Android's back key. A stick or button held from the world reads nothing while a screen is open and again once it closes, and the sprint latch is not updated under a screen, so a rim crossing there is no fresh press when it closes. `test_with_a_screen_open_only_start_and_back_read` became `test_with_a_screen_open_only_start_and_back_read_and_are_drawn`, and `test_touch_overlay_draws_start_and_back_alone_over_a_screen` checks the draw list.
- A finger the overlay claimed never reaches the UI as a click: a Back tap over the technology screen also arrived as a mouse press on the Recipes tab under the pill (raylib's first touch holds the left mouse button), and a tall machine panel clamped to the top can put a slot there. When the first touch (touch index 0, or the mouse on the desktop) is a slot the overlay classified as a button, in the world or over a screen, both backends read the raw left mouse button up for that frame (`Touch_Overlay_Frame.pointer_claimed` from `touch_overlay_frame`, applied by `touch_overlay_mouse` where the raw mouse enters the frame, and skipped in `keyboard_mouse_actions`); the position still follows it. `test_a_touch_the_overlay_claims_is_no_pointer_click` covers a Start tap over a screen, a pointer tap elsewhere, a button and the stick in the world, and a second finger on Start.
- Known overlap: at the top centre the pills (y 20 to 100 at 1080 high) overlap the discovery card (`ui_mission_control.odin`, top centre from the safe area's top, y 54 to 110 at UI scale 1) while it shows. Accepted: the card is transient and takes no input; a later item can move it below the pills while the overlay is on (`TODO.md`).
- Back and Start moved to the top centre (new anchor `top_center`, signed x offsets from the centre line, y from the top edge; centres at y 60, x -99 and +92, same size): at the bottom centre they covered hotbar slots 3 to 6. `test_no_touch_overlay_element_covers_a_hotbar_slot` checks every element against `hud_hotbar_rectangles` at 2424 by 1080 and 1280 by 720, UI scale 1, for every selected slot; it fails with the old bottom centre layout (checked).
- The `dead_zone` key is gone from the file, the parser and the docs: the backend's `STICK_DEADZONE` applies after the merge, as for any stick.
- The overlay's look delta is converted to the mouse delta's window units (`render_pixels_to_window_units` in `read_touch_overlay_frame`); on the phone they are the same.
- `Raw_Gamepad.on_screen`, set on the overlay's gamepad alone, is skipped by `detect_input_device`, so a touch press leaves touch the active device. A merged physical gamepad keeps `on_screen` false.
- The Touch controls tooltip and `doc/input.md` say that on the desktop the mouse is the finger, so mouse look, gyro and mouse mining are off while the overlay drives the world.
- `free` in `update_touch_overlay` became `free_index`.
