# 0266: The terrain manipulator

Status: todo (2026-10-05, from the user, after 0265 and the tech tree of 0237)

## Goal

A high tech tool that improves or simplifies the ground work of 0265's hand tools: a larger brush, a flatten and a fill, a smooth, at a cost in power or charge, unlocked late in the tech tree. Which of these, and what it costs, is decided when the chapters of 0237 place it.

## Controls

Mine and Place as the hand tools; a mode or a size is chosen on the inventory view or a panel, never by cycling a world control (Code rules, keybindings). The control design is written before the design stage starts.
