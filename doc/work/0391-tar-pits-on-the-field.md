# 0391: Tar pits on the field

Status: todo (2026-11-13)

Found by 0376's design. The field generates no tar, so on a planet the tar pit pump has no source to stand on, and it produces wherever it is placed. 0376 gives the offshore pump its source and leaves this pump alone, since refusing it everywhere would take the oil chapter out of every field playthrough.

A planet has tar pits, and the tar pit pump produces only on one.

None.

- Chapter 6 on the field: its quests can be met from the home's surroundings, or their text says where tar is.
