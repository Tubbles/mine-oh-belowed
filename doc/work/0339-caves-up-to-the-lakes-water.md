# 0339: Caves up to the lakes' water

Status: implementing (2026-11-11)

0327 keeps its caves and notches out of every lake's bound sphere plus 4 m (`cave_lake_fade`). The sphere is sized from 0312's refine window, not from the water: about 300 m round each pit (`bound_radius`, `find_planet_lakes`). With 0326's lakes over the whole planet (1.6 a square kilometre at 8 km) that leaves no cave under roughly half the land, as 0327's fix round reported for the 1500 m round the home. After 0327, on its tree.

## Goal

Caves and wall overhangs everywhere 0327's rules want them, up to a margin round a lake's water itself, and no cave that opens into a lake or drains one.

## Controls

None.

## Change

- The caves' fade near a lake reads the lake's water (its mask, its level and its floor) instead of its bound sphere, in integers, the same from every thread and in any order of asking.
- A lake's water never meets cave air: not at its shore, not under its floor, not through a notch in a wall beside it.
- Worlds: 0327's caves are new with it, so the rule lands with 0327 before a play build holds caves, or behind a recorded key if it cannot.
- The cost of the query per sample stays what 0327's fix round left (asked only for a sample that can be cave air).
- Docs: `doc/architecture.md` (World generation, the caves), `doc/content.md` if a key is added.

## Verify

- The checks and the suite, with 0327's lake test (`test_the_caves_leave_the_lakes`) holding cave air within a lake's old bound and no changed water.
- The share of the land within 1500 m of the 8 km home that can hold a cave, before and after.
- A capture of a cave mouth near a lake's shore, sent to the user.

## Specification (design, 2026-11-11)

Counts from probes on a copy of that tree with this rule in it (`tmp/0339/tree/src/probe_0339_test.odin`, logs `tmp/0339/probe_*.log`), the shipped home at 8000 m, default seed, tiles; counts only.

### The rule

The water of a lake is its mask's cells (0312's `LAKE_CELL_METRES` squares on the lake's plane, the water grown by one cell, as `planet_lake_holds` rounds a point) under its level, as a prism down from the level: the fill (`fill_field_chunk_lakes`) and the coarse grids (`planet_lake_density`) put water exactly there and nowhere else. A sample's distance to it: horizontally the distance on the lake's plane at the radius from the sample's projection to the nearest set cell's square, vertically the sample's height over the level (0 at or under it), the two combined by the root of the sum of squares. No cave or notch within `CAVE_LAKE_MARGIN_METRES` (4) of it, fading in over `CAVE_LAKE_FADE_METRES` (12) beyond, as 0327 faded from the bound sphere. So no cave lies under a lake at any depth, beside its water within the margin, or over it within the margin of the level; the shore within the margin, which 0312's seal holds the water with, stays the height map's.

A lake is read only for a sample within its bound sphere plus `CAVE_LAKE_LIST_METRES` (0327's gate, kept): 0312's bound holds every water sample, so a sample within the reach of the water lies inside, and the lists a chunk, a grid or a sample already take (every lake whose bound meets its box widened by `CAVE_LAKE_LIST_METRES`) hold every lake the rule reads. The box bound (`cave_box_bound`, `cave_sample_clear`) still reads no lake: the fade lies in 0 to `NOISE_ONE` and only raises a distance, so `generation_planet_bound.odin` is unchanged.

Counts: within 1500 m of the home 16 lakes, bound radius 295 to 307 m (mean 299), the water's farthest cell 21 to 147 m from the pit (mean 67), masks of 656 to 16,976 m² (mean 5,014). Of the land columns with a cave share on a 10 m grid (62,750), the lakes left a cave possible (fade above 0, 4 m under the relief) at 48.4 percent with 0327's bound and 98.4 percent with this rule; at full strength 45.4 and 97.3. 705 columns (1.1 percent) lie under a lake's level. A 20 m grid gives the same shares to 0.1.

### Reading the tiles without a copy per sample (0327's second review)

0327's `cave_lakes` lists the lakes per sample through `generation_lakes_meeting_box`, which clones the mask of every kept lake of the near tiles (`take_water_tile_finals`): 53 to 67 KB per call. A sample read alone (the sky march `field_sky_is_open` on a chunk not streamed, the water's missing sea samples `field_missing_sample_sea`, the spring climb `planet_spring_sample_at`, the tests) now reads the tiles' kept lakes in place while it holds the cache's mutex (`water_tile_cave_lake_fade`), which an eviction (`evict_water_tiles`, `free_water_tile`) also takes, so nothing it reads is freed under it; it copies no lake and no mask, only the near tiles' keys (the map `water_tiles_near` fills and `water_tile_keys_sorted`'s slice, at most 12 keys and under 0.5 KB with the shipped 1024 m tiles). A chunk or a grid keeps 0327's one list per box (`cave_lakes_generation`; 91 to 160 KB a chunk job by 0327's second review, freed per job). The set of lakes read is the same either way (each the kept lakes whose bound plus the reach holds the sample), and the fade is their least, so the answer is the same bytes: probe, the bounded chunks and grids at a shore against `planet_sample_reading` with tiles, 0 differences.

`planet_sample` reads the density only (shortcut, column, cave column, `planet_sample_at_column`), not `planet_sample_reading`, whose shade march (`carved_air_roofed`) it computed and dropped: inside carved air that march reads the lakes once more per spacing up to the roof. Same samples (probe: 160 marches, 0 answers changed).

The bound: a lake query allocates at most 0.5 KB (the keys); a sky march reads the lakes at most once per sample it steps through (quarter spacing steps, at most 4 samples a spacing), and only from the generation's carved air up to its first ground or a spacing over the relief, where a cave lies within `shell_metres` of the relief and a notch within its box; with the shipped caves and gorges (a 32 m shell, gorges 26 m deep, so every notch lies within the shell) at most 4 (32 m plus 2 spacings) over the spacing samples: 69 KB a march at 1000 mm and 200 KB at 333 mm, and 130 marches a tick at most 9 MB and 26 MB. Measured over 80 marches up from cave air by the lakes within 900 m of the home with the dense test caves: at most 1,340 B (10 allocations, the largest 256 B) at 1000 mm and 4,824 B at 333 mm, where 0327's listing took 41,940 B and 150,984 B (the largest allocation a 4,664 B mask).

### Changed files

`src/generation_planet_waters.odin` (add `import "base:intrinsics"`):
- `planet_lake_mask_run :: proc(lake: Planet_Lake, first, last, v: i64) -> u64`, after `planet_lake_mask_bit`: the mask's bits of cells `first` to `last` of row `v` (all inside the window, `last - first < 64`), bit `k` the cell `first + k`: `index := (v + half) * (2 * half + 1) + first + half`, `word := index >> 6`, `shift := uint(index & 63)`, `run := mask[word] >> shift`, or'ed with `mask[word + 1] << (64 - shift)` when `shift + count > 64`, masked to `count` bits when `count < 64`. Called by the next.
- `planet_lake_mask_squared_distance :: proc(lake: Planet_Lake, east, north, step, reach: i64) -> i64`: the least squared distance on the plane from (east, north) (position units) to a set cell's square, at most `reach * reach`. `cells := (reach + step - 1) / step`; the point's cell `floor_divide_i64(east + step / 2, step)` and likewise north (`planet_lake_holds`'s rounding); the rows `centre_v - cells` to `centre_v + cells` and the columns `centre_u - cells` to `centre_u + cells`, each clipped to the window `-half` to `half` (none left: `reach * reach`); per row `gap_v := max(abs(north - v * step) - step / 2, 0)`, skipped when `gap_v * gap_v` is already at least the best; its run; with `split := centre_u - first`, the nearest set cell at or right of the point's column (`first + max(split, 0) + count_trailing_zeros(split <= 0 ? run : run >> uint(split))`) and left of it (`first + 63 - count_leading_zeros(run & ((1 << split) - 1))`, only for `split > 0`), each `gap_u := max(abs(east - u * step) - step / 2, 0)`, best the least `gap_u * gap_u + gap_v * gap_v`. In a row the nearest set cell on each side of the point's own cell has the least gap, so two per row suffice. The run fits a word while `cells` is at most 31 (`#assert` below).
- `planet_lake_water_distance :: proc(lake: Planet_Lake, radius, distance: i64, on_sphere: [3]i64, reach: i64) -> i64`: the distance from a sample (its distance from the centre and its point on the sphere) to the lake's water, at most `reach`: `over := max(distance - radius - lake.level, 0)`, `reach` when `over >= reach`; the plane `planet_lake_plane(lake, radius, metres(LAKE_CELL_METRES))`, `waters_plane_coordinates` of `on_sphere` (`reach` when not ok); `min(integer_square_root(across + over * over), reach)` with `across` from the previous. The root floors, so a distance errs short by under a position unit and the fade errs towards no cave. Called by `cave_lake_fade_of`.

### Questions decided

1. The vertical part is none under the mask at any depth, not a gap under the floor: `floor` is the pit's cell on the 2 m grid, not a bound of the bed (the refine's flood passes inner saddles into lower cells, and the relief dips between cells), and the land under the lakes' levels is 1.1 percent.
2. The horizontal distance is the plane's at the radius, `planet_lake_holds`'s cells, not a 3D one at the sample's height: the same cells the fill reads. It exceeds the 3D distance by at most 0.6 percent (the gnomonic scale within a 300 m window at 4 km) plus 0.3 (a lake under the radius, over a sea at -11 m, at 4 km), 3 cm at the margin; a lake over the radius only makes it shorter than the 3D one.
3. The margin and the fade stay 4 and 12 m: the margin counts from the grown mask, a cell past the water, and leaves 3 or more samples of untouched rock at 1 m; the fade tapers a tunnel and a chamber instead of cutting them flat at the margin. With the dense caves the nearest cave air lies 7.3 m from lake water.
4. 0327's bound gate stays inside the rule: it is what the chunks', grids' and tiles' lists are exact for, so the lists, `cave_lakes_generation` and the box bound stay as they are.
5. The lake test and the 3D margin check are one test: the 3D check needs the shore chunks, which hold the water 0327's edge chunks did not, and the cave air inside the old bound comes from the same chunks; one tile cache, one generation.
6. A sample read alone reads the tiles in place rather than a list per march: fewer bytes (keys only, against one mask per lake per march) and no change to the light's code; `planet_sample` dropping the shade bounds the queries per march by its steps.
