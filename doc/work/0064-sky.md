# 0064 Sky and atmosphere

Status: todo
Milestone: M11

## Goal

A flat sky colour changes with the day. Sun and moon, stars, horizon haze and distance fog tied to the load radius so the world edge never shows.

## Deliverables

- Sun and moon discs on the day path, stars at night, a horizon gradient, dusk and dawn colours, distance fog that hides the load boundary.
- Sun direction feeds the day light tint already computed by the day cycle.
- Tests: the sun position per day tick, fog distance from the load radius.

## Verify

- Builds and tests pass.
- User: Screenshots at dawn, noon, dusk, night.
