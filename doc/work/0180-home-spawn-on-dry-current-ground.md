# 0180: The home spawn on dry, current ground

Status: verified (2026-10-05, implementer agent ac2836aa099bd5790, verified the same day with no fix round (three nits, none taken: the two machines test hashes once after the ticks and the join, the zero home site check is 0.8 degrees, the spawn scan per place edit), in `.claude/worktrees/0180` on `item/0180` from `main` at be6f528, the specification approved the same day with the decisions below; M13 follow up, from the switch of 0179; before or with the M14 items)

## Goal

Every new or joining player lands at the home on ground that is dry and standable, for every seed and however the home has been dug or built since the world began. Today `field_home_player` (`simulation_field.odin`) stands the player on the generated surface at the record's home whatever its height (only the default seed was measured dry, `doc/log/2026-10-03.md`), the spawn ignores a pit dug or a pad laid at the site, the spawn's pad pitch comes from the current data while the pod's frame keeps its saved pitch, and a home of latitude 0, longitude 0 reads as no home (`planet_home_direction`).

## Change

- Generation picks the home: from the record's point, the nearest surface sample above the sea level along a deterministic outward search on the sphere (the same on every machine), so a seed whose home point lies under the sea still starts dry; the chosen point is what the world file records.
- The planet record carries whether a home is set (a flag or the loader refusing 0/0), so no real point reads as absent.
- The spawn site is the home and the pod's forward as today, but the feet stand on the current ground there: the field as edited plus the frame table (`field_ground_under` over the loaded set), so a pit puts the player at its bottom and a pad on top of it; the pad distance uses the pod frame's saved pitch.
- `doc/architecture.md` (the spawn), `doc/content.md` (the home record) updated.

## Verify

- The build and check commands of 0168.
- Tests: a seed whose home point lies under the sea spawns on dry ground within a bounded distance; a home of 0/0 is a home; a pit dug at the spawn site puts a joining player at its bottom and a foundation laid there puts the joiner on it; two machines compute the same spawn from the same save.

## Specification (design, 2026-10-04)

### What changed since the item was written

The item predates the crater (0199), the cabin spawn (0221), the pod's frame saved whole and 0260. Today a new world stands the pod on the flat floor of a crater dug at the record's home (`make_crater_term`: the floor lies `crater.depth_metres` below the home's uncratered relief), and every new player, the first and every joiner, spawns in the pod's cabin 250 mm above its floor facing the door (`field_pod_spawn`, through `field_spawn_player` and `make_field_session_player`). The spawn reads the saved pod and its saved frame (`write_frame_tables`/`read_frame_tables`; an old pod of another size is replaced at load on a frame of its own by `upgrade_resized_pods`), so its pitch and axes are the save's, never the current data's. `field_home_player` (the generated surface at the home) is only the fallback for content without a pod: the shipped content always has one. Since 0260 `field_home_heading` normalises the home to spring chord, so the door's heading no longer depends on the spring's distance; `field_home_site` is the generated surface (the crater floor) at `planet_home_direction(planet.home)`. Everything that reads the home (the crater, the starter veins, the trees' clearing, the pod, the preview's cameras of 0184) reads `planet.home` of the session's planet, which is `make_recorded_planet` of the data and the world file's `Planet_Generation_Record`; `planet_generation_record(state.world.planet)` writes it into `world.sjson` at every save (`home` with `home_recorded`), and a joiner gets the host's `world.sjson` in the join snapshot. The cabin's floor is the crater floor's terrain: the pod's first `open_cells` box starts on row 0 and its cells are occupied but not solid. Measured this session (probes below): the shipped home's crater floor stands 6.0, 5.9 and 2.9 m above the sea at 4, 8 and 16 km with the default seed; with the shipped record, 24, 15 and 50 of the seeds 1 to 400 put the floor less than 1 m above the sea at 4, 8 and 16 km (the first such seeds are 9, 3 and 7).

The four goals now:

1. A dry home: still applies, and keeps its shape. The search runs once, for a new world, in whole degrees: the world file already records `home` as a `Planet_Home` of whole degrees, so the chosen point is recorded as it is, with no layout change. "Dry" is the crater floor (the pod's ground) at least 1 m above the sea level. A loaded world never searches: its pod already stands where its record says.
2. A home of latitude 0, longitude 0: still applies. The fix is to drop the special case: the data's `home` key is required (`missing_struct_key`), so only planets built in test code had the zero home, and `make_test_planet` names the pole explicitly instead. No flag, no record change.
3. The feet on the current ground: changes shape. The pitch part is met (the cabin spawn reads the saved frame). A foundation cannot reach the spawn: the cabin's cells are the pod's, and a new frame whose cell centre lies in them is refused (`new_frame_cells_meet_a_frame`, Frame_Cell_Taken). A pit dug under the cabin's floor is met by the physics: the joiner spawns in the air of the pit and drops to its bottom (probe: 2.7 m down for a sphere of 1.5 m radius centred 1.5 m under the floor). Ground raised in the cabin is the one remaining gap: the place's bury check looks at the players only, so with the cabin empty a place fills it, and the next joiner spawns inside the mound and stays buried (probe: ground from the floor to 1 m up, the joiner stuck 117 mm over the floor, `on_ground` true). The fix is to keep the pod spawn's capsule clear as a player's. `field_home_player` (no pod) is left as it is (question 2).
4. The docs: still applies, with the sections below.

### Procedures

`src/generation_planet_record.odin` (beside `planet_home_direction`), all integer and fixed point, no spacing dependence (`uncratered_relief` reads no spacing):

- Changed `planet_home_direction :: proc(home: Planet_Home) -> [3]i64`: the `if home == {} { return FRAME_NORTH }` goes; it is `planet_spring_direction` of the degrees for every home. Its comment loses the sentence about the north pole and says that latitude 0, longitude 0 is a home like any other (+x).
- New constants with comments: `HOME_SEARCH_DEGREES :: 5` (the search's reach in degrees of arc: about 350, 700 and 1400 m at 4, 8 and 16 km; every wet seed of 1 to 400 found dry ground within it, the farthest move 177, 144 and 280 m), `HOME_DRY_MARGIN_MILLIMETRES :: 1000` (the crater floor at least this above the sea level: one sample at the coarsest spacing, so no air sample of the cabin holds the sea's fill).
- New `planet_home_floor_height :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, crater: Planet_Crater, home: Planet_Home) -> i64`: the height above the radius the pod would stand on at the home: `make_crater_term(generation, crater, direction).floor_height` for a crater, `uncratered_relief` at `fixed_scale(direction, generation.radius)` for the zero crater. Called by `planet_home_is_dry`.
- New `planet_home_is_dry :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, planet: Planet, home: Planet_Home) -> bool`: `planet_home_floor_height(generation, planet.crater, home) >= metres_to_position_units(i64(planet.sea_level_metres)) + millimetres_to_position_units(HOME_DRY_MARGIN_MILLIMETRES)`. Called by `find_dry_planet_home` and the tests.
- New `squared_chord :: proc(first, second: [3]i64) -> i64`: the squared length of the difference (unit vectors of `UNIT_VECTOR_ONE` = 2^24, so at most 3 * 2^50, inside an i64). Called by `find_dry_planet_home`.
- New `find_dry_planet_home :: proc(seed: u64, planet: Planet) -> (home: Planet_Home, found: bool)`: `generation := make_planet_generation(seed, planet, DEFAULT_SAMPLE_SPACING_MILLIMETRES)`; the record's home when it is dry; otherwise, over latitude from `max(-90, planet.home.latitude_degrees - HOME_SEARCH_DEGREES)` to `min(90, ... + HOME_SEARCH_DEGREES)` ascending and longitude -179 to 180 ascending (at latitude 90 and -90 only longitude 0, the pole once), the candidate whose `squared_chord` to `planet_home_direction(planet.home)` is at most `squared_chord(planet_home_direction({}), planet_home_direction({0, HOME_SEARCH_DEGREES}))` and strictly less than the best so far and which `planet_home_is_dry`. The strict less keeps the first in that order, so a tie goes to the lower latitude, then the lower longitude, on every machine. found is false when no candidate is dry. Pure, no log. Called by `new_world_home` and the tests.
- New `new_world_home :: proc(seed: u64, planet: Planet) -> Planet_Home`: `find_dry_planet_home`; when the result differs from `planet.home`, logs `world: the home at latitude %d, longitude %d lies under the sea with seed %d, the pod lands at latitude %d, longitude %d`; when none is found, logs `world: no dry ground within %d degrees of the home at latitude %d, longitude %d with seed %d, the pod lands there` and returns `planet.home`; silent when the record's home is dry. Called by `resolve_session_planet`.

### Open questions answered

- Whole degrees or a finer recorded point: whole degrees. The record's `home` already holds them, so nothing in the world file or the planet record changes and every reader of `planet.home` follows; a finer point would need a new record field and a remap. The cost is coarse moves (17 to 280 m), accepted since only a wet seed moves.
- What "dry" measures: the crater floor (the uncratered relief less the depth), since the pod and the cabin stand on it, with 1 m to spare; not the uncratered surface, which is 3 m higher with the shipped crater.
- Where the search runs: `resolve_session_planet`, once per new world, so the session's planet holds the chosen home before the field, the streaming, the veins, the water and the save read it. Not on load.
- The zero home: no flag; the special case goes, since the data requires the key.
- The "current ground" of the cabin spawn: the spawn point itself stays a pure function of the saved pod (no field read at the spawn, since a joiner's chunks may not be loaded on the tick it joins); the field is kept from burying it instead.

### Decisions at the approval (main agent, 2026-10-05)

1. Question 1: a refusal of its own. `Field_Edit_Refusal.Would_Bury_Spawn` with the string key `field_refused_would_bury_spawn` ("That would bury where players arrive") in every string file under `data/strings/`, the preview's refusal switch (`loop_planet_preview.odin`) gaining its case beside `Would_Bury_Player`, and `test_ground_raised_in_the_cabin_is_refused` asserting `.Would_Bury_Spawn`.
