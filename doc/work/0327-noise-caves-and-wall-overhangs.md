# 0327: Noise caves and overhangs along the walls

Status: designed (2026-11-11)

0324 carves its arches, grottos and mushroom rocks as shapes under 0323's sites and left out the rest of its scope: Minecraft's noise caves (cheese, spaghetti, noodle) and overhangs along the gorge walls and the mesas' tier edges. Each needs a three-dimensional noise at every sample of the shell near the surface, on a relief 0306 made three times as costly, and air under ground that the generated light of 0324 shades everywhere instead of in a few chunks. After 0314 (the generation's cost).

## Goal

Caves that open from the hillsides and run under the ground, and walls that overhang, generated deterministically, without a slower fill of the view.

## Controls

None.

## Change

- A three-dimensional density term bounded to a shell under the surface and to where it is wanted (hills, ranges, gorge walls, tier edges), integer and fixed point. None under the home's calm circle.
- The generated light shades all of its roofed air, on 0324's seed at arrival.
- The readers of 0324's table follow it.
- What a cave under a player's building does: a rule the design proposes (no caves under ground a player has edited, or none near the start, or accepted).
- Old worlds keep their ground through the record.
- The cost per sample and per view measured against 0314's.
- Docs: `doc/content.md` (Planets), `doc/architecture.md` (World generation).

## Verify

- The checks and the suite, with the bound, determinism and old world parity tests.
- Captures into a cave mouth and along an overhanging gorge wall, sent to the user.

### The shape

After Minecraft 1.18's noise caves, a subset, in integers and fixed point:

- Tunnels (its spaghetti 2D): along the zero lines of a three dimensional gradient noise on levels at fixed heights over the radius, `level_metres` apart and swung up and down together by a noise of the column. The levels are absolute, so the terrain cuts them: a tunnel opens where a hillside, a gorge wall or a mesa's cliff falls through its level, and runs under the ground elsewhere. Its cross section is an ellipse of `tunnel_width_millimetres` by `tunnel_height_millimetres` in half axes, measured across by the noise over its slope (`CAVE_NOISE_SLOPE_PERCENT`, the probe's median slope at the zero lines, 1.20 per wavelength), so a tunnel narrows and widens where the noise steepens and flattens (half of it within about 0.7 to 1.4 of the width, a flat stretch a low room).
- Chambers (its cheese): where a gradient noise read at the point squashed along the radial by `CAVE_CHAMBER_SQUASH_PERCENT` lies under minus `chamber_threshold_percent`, keeping `roof_metres` of rock under the height map, so a chamber never opens upward; a tunnel crossing one is its door.
- Undercuts along the walls: a notch at half a wall's height cut `undercut_reach_millimetres` back into a gorge's wall or a mesa tier's cliff, `undercut_height_millimetres` in half height, where a noise of the column leaves one, so the wall above it overhangs. Analytic per column (the gorge and the mesa know their walls), no three dimensional noise.
- Where: the cave share is the wildness times `region_caves_percent` at the landforms' region (none at its three lowest knots), times the presence ramp of a noise of the column; none within the home's calm circle (wildness 0); none deeper than `shell_metres` under the height map, the tunnels narrowing and the chambers shrinking over the last `CAVE_SHELL_FADE_METRES`; no cave air under the sea level plus `dry_metres`; none within a lake's bound plus `CAVE_LAKE_MARGIN_METRES`, fading over `CAVE_LAKE_FADE_METRES` beyond. The undercuts take only the lakes' rule and their walls' own patches.
- The levels coarser than 2 m (`CAVE_GRID_MOST_SPACING_MILLIMETRES`) have neither caves nor undercuts: the chunks and levels 0 and 1 at the default spacing do, so a cave mouth shows to 160 m.

Rejected:
- Noodles: their 1 to 3 m tunnels lie under the field's metre and cannot be walked.
- Three dimensional spaghetti (two isotropic noises): as many of its tunnels climb steeper than the walkable angle as run level.
- Levels relative to the height map: a tunnel at a fixed depth never meets the surface.
- A radial roof over the tunnels too: a roof r keeps a tunnel r / tan θ short of a face of slope θ, so no hillside mouth opens; only the chambers keep one.
- Overhangs from a three dimensional noise in the ground's density (Minecraft's own): a noise at every sample of every wall, and a wall is not known without the slope, where the gorge and the mesa know theirs.
- Caves in the levels of 4 and 8 m: a tunnel of 5 m sampled at 4 or 8 m aliases into dents and holes on the far ground.

### New file `src/generation_planet_caves.odin` (world cluster)

Header comment: the caves and the walls' undercuts (work item 0327, doc/architecture.md, World generation), after Minecraft 1.18's noise caves: tunnels along the zero lines of a noise on levels at fixed heights, which the terrain cuts into mouths on its walls, and chambers where a squashed noise lies low under a roof; notches at half a gorge wall's or a mesa cliff's height. A sample's depth is the least of the column's (0324) and theirs; the cave share comes from the landforms' reading, so no cave lies in the calm circle; none deeper than the shell, under the sea level plus the dry margin or by a lake. Integers and fixed point, a pure function of the position and the record.

Constants, a line each: `CAVE_NOISE_SLOPE_PERCENT :: 120` (a gradient noise's median slope at its zero lines, NOISE_ONE per wavelength in percent, which turns a noise into metres); `CAVE_SHELL_FADE_METRES :: 8`; `CAVE_PRESENCE_FADE_PERCENT :: 6` (the presence's and the undercut noise's ramp); `CAVE_CHAMBER_SQUASH_PERCENT :: 160` (the chambers' noise runs this much faster along the radial, so they are wider than tall); `CAVE_LAKE_MARGIN_METRES :: 4`, `CAVE_LAKE_FADE_METRES :: 12`; `CAVE_LAKE_LIST_METRES :: CAVE_LAKE_MARGIN_METRES + CAVE_LAKE_FADE_METRES` (a chunk's or a grid's box widened by it lists every lake the fade reads at its samples); `CAVE_GRID_MOST_SPACING_MILLIMETRES :: 2000`; `CAVE_NONE :: i64(1) << 40` (the distance of no cave); `UNDERCUT_BAND_METRES :: 4` (a notch is computed this far past its reach, where its distance is at least 2 m); `UNDERCUT_LEAST_REACH_MILLIMETRES :: 500`; the salts on `generation.cave_seed`: `CAVE_PRESENCE_SALT :: 0`, `CAVE_LEVEL_SALT :: 1`, `CAVE_TUNNEL_SALT :: 2`, `CAVE_CHAMBER_SALT :: 3`, `UNDERCUT_SALT :: 4`.

Types:
- `Cave_Column :: struct {strength, offset: i64}`: a column's cave strength (0 to NOISE_ONE, the share times the presence) and its levels' swing in position units.
- `Wall_Undercut :: struct {offset, centre, reach, half_height: i64}`: a column's notch, position units: its offset from the wall's face at the notch's centre (positive into the rock), the centre's height over the radius, the reach (0 none) and the half height.
- `Gorge_Cut :: struct {cut, noise, width, root: i64}`: what `landmark_gorge_cut` reads: the cut, the meandered noise's absolute value, the width (both in noise units) and the strength's root.

Procedures, in this order, each with a one line comment:
- `cave_share :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, reading: Landform_Reading) -> i64`: 0 when `generation.caves` is zero or the wildness is 0; else `landform_curve(caves.region_caves_percent, reading.region, NOISE_ONE) / 100`, 0 when at most 0, times the wildness over NOISE_ONE. Called by `uncratered_column`.
- `cave_column :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, column: Surface_Column, on_sphere: [3]i64) -> Cave_Column`: `{}` at a share of 0; the presence noise (salt 0, `presence_wavelength_metres`) at the point, `strength := share * landmark_ramp(noise, threshold, CAVE_PRESENCE_FADE_PERCENT * NOISE_ONE / 100) / NOISE_ONE`; while above 0, `offset := metres(level_swing_metres) * level_noise / NOISE_ONE` (salt 1, `level_wavelength_metres`, at the point). Two noises per column.
- `cave_lakes :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, position: World_Position) -> []Planet_Lake`: `generation.lakes` without tiles; with `generation.water_tiles` (0326) `generation_lakes_meeting_box` over the position plus and minus `metres(CAVE_LAKE_LIST_METRES)`.
- `cave_lake_fade :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, position: World_Position) -> i64`: NOISE_ONE, lowered by every listed lake whose bound sphere's centre lies within `bound_radius + margin + fade` of the position to `clamp((root - bound_radius - margin) * NOISE_ONE / fade, 0, NOISE_ONE)` (the root only inside that reach). The bound holds the lake's water (0312), so no cave or notch comes within the margin of it.
- `cave_distance :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, cave: Cave_Column, relief: i64, position: World_Position, distance: i64, on_sphere: [3]i64) -> i64`: `CAVE_NONE` at a strength of 0; with `height := distance - radius`, `depth := relief - height`: the strength times `clamp((metres(shell_metres) - depth) * NOISE_ONE / metres(CAVE_SHELL_FADE_METRES), 0, NOISE_ONE)` and `cave_lake_fade`, each over NOISE_ONE, `CAVE_NONE` at 0; the tunnel: `u := height - cave.offset`, `level := metres(level_metres)`, `up := u - level * floor_divide_i64(u + level / 2, level)` (from minus half a level to half), `across := tunnel_noise * wave / (CAVE_NOISE_SLOPE_PERCENT * NOISE_ONE / 100)` (salt 2 at the position, `wave` the tunnels' wavelength in position units), `width := millimetres(tunnel_width_millimetres)`, `stretched := up * width / millimetres(tunnel_height_millimetres)`, `tunnel := integer_square_root(across² + stretched²) - width * strength / NOISE_ONE` (`CAVE_NONE` at a width of 0); the chamber: `squashed[axis] := on_sphere[axis] + on_sphere[axis] * (height * (CAVE_CHAMBER_SQUASH_PERCENT - 100) / 100) / radius`, `threshold := NOISE_ONE - (NOISE_ONE - chamber_threshold_percent * NOISE_ONE / 100) * strength / NOISE_ONE`, `chamber := (chamber_noise + threshold) * wave / (CAVE_NOISE_SLOPE_PERCENT * NOISE_ONE / 100)` (salt 3 at `squashed`, its own wavelength; `CAVE_NONE` at 100 percent), raised to at least `metres(roof_metres) - depth`; the result `max(min(tunnel, chamber), sea_radius + metres(dry_metres) - distance)`. Negative inside a cave; the tunnel's axis lies on a level and the noise's zero line, its cross section an ellipse of the two half axes.
- `undercut_distance :: proc(undercut: Wall_Undercut, height: i64) -> i64`: `CAVE_NONE` at a reach of 0, else `ellipse_distance(undercut.offset, undercut.reach, height - undercut.centre, undercut.half_height)`.
- `gorge_undercut :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, gorge: Gorge_Cut, relief, floor: i64) -> Wall_Undercut`: none while the undercuts are off or the width is 0; `full := metres(gorge_metres) * gorge.root / NOISE_ONE`, the face at half the full cut lies at the meandered noise `middle := (NOISE_ONE - (NOISE_ONE - floor_share) / 4) * gorge.width / NOISE_ONE` (`landmark_gorge_cut`'s shape is a half there); `offset := (gorge.noise - middle) * metres(gorge_wavelength_metres) / (CAVE_NOISE_SLOPE_PERCENT * NOISE_ONE / 100)`, `centre := relief - full / 2`; a notch when `|offset| < reach + metres(UNDERCUT_BAND_METRES)`, `centre - half >= floor` and `full / 2 >= half + metres(1)`, its reach and half height the record's. `relief` is the column's before the landmarks.
- `mesa_undercut :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, noise, strength, relief: i64) -> Wall_Undercut`: the threshold as `mesa_raise_at`'s; for tier 0, and tier 1 when `mesa_tier_percent` (in NOISE_ONE) is at least the edge, the edge's middle `threshold + tier * index + edge / 2`, `offset := (noise - middle) * metres(mesa_wavelength_metres) / (CAVE_NOISE_SLOPE_PERCENT * NOISE_ONE / 100)`, the tier with the least `|offset|` within the band as above; `centre := relief + index * metres(mesa_metres) + metres(mesa_metres) / 2` (the smoothstep's middle is half the tier); a notch only when `metres(mesa_metres) / 2 >= half + metres(1)`.
- `undercut_presence :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, point: [3]i64, undercut: Wall_Undercut) -> Wall_Undercut`: a found notch's reach times `landmark_ramp(noise, threshold, CAVE_PRESENCE_FADE_PERCENT * NOISE_ONE / 100)` of the undercut noise (salt 4 at the point) over NOISE_ONE; `{}` under `UNDERCUT_LEAST_REACH_MILLIMETRES`. One noise per notch column, called by `landmark_relief`.
- `sample_depth :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, column: Surface_Column, cave: Cave_Column, position: World_Position, distance: i64, on_sphere: [3]i64) -> i64`: `column_depth`; in a notch column its least with `undercut_distance` of the notch whose reach is scaled by `cave_lake_fade` at the position (skipped at a fade of 0); while the cave's strength is above 0 and the depth above minus a spacing, its least with `cave_distance` (a deeper air sample's density saturates already). The lakes are read at the sample, so a chunk's list covers them.
- `carved_air_roofed :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, column: Surface_Column, cave: Cave_Column, position: World_Position, distance: i64, on_sphere: [3]i64) -> bool`: true at once for a sample `roof_metres` or more under `column.relief` in a column without a notch and outside a carving site (a column's air open to the sky is a tunnel's band of at most twice its half height from the top, and `caves_problem` keeps a spacing of rock under it at the coarsest spacing with caves, so a chamber is always roofed); else steps of one spacing up the sample's radial (`position[axis] * (distance + k * spacing) / distance`) while under `column.relief`, true at the first step whose `sample_depth` gives a density above 0, false at the top. The same column and cave column at every step.
- `cave_box_bound :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, lowest, highest: World_Position, box: Point_Box, relief: Value_Range, reading: Landform_Range) -> (caves: bool, floor, strength: i64, offset: Value_Range)`: the steps of `cave_column` and `cave_distance` on ranges (the probe's `probe_cave_box`): false when the caves are off, the wildness's or the share's highest is 0 (`landform_curve_range` of the region), the presence's ramp at its noise's highest is 0, the shell's fade at the depth's lowest (`relief.lowest` less the positions' highest height, from `squared_chord_range` of the positions to the centre and its roots) is 0, the box lies a spacing or more under the dry level, or the least tunnel and the least chamber distance are both at least a spacing. The least tunnel: the least `|up|` over the heights less the swing's range (0 when a multiple of the level lies in it, else the nearer gap), the least `|across|` from `lattice_noise_range` of the tunnel noise over the positions' box (0 when it straddles 0), through the same steps with the strength's highest. The least chamber: the chamber noise's lowest over the box of the squashed points (per axis the projected box plus `range_product` of it and the heights' range times the squash, over the radius), plus the threshold at the strength's highest, through the scale, raised to the roof less the depth's highest. The lakes are not read (they only take caves away). `floor := max(relief.lowest - metres(shell_metres), dry level - spacing)`: no cave changes a sample at or under it; `strength` the share's highest times the presence ramp's (before the shell's fade) and `offset` the swing's range, for `cave_sample_clear`. In `generation_planet_bound.odin`.
- `cave_sample_clear :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, bound: Relief_Box_Bound, position: World_Position, distance: i64, on_sphere: [3]i64) -> bool`: whether no cave changes a stone sample the bands decided, from the bound's ranges and the two noises at the sample (the probe's `probe_cave_pre_test`): true a spacing or more under the dry level, or at a shell fade of 0 at the depth's lowest (`bound.relief.lowest` less the height), or when the least tunnel (`bound.cave_strength` faded there, the least `|up|` over the height less `bound.cave_offset`, the tunnel noise at the position) and the least chamber (the roof less the depth's highest, and only while that is under a spacing the chamber noise at the squashed point with the threshold at that strength) are both at least a spacing. In `generation_planet_bound.odin`.
- `undercut_box_bound :: proc(generation: Planet_Generation, box: Point_Box, relief: Value_Range, reading: Landform_Range) -> (undercut: bool, floor: i64)`: false while the undercuts or the landmarks are off or the wildness's highest is 0; a gorge's when its patch may hold the box (`landmark_gorge_most`'s mask test) and the meandered noise's least comes under its full width plus the band in noise units (`(reach + band) * CAVE_NOISE_SLOPE_PERCENT * NOISE_ONE / 100 / wave + 1`), floor `relief.lowest - metres(gorge_metres) / 2 - half`; a mesa's when `landmark_mesa_range`'s strength is above 0 and the roughened noise's range meets the lowest edge middle (the threshold at the strength's highest) less the band to the highest (at the strength's lowest, plus the tier) plus the band, floor `relief.lowest - metres(mesa_metres) / 2 - half`. In `generation_planet_bound.odin`.
