# 0082 Ground cover per biome

Status: implemented
Milestone: M11

## Goal

Moved out of 0058 and 0061: grass tufts, flowers and dead wood as decoration on the ground, per biome, so plains, forests, steppe and wetlands read as living ground rather than flat grass.

## Deliverables

- Cross shaped blocks (0061) in `data/blocks.sjson`: `grass_tuft`, `tall_grass`, `flower_red`, `flower_yellow`, `dead_bush`, `reeds`, each non solid, no collision, mined instantly, dropping nothing (the tuft and tall grass) or a `sapling` like item where it makes sense later; textures from the generator with transparency.
- `Biome_Definition` gains `ground_cover = [{block = "grass_tuft", chance = 0.2}, ...]`: generation places one cover block on a column's top block from a per column hash when the column is dry land, not in a vein footprint and the cell above the surface is air after trees and boulders, so cover never floats and never blocks an outcrop. Plains and forest get tufts and flowers, steppe dry tufts and dead bushes, wetland reeds and tufts, badlands and desert a rare dead bush, cold barrens nothing.
- Walking through cover is free (no collision), mining it is instant, and placing a block on a cover cell replaces it.
- Tests: cover only on dry land top blocks with air above, the chance per biome over a sample, no cover on outcrops, placement replaces cover, and determinism.

## Verify

- Builds and tests pass.
- User: Walk a plain and a wetland; screenshots.

## Notes

Implementation pointers (main agent, 2026-09-28), decisions taken so the item is unambiguous:

- Blocks in `data/blocks.sjson`, all `shape = "cross"`, `solid = false`, `hardness_seconds = 0.05`, tool tier 0: `grass_tuft`, `tall_grass`, `flower_red`, `flower_yellow`, `dead_bush`, `reeds`. Every minable block must yield an item (`validate_item_drops`), so each gets an item of category `block` in `data/items.sjson` that places it back (`places_block`), price 1, with strings; that also lets the player decorate. Textures: the generator (`tools/make_placeholder_textures.py`) gains a `cover` family checked before the others by id (`tuft`, `tall_grass`, `flower`, `bush`, `reeds`), drawing a plant silhouette with transparent texels (blades, a stem with a coloured head, a bare twiggy bush, tall reeds), and the item icons reuse the block file as for other block items. Run it and commit only the new files; report if it rewrote any existing one.
- Data: `Biome_Definition` (`src/generation_biome.odin`) gains `ground_cover = [{block = "grass_tuft", chance = 0.2, on = ["grass"]}, ...]`: `block` the cover block, `chance` 0 to 1 per column, `on` the top blocks it may stand on (ids). Validation: blocks exist, chances in range, the cover block has the cross shape, `on` blocks exist. Plains: tufts 0.25 and tall grass 0.05 and both flowers 0.02 on grass; forest: tufts 0.15 on grass; steppe: tufts 0.1 (dry) and dead bush 0.04 on dry grass; wetland: reeds 0.2 and tufts 0.1 on mud; highland: tufts 0.1 on grass; badlands and desert: dead bush 0.01 on red rock and sand; the rest none.
- Generation (`src/generation_features.odin`, a new `apply_ground_cover` after `clear_above_outcrops` and before `apply_landing_pad` in `generate_chunk_blocks`, `src/generation_chunk.odin`): per column of the chunk, when the surface is dry land, the column is not in a vein footprint (`column_in_vein_footprint`), the top block is in a cover entry's `on` list and the cell above it is air, one roll from a column hash (new `Generation_Purpose` `Ground_Cover`, appended) picks at most one entry by its chance (entries in order, the roll compared against the running sum), and its block goes into the cell above the surface. The pad stamp runs after and clears its own cells, so cover never sits on the pad. Cover is not opaque, so `find_open_columns` and the sky light are unaffected (check `column_light_top` treats a cross as not blocking).
- Placement: in `place_block_with_player` (`src/player_interaction.odin`) a target whose block is a cross shape takes the placed block in its own cell (the cover is replaced), and `placement_allowed` treats a cross cell as free. For machines, `cell_is_free` (`src/entity_placement.odin`) accepts a cross block and `commit_placement` clears cover from the footprint cells it takes. Mining cover is instant and drops its item; walking through it is free (no collision boxes for a cross, 0061).
- Generator version: `GENERATOR_VERSION` goes to 5 (`src/generation_terrain.odin`), since unmodified chunks change.
- Tests (`src/generation_features_test.odin` or `src/generation_biome_test.odin`, `src/player_interaction_test.odin`, `src/entity_placement_test.odin`): cover only on listed top blocks with air above and on dry land; none in a vein footprint or on the pad; the share of covered columns of a plains sample is near the summed chances; determinism across chunk borders (the column hash, not the chunk); block placement replaces cover; a machine placed over cover clears it; validation rejects a cover block that is not a cross and an unknown `on` block; the item and recipe counts.
