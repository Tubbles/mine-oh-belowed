# 0072 Lighting polish

Status: implemented
Milestone: M11

## Goal

Light is white and flat. Coloured light from lamps and furnaces, torch flicker, smooth day transitions, and sun shadows if they are cheap.

## Deliverables

- Block light carries a colour per emitter in data; the chunk shader blends it; torches flicker slightly; day transitions ease.
- A cheap sun shadow (a single cascade shadow map on the terrain near the player) behind a setting, measured on the couch machine.
- Tests: light colour propagation stays bounded and deterministic.

## Verify

- Builds and tests pass.
- User: Screenshots at night by a furnace.

## Notes

Scope decisions (main agent, 2026-09-28): day transitions are eased already (`daylight_blend` is a smoothstep over the sun height, and 0064 blends the sky colours through dawn and dusk), so that deliverable is done. This item delivers coloured block light, the flicker and the shadow map behind a setting.

Implementation pointers, decisions taken so the item is unambiguous:

- Storage: a cell's light grows from a `u8` (sky in the high nibble, block in the low) to a `u16` with four nibbles: sky, red, green, blue (`pack_light`, `light_level`, `with_light_level` in `src/world_light.odin`, `Chunk.light` in `src/world_chunk.odin`, the sky fill in `src/world_light_sky.odin`). `Light_Channel` becomes `Sky`, `Red`, `Green`, `Blue`; the removal and addition queues already carry the channel, so the propagation generalises to four channels with the same steps and budgets (`MAXIMUM_LIGHT_STEPS_PER_TICK` counts steps over all channels, so a coloured edit costs up to three times a white one, still bounded). Light is not saved, so the save format is untouched; the memory per chunk doubles (64 KB), which is fine.
- Emitters in data: `light_color = [r, g, b]` (0 to 15 each, optional) on blocks (`data/blocks.sjson`, `Block_Definition`) and on the lamp machine (`data/machines.sjson`), defaulting to `light_level` in all three channels (white); a block's `light_level` stays the largest channel and drives everything that reads one level (the mesher's flame cells, the sound, the markers). The torch gets a warm colour (15, 11, 6), the lamp a cool white (14, 14, 15), the furnace's and boiler's models keep their emissive glow (that is not light). `cell_emission` and `set_entity_light` carry the three channels.
- Meshing and shading: the vertex colour keeps sky in red, occlusion in blue and the sway in alpha; the block light's three channels go into the mesh's `normals` attribute (`Mesh_Part.normals`, uploaded like the tangents, `[3]f32` per vertex, smoothed per channel by `vertex_light` over the same four cells). `data/shaders/chunk.vs` passes it, `chunk.fs` computes `light = sky * day_factor * cloud * sky_tint + block_rgb * flicker` per channel; `water.vs` and `water.fs` the same. Models (`model_light_tint`, `src/model_motion.odin`) read the four channels from `world_get_light`.
- Flicker: a uniform `flicker` (0.96 to 1) from two sines of the render time (periods 0.17 and 0.41 seconds) multiplies the block light term in both fragment shaders, set with the other per frame uniforms; a pure `light_flicker(seconds) -> f32` with a test that it stays in range. Every block light flickers together, which reads as torchlight; lamps flicker three percent too, accepted.
- Shadows, in a new `src/render_shadows.odin`, behind a `shadows` setting (`src/settings.odin`, default false, a toggle on the Display tab, `src/ui_screens.odin`, `src/configuration_test.odin`, strings): when on, before the main pass, the chunk meshes within `SHADOW_RANGE_BLOCKS` (96) of the camera are drawn again into a 2048 by 2048 depth texture from an orthographic camera looking along the sun direction (`sun_direction` of 0064), the frustum a box of the range around the camera snapped to whole blocks so the map does not shimmer; raylib's `LoadRenderTexture` gives a depth renderbuffer that cannot be sampled, so the framebuffer is built with `rlgl.LoadFramebuffer`, `rlgl.LoadTextureDepth(width, height, false)` and `rlgl.FramebufferAttach`, and the depth pass uses a small `data/shaders/shadow.vs` and `shadow.fs` (position only, hot reloaded with the others). The chunk and water fragment shaders take the light space matrix and the depth texture (bound in a free material map slot as the cloud texture is), compare with a slope scaled bias and a two by two percentage closer filter, and darken the sky light term to 40 percent in shadow; nothing else receives shadows (models keep raylib's default shader). At night the pass is skipped and the shaders get `shadow_strength` 0. The Deck preset of 0076 keeps it off.
- Tests (`src/world_light_test.odin` extended, `src/render_shadows_test.odin`): the four nibble packing round trips, a red emitter lights only the red channel and two emitters of different colours mix by taking the larger level per channel, a removal darkens every channel, the propagation is deterministic and bounded as before, the mesher's block light channels per vertex, the flicker range, the light space matrix maps the camera's box into clip space and the snapping to whole blocks, the settings round trip, the UI audit with the toggle.
