# 0059 Bigger trees, species, felling

Status: implemented
Milestone: M11

## Goal

Trees are a trunk of a few blocks under a fixed crown. Species per biome, trunks of eight to sixteen blocks, real crowns, roots and clearings, and a felled trunk brings its crown down.

## Deliverables

- Tree species in `data/biomes.sjson`: trunk height range, crown shape (round, conical, flat), leaf and log blocks, density, clearings.
- Generation places crowns of several layers with roots at the base; forests dense with clearings; the outcrop clearing rule (0045) keeps footprints open.
- Felling: mining a trunk block drops the logs above it and the crown decays over a few seconds into leaf litter and saplings that count as items, so no floating canopies remain.
- Tests: species placement, no floating leaves after a felling in a test world, the landing site rule.
- Moved here from 0058: a tree species for every biome, the climate biomes (cold barrens, highland, badlands, steppe, wetland, coastal dunes) included, which until then grow the one tree shape at their `tree_density`.

## Verify

- Builds and tests pass.
- User: Fell a tree on the couch; screenshots.

- Biome species lists: forest oak 3 and birch 1; plains oak 1 and birch 1; highland pine 2 and birch 1; hills pine 1; cold barrens pine (density stays as it is); steppe acacia 3 and dead tree 1; wetland oak 1 and birch 2; badlands dead tree; beach and coastal dunes palm; desert dead tree at a tiny density if the biome has any; mountains none (density 0).
- Shapes, in `src/generation_features.odin` (new pure procedures next to `tree_leaves_contain`): `Tree` gains `species: int` and its crown from the species. Round: layers from two below the trunk top to two above, radius shrinking towards both ends with the corners cut. Conical: from `crown_radius` at about a third of the trunk up to 1 at the top and a tip block above, radius stepping down every two layers, alternating full and narrower layers so it reads as a fir. Flat: one full disc of `crown_radius` at the trunk top and a disc of radius 1 above it. None: trunk only. Roots for species with `roots` and a trunk of 8 or more: log blocks at surface level in two or three of the four directions from the root (from the hash), filling air only like every feature. Constants: `LEAF_REACH` becomes the largest crown radius (4) so `FEATURE_REACH` covers it, and `FEATURE_MAXIMUM_HEIGHT` becomes the tallest trunk plus its crown above (16 plus 4). Check what depends on them (`GENERATION_CEILING`, `feature_cell_range`, the spawn's `feature_near_spawn`) and report the generation time per chunk before and after (the 0057 budget: within one and a half times).
- Clearings: `Biome_Definition` gains `clearing_share` (0 to 1, default 0): a clearing noise (new `Generation_Purpose` `Clearings`, appended; wavelength 48) below the share's quantile leaves no trees, so a forest at 0.35 has open patches. Forest gets 0.35, plains 0.5 (with a higher `tree_density` so the groves read as groves), the rest 0. The outcrop clearing rule (`column_in_vein_footprint`) and the spawn's tree requirement stay.
- Felling, in a new `src/tree_felling.odin`: when the player mines a log block (`mine_block` in `src/player_interaction.odin`, after `world_set_block`), the log blocks straight above it in the column (any log species, up to `FEATURE_MAXIMUM_HEIGHT`) become loose `log` items at their cells (`spill_stack`, 0062) and their cells air, and every leaves block within `LEAF_REACH` horizontally and the trunk's height vertically of the felled column is scheduled for decay. Decay is a queue like the water flow (`Water_Flow` in `src/world_water.odin`, saved through `write_later_tables` in `src/save_state.odin` as the second later table, read only while bytes are left): a scheduled leaves block whose due tick has come (a delay of 1 to 4 seconds from a hash of the cell and the tick it was scheduled) is removed when no log block lies within `LEAF_SUPPORT_DISTANCE` (4, Manhattan) of it, with a 1 in 8 chance of a `leaves` loose item and a 1 in 20 chance of a `sapling`, both from the same hash, and its leaves neighbours are scheduled in turn; a supported leaf stays. Bounded per tick like the water updates. Only felling schedules decay, so leaves the player places stay unless a log near them is felled. `run_leaf_decay` runs in `tick_world` (`src/simulation_world.odin`) after the water. Machines placed by the player are entities, not blocks, so a trunk never falls onto machine cells; loose items lift themselves out of solid cells (0062).
