Status: verified (2026-10-04, pass A verified the same day with three nits fixed in the landing amend (the hatch comment, a docstring, the lab handover in DOOR.md), pass A lands alone so 0233 and 0234 can follow, pass B after the lab round; implementer agent a9f25c915235b5743, in `.claude/worktrees/0231` on `item/0231` from `main` after 0230 landed, the specification approved the same day with the decisions below; from the user on the phone with build 478 (0222 and the B button): "i dont want the doors to close with a delay, and the open and close animation should not be the door sliding up and down, of course i want the shutters to open and close like on a camera, and fast! ## Goal

Each of the pod's two doors is an iris that snaps open the moment any player's capsule comes within 15 cm of it and snaps shut the moment none is, like a camera shutter and as fast: no hold, no interlock, no facing test, no waiting for the other door. The 15 cm reach alone keeps both doors shut around a player in the middle of the 1 m bore, which is what the user asked of the airlock in the first place. Any player counts, standing or crouched; the crouch is only what fits through the 1 m door (0221, the user's rule), never a condition of the door.

## Controls

No binding changes. Interact on a hatch no longer toggles it and the HUD shows no Open or Close hint on one: the rule runs every tick, so a press would be undone on the next tick (a door opened from afar shuts at once, a door shut in front of a near player opens at once). A hatch is therefore like any other cell for the A press: it jumps (`without_field_interact_jump` no longer drops Jump on a hatch). This is the main agent's decision, named to the user with the item.

### The save

`save_binary.odin` reads a struct's fields by name (`read_struct_schema`: a field whose name the type no longer has gets `entry.field = -1`; `read_struct_body` then sets `reader.offset = end` and goes on). So a save written by 0222 with `Foundation.hatch_close_tick` loads into a `Foundation` without it: the field's 8 bytes are skipped, nothing is refused, `reader.problem` stays empty, and no log line is written or needed (nothing the player sees is lost: the field only timed the hold, which is gone). Decision: remove the field, do not keep it dead. A dead field would be written, hashed and carried in every join snapshot for nothing, and the skip by name is the reader's documented contract. A save with a door open and nobody near (any build 0198 to 0230) shuts it on the first tick after the load that is not inside a fall (the step's own rule); a door with a player in reach stays open. Tested by test 13 (the codec) and test 5 (the first tick).

- `iris_blade_transform :: proc(motion: Machine_Motion, footprint: [3]i32, blade: int, fraction: f32) -> matrix[4, 4]f32`: `rotation_about_pivot(motion.axis, 2 * math.PI * f32(blade) / f32(motion.blades), footprint_point_to_model(motion.pivot, footprint)) * motion_transform(motion, footprint, fraction)`. Pure.
- `Motion_Part_Transforms :: struct { transforms: [MAXIMUM_IRIS_BLADES]matrix[4, 4]f32, count: int }` and `motion_part_transforms :: proc(motion: Machine_Motion, footprint: [3]i32, phase: f32) -> Motion_Part_Transforms`: for `.Iris` `count = clamp(motion.blades, 1, MAXIMUM_IRIS_BLADES)` and `transforms[k] = iris_blade_transform(motion, footprint, k, phase)`; for every other kind `count = 1`, `transforms[0] = motion_transform(motion, footprint, phase)`. Pure, by value (1 KiB). Callers write `poses := motion_part_transforms(...)` and loop `for transform in poses.transforms[:poses.count]`.

The pose maths, in the model's frame (cells, x and z centred on the unrotated footprint, y from its bottom, +x the front): with `c = footprint_point_to_model(pivot, footprint)`, `h = footprint_point_to_model(hinge, footprint)`, `R(p, theta) = T(p) Rx(theta) T(-p)` (`rotation_about_pivot`, `T` a translation, `Rx` `axis_rotation_matrix` about the motion's axis), blade k of n at open fraction f is posed by `P_k(f) = R(c, 2 pi k / n) R(h, 2 pi a f)`, applied to the part's vertices as `P_k(f) v` (column vectors, the rightmost first: the blade opens about the first pin, then turns into its slot). This equals `R(h_k, 2 pi a f) R(c, 2 pi k / n)` with `h_k = R(c, 2 pi k / n) h`: the k-th blade turned into its slot first, then opened about its own pin, which is the item's formulation; the pin of blade k stays at `h_k` at every fraction. The direction: `axis_rotation_matrix` is right handed (about x a positive angle takes +y towards +z), so both the k / n turns and a positive amplitude turn counterclockwise seen from +x, the door's front, which faces out of the pod for both doors (both fixtures have rotation 0). The modeller is told: blade k is blade 0 turned k / n of a turn counterclockwise seen from the front, and opening turns each blade counterclockwise about its pin, so the blade must be modelled so that this turn swings it out of the aperture. The rendered open fraction is `hatch_open_fraction` as today (eased by `motion_stroke` over `period_seconds`), so f is presentation only, f32 as the slide's.

Every part draw goes through `motion_part_transforms` (one place, rather than a hatch only array from `hatch_pose`): `hatch_pose` keeps returning the open fraction as `Model_Pose.phase` and `draw_hatch` is unchanged.
