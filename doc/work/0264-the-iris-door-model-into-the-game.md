### The record (`data/machines.sjson`, `pod_hatch`)

The slide's two comment lines and `motion` line are replaced by:

```
		// A camera iris (0231, 0264): the part is one blade, modelled
		// shut, drawn 14 times round the 0.7 m aperture (radius 0.7
		// cells). Each curved leaf is pinned 0.78 cells from the axis
		// and a 0.15 turn about its pin swings it from the rim to past
		// the centre, in 0.15 s (9 ticks at 60 Hz).
		motion = {kind = "iris", axis = "x", blades = 14, pivot = [0.5, 1, 1], hinge = [0.5, 1.78, 1], amplitude = 0.15, period_seconds = 0.15}
```

These are the lab record's values (`tmp/pod_lab/data/machines.sjson`). ### Tests

- `test_the_shipped_pod_hatch_is_an_iris` (new, `src/model_motion_test.odin`, 0231's test 19): `make_test_machines`, the `pod_hatch` machine: motion kind `.Iris`, axis 0, blades 14, pivot `{0.5, 1, 1}`, hinge `{0.5, 1.78, 1}`, amplitude 0.15, `period_seconds` 0.15, `hatch_open_fraction(true, 101, 108, 0, 60, 0.15)` below 1 and `hatch_open_fraction(true, 101, 109, 0, 60, 0.15)` within 1e-5 of 1 (open in 9 ticks).
- `test_the_shipped_iris_covers_its_aperture_shut_and_clears_it_open` (new, `src/model_check_test.odin`, beside `test_the_shipped_models_pass_the_checks`): `shipped_machines`, `shipped_machine(machines, "pod_hatch")`, `load_machine_model_mesh(test_data_directory(), machine, context.temp_allocator)` (destroyed after). The blades are `model_layers_check_triangles(mesh.part, transform)` for every transform of `motion_part_transforms(machine.motion, machine.footprint, fraction)`. The aperture's centre in the model's frame is `footprint_point_to_model(machine.motion.pivot, machine.footprint)` (y 1, z 0), the plane is y and z. Shut (fraction 0): every sample point at radii 0.05 to 0.65 step 0.1 and every 10 degrees (252 points) lies inside the y z projection of at least one blade triangle (inclusive, with `MOTION_TEST_TOLERANCE`), so the shut iris shows no gap. Open (fraction 1): every blade vertex lies at least 0.68 cells from the axis in y z (the script lays the open leaf from radius 0.695), so the open iris leaves the aperture empty. One local pure helper in the test file: `point_in_triangle_y_z :: proc(point: [2]f32, corners: [3][3]f32) -> bool` (barycentric on the corners' y and z). A failure is reported as a finding against the model, never loosened.
- `tools/models/records_test.py`, `test_motions_pivots_and_footprints` gains: `hatch = MACHINES["pod_hatch"]`, then `(hatch.motion.kind, hatch.motion.axis, hatch.motion.blades, hatch.motion.amplitude, hatch.motion.period_seconds) == ("iris", "x", 14, 0.15, 0.15)`, `records.pivot(hatch) == (0.0, 0.0, 1.0)` and `records.hinge(hatch) == (0.0, 0.0, 1.78)`.
