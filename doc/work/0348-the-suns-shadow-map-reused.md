# 0348: The sun's shadow map reused

Status: implementing (2026-11-12)

The sun's shadow map is drawn every frame for every viewport: 0328 estimated 0.3 to 0.7 ms on the GPU and 0.4 to 1.2 ms on the CPU a viewport (`work/performance_research_0340.md`, 2.3). While the eye stands still nothing in it changes for many frames.

## Goal

The shadow pass costs nothing in the frames where its map would come out the same.

## Controls

None.

## Change

- The map is kept and redrawn when its snapped centre moved, the sun turned past a set angle, or a caster in its box changed (a frame, a felled tree, an edited chunk, a player, a moving part).
- Moving casters over a still map: a small pass of their own, or a redraw while one moves, as the design finds cheaper.

## Verify

- The checks and the suite, with a test per invalidation that the map is redrawn.
- 0342's table: the shadow pass in the still views and in split screen, before and after.
- Captures after a placement and after a felling showing no stale shadow, sent to the user.

## Specification (design, 2026-11-11)

Designed against `main` at `cfbad26` (0343 landed) and 0342's specification, whose marks and counters sit in the procedure this item rewrites. It runs in 0342's stream after it; its acceptance compares 0342's night table on `main` before and after.

### Decisions

1. **The seam: a kept map of the still casters per viewport, the moving casters drawn over a copy of it every frame.** The still casters go into a depth map of the viewport's own, redrawn by decision 2's rule. Every frame that map is copied into 0328's map, which the receivers read as today (its material slot, shaders and uniforms unchanged), and the moving casters are drawn over the copy. One row per draw call of today's `draw_field_sun_shadows`:

   | Draw call | What it reads | Pass | Change signal |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `draw_sun_shadow_terrain`: a `DrawMesh` per selected node with a terrain mesh in the box | the frame's selection; `Field_Renderer.meshes` (mesh, origin, `has_mesh`) | kept | `Field_Renderer.mesh_revision` (new: `apply_field_mesh`, `unload_field_node`) and the selection's hash (new); when either moved, the terrain fingerprint over the casting nodes and their `terrain_hash` (new: the terrain mesh's positions and indices, hashed on the worker) |
   | `select_shadow_trees`, `draw_tree_instances`: a `DrawMeshInstanced` per species model | the tree cache's tiles (keys, instances, boxes), the felled table, `tree_limit`, the models' extent, the species, the models | kept | `Field_Tree_Cache.revision` (new: a tile taken, evicted or cleared, a key dropped by a felling); when it moved, the tree fingerprint over the casting tiles and their `revision` (new); `Model_Renderer.revision` (new, moved by `unload_model_meshes`, so by every model and content reload); the row; whether trees cast |
   | `draw_frames`: `DrawCubeV`, `DrawCubeWiresV` per foundation cell | `Frame_Table` (frames, occupants), the occupants' machines | kept | `Frame_Table.revision` (exists: every occupied or vacated cell, every pose by `set_frame_pose`, the occupants cleared at a load); `Model_Renderer.revision` |
   | `draw_entities`, the still machines: every pool's entity but inserters, drills and launch pads, unless a hatch or its motion moves a part | the placement (origin, size, rotation, frame), the model | kept | `Frame_Table.revision` (a placement or removal occupies or vacates cells: `occupy_entity_cells`, `vacate_entity_cells`); `Model_Renderer.revision` |
   | `draw_entities`, the moving machines: inserters (arm, held item), drills (pump or bar), launch pads (rocket), hatches (door), any machine whose motion is `Pump`, `Spin`, `Swing`, `Bob`, `Slide` or `Iris` | the tick and alpha | every frame | none needed |
   | `draw_field_players` | the players as predicted | every frame | none needed |

   A still machine's depth is its model, its body matrix and its footprint cells: the part transform of `None` and `Glow` is the identity (`motion_transform`), working only colours, broken only tints. Not in the pass, so nothing to follow: belt runs and their items, loose and falling items, flames, ghosts, debris, the arrival's wake and flakes, cards, water, cover, globe.
2. **The reuse rule** (`sun_shadow_kept_step`). A kept map is kept when all hold: it was drawn, at the same row; the sun turned at most the turn limit (decision 4) from the sun it was drawn with; the eye lies within half a step of its centre along each of its three axes (decision 3); trees cast as then; the frame table's and the models' revisions are as then; and the mesh, selection and tree revisions are as then, or else (`Compare`) the terrain and tree fingerprints over its own box are, in which case the revisions are stored so the next frame skips the fingerprints. Otherwise `Turn` (never drawn, the row changed, the sun past the limit) redraws it on the frame's sun, and `Recentre` (the eye left its step, a still caster changed) on the sun and path axis it holds; both anchor at its old centre. The whole pass draws into the shared map every frame, as today, in three cases: while the pod's transform is set (the descent moves the frames, the machines and the players; the kept map is marked undrawn), in the planet preview (no slot), and for a viewport whose kept map did not load.
3. **The centre moves in steps from an anchor.** Today's snap does not hold while the sun turns: the eye sits about 8 km from the origin the snap counts from, so `dot(eye, right)` moves about 0.65 m (10 texels) a tick as the basis turns with the sun (7500 m off the path's axis times 8.73e-5 rad), and the map's texel grid lands at a new sub-texel phase on the world every tick, every edge re-rasterised (read in `sun_shadow_cascade`; a finding for the log). The kept map's centre is its anchor plus whole steps of `SUN_SHADOW_SNAP_TEXELS` (32) texels along each light axis, the sun's included; the anchor is its previous centre (the eye for its first draw). Between turns the axes are held, so a Recentre shifts the map by whole texels and no edge changes; a Turn rotates the grid about the old centre, within a metre of the eye. The margin grows by half a step so the receivers stay covered anywhere in the step: texel = 2 radius / (texels − 2 × 4 − 32), 6.37 cm on the desktop row (6.27 today) and 9.76 cm on Android (9.45); a step is 2.04 m (3.12 m). A walk at 5 m/s (0340's figure) recentres about 2.5 to 3.5 times a second instead of redrawing every frame. 16 texels would double the walking redraws for 0.8 % of texel; 64 would halve them for 3.2 % (6.5 % on Android).
4. **The turn limit and the step.** The sun turns 2π a day: 8.73e-5 rad a tick at the shipped 1200 s day (`day_fraction` reads the whole tick, so the sun moves once a tick and stops with `pause`). A map drawn under sun s0 and read under s puts an edge cast from l metres up the ray l·θ off in the light's plane; the receivers' edge is 3 texels soft. A caster h tall on flat ground under a sun e above the horizon casts its tip from l = h / sin e. The limit `SUN_SHADOW_STEP_TEXELS × texel × sin e / SUN_SHADOW_STEP_CASTER_METRES` (0.25 texel, 10 m) holds the tip of a 10 m caster's shadow to a quarter texel a step at every sun height, a twelfth of the edge's width: a 3 m machine moves under a tenth of a texel, a 20 m tree half a texel. The grid's turn about the anchor moves texel corners d·θ: 1.5 cm (0.23 texel) 10 m from the eye at noon. Today the grid re-phases by up to half a texel every tick (decision 3); a step is smaller, comes at most once every second tick (18 times less often at noon), and moves the edge the way the sun moves it, so it does not read as a hop and its cadence has nothing to repeat (DESIGN.md). Redraws: noon (70°, the tilted path's top) θ = 1.50e-3 rad, 17.2 ticks, so every 18th tick; 30° every 10th; 10° every 4th; 5° every 2nd; under 3.1° and down to the light's end (height −0.05) every tick. The 5 minute day steps four times as often. `time` jumps and walks across the planet (the sky turns with the viewer's up) pass the limit. Blending two maps was rejected: 32 reads instead of 16 in four receiving shaders, the larger part of the pass's GPU time by 0328's estimate, and the next map drawn ahead.
5. **Moving casters: a small pass of their own, and what it saves in the user's bar.** A redraw while anything moves in the box saves nothing in the bar: the viewer's own body is in every view's pass (`viewer_body_shown` is set, first person too) and turns with the view, a working factory's arms and drills move every frame, and in split screen the other player walks through the box. The kept map takes out of the frame, in 17 of 18 frames at noon and 3 of 4 under a 10° sun: the terrain's 20 to 50 draws (50 to 120 thousand triangles), the tree scan (up to 35 thousand instances in a forest) and the instanced trees (up to 615 thousand vertices), the foundation cells (immediate mode, transformed on the CPU, not culled), the pod with its fixtures (about 10 to 20 draws), the furnaces and the chests (0328's counts; not measured). Every frame keeps one depth copy (2048² at 4 bytes: 32 MB of traffic, about 0.07 ms at the RX 5700's 448 GB/s and 0.4 ms at the Deck's 88 GB/s, estimates) and the moving casters: an arm is up to about a dozen `DrawMesh` (five parts and the fingers, two layers), a drill, a spinning machine or a hatch up to 4, a player 12. With a few inserters the arms are most of what is left; cutting their draws is 0340's factory draw item, not this one.
6. **Split screen and the view distance.** A kept map per viewport (`Sun_Shadow_Renderer.kept`, by the viewport's index); the shared map stays one and is rebuilt per viewport by the copy before that viewport's moving casters and 3D pass. A viewport's map turns with its own sun (the sun follows its up) and recentres with its own eye. The other player's walk changes the shared selection, meshes and tree tiles, which asks for the fingerprints over this viewport's box; they change only when a node or a tile in that box changed. Its body is a moving caster. The view distance (0343) changes the selection and the trees' far row: the selection's hash moves and the fingerprints decide (packing moves level 1 into the box only at 256 m, 145 m against 160 m). A removed viewport's map is released between frames; a viewport that shifts into its slot finds a map holding no player and keeps, recentres or turns it by the rule. Memory: a kept map is a depth texture of `map_texels` a side, 16 MB at 2048 and 4 MB at 1024: one viewport 32 MB in all, two 48, four 80.
7. **GL: one new call, core in GL 3.0 and GLES 3.0.** The copy is `glBlitFramebuffer` of the depth buffer, nearest, between two depth only framebuffers of one size whose textures the same `rlLoadTextureDepth` call made (one format, which a depth blit requires on GLES 3.0), through rlgl's `rlBindFramebuffer` (the read and draw targets) and `rlBlitFramebuffer`; the depth mask on and the scissor test off first. `rlBindFramebuffer` is in the Linux and Android archives (`nm`) and the Windows library (`strings`) but not in the Odin binding: declared in `shared/raylib/platform.odin` (ours), outside its GLFW `when`; the nix job links nixpkgs' raylib, whose symbol CI shows. No shader, format or compare mode is new. Android's row is off by default; turned on, 4 MB a viewport more. Gladio (Winlator, the Windows build's GL 3.3) has never been given a framebuffer blit: unverified (question 1); a copy that silently did nothing would leave the moving casters' old shadows in the map, a trail behind a working arm.
8. **Rejected.** Receivers sampling a still and a moving map: 32 reads in four shaders. One map per viewport with dirty rectangles restored by redrawing the still casters under a scissor: no copy and no second map, but every moving caster needs an exact rectangle (an arm past its box's margin leaves a trail) and the still draw repeats per rectangle. Marking kept maps at each mesh upload: complete only while the streaming drops every unselected node in the frame it leaves; the fingerprint checks what the pass would draw. A shader copy (a quad writing `gl_FragDepth`): a shader pair and a quad for what one blit does. Keying the terrain on uploads alone: a pump's intake keeps waves going (0294) and every wave remesh would redraw the map; the hash keeps the terrain's identity when only the water moved. All machines in the moving pass: simpler, but the pod and the still machines would stay in every frame for no signal the frame table does not already give.

### Constants and types (`src/render_sun_shadows.odin`)

- `SUN_SHADOW_SNAP_TEXELS :: 32` (decision 3). `SUN_SHADOW_STEP_TEXELS :: 0.25` and `SUN_SHADOW_STEP_CASTER_METRES :: 10.0` (decision 4). `SUN_SHADOW_STEP_TOLERANCE_METRES :: 1e-4` (float noise in the step test). `SUN_SHADOW_KEPT_MAPS :: 4` and `SUN_SHADOW_NO_KEPT_MAP :: -1` (decision 6). `SUN_SHADOW_DEPTH_BUFFER_BIT :: 0x00000100` (`GL_DEPTH_BUFFER_BIT`, the blit's mask).
- `Sun_Shadow_Cascade.precise_centre: [3]f64`: the centre in f64, read by the step test and as the next anchor.
- `Sun_Shadow_Revisions :: struct {meshes, selection, trees, frames, models: u64}`.
- `Sun_Shadow_Kept_Key :: struct {cascade: Sun_Shadow_Cascade, toward_sun, path_axis: [3]f32, row: Sun_Shadows_Row, trees_drawn: bool, revisions: Sun_Shadow_Revisions, terrain_fingerprint, tree_fingerprint: u64}`: `toward_sun` and `path_axis` as the frame gave them, so a Recentre rebuilds the same axes bit for bit.
- `Sun_Shadow_Kept :: struct {state: Sun_Shadow_State, framebuffer, depth_texture: u32, drawn: bool, key: Sun_Shadow_Kept_Key}`; `Sun_Shadow_Renderer.kept: [SUN_SHADOW_KEPT_MAPS]Sun_Shadow_Kept`.
- `Sun_Shadow_View :: struct {eye, toward_sun, path_axis, up: [3]f32, row: Sun_Shadows_Row, trees_drawn: bool, revisions: Sun_Shadow_Revisions}`.
- `Sun_Shadow_Kept_Step :: enum u8 {Keep, Compare, Recentre, Turn}`.
- `Sun_Shadow_Part :: enum u8 {All, Still, Moving}`; `Sun_Shadow_Casters` gains `machines: Machine_Registry` and `part: Sun_Shadow_Part` (zero is `.All`, today's).

### Procedures

`src/render_sun_shadows.odin`:
- `sun_shadow_cascade :: proc(eye, toward_sun, path_axis: [3]f32, row: Sun_Shadows_Row, anchor: [3]f64 = {}) -> Sun_Shadow_Cascade`: texel `2R / (texels − 2 × SUN_SHADOW_MARGIN_TEXELS − SUN_SHADOW_SNAP_TEXELS)`, step `SUN_SHADOW_SNAP_TEXELS × texel`; centre = anchor plus, along right, up and toward_sun, `round(dot(eye − anchor, axis) / step) × step`, in f64; `precise_centre` set; the rest as today. The default anchor (the origin) is the whole pass's and the preview's.
- `sun_shadow_eye_in_step :: proc(cascade: Sun_Shadow_Cascade, eye: [3]f32) -> bool`: `|dot(eye − precise_centre, axis)| <= SNAP × texel / 2 + SUN_SHADOW_STEP_TOLERANCE_METRES` on the three axes, f64.
- `sun_shadow_turn_angle :: proc(held, current: [3]f32) -> f64`: `atan2(|a × b|, a · b)` of the two normalised in f64.
- `sun_shadow_turn_limit :: proc(toward_sun, up: [3]f32, texel_metres: f32) -> f64`: decision 4's formula, sin e the dot of the normalised sun and up, clamped at 0.
- `sun_shadow_kept_step :: proc(kept: Sun_Shadow_Kept, view: Sun_Shadow_View) -> Sun_Shadow_Kept_Step`: in order, `Turn` (not drawn, `row` differs, `sun_shadow_turn_angle(key.toward_sun, view.toward_sun) > sun_shadow_turn_limit(view.toward_sun, view.up, key.cascade.texel_metres)`); `Recentre` (not `sun_shadow_eye_in_step`, `trees_drawn`, `frames` or `models` differ); `Compare` (`meshes`, `selection` or `trees` differ); `Keep`.
- `sun_shadow_kept_cascade :: proc(kept: Sun_Shadow_Kept, view: Sun_Shadow_View, step: Sun_Shadow_Kept_Step) -> Sun_Shadow_Cascade`: anchor = `key.cascade.precise_centre` when drawn, else the eye in f64; `Recentre` on the key's `toward_sun` and `path_axis`, `Turn` on the view's; the view's row.
- `sun_shadow_fingerprint :: proc(words: ..u64) -> u64`: each word xored into a running value, then splitmix64's finalizer (`v ~= v >> 30; v *= 0xBF58476D1CE4E5B9; v ~= v >> 27; v *= 0x94D049BB133111EB; v ~= v >> 31`).
- `sun_shadow_selection_hash :: proc(selection: []Field_Node) -> u64`: `xxhash.XXH3_64_default(slice.to_bytes(selection))` (`core:hash/xxhash`; `Field_Node` is four `i32`, no padding).
- `sun_shadow_node_casts :: proc(renderer: ^Field_Renderer, cascade: Sun_Shadow_Cascade, node: Field_Node) -> (render: Field_Node_Render, casts: bool)`: the node's render when it has a terrain mesh whose box casts (the test `draw_sun_shadow_terrain` makes today, moved here).
- `sun_shadow_terrain_fingerprint :: proc(renderer: ^Field_Renderer, cascade: Sun_Shadow_Cascade, selection: []Field_Node) -> u64`: the wrapping sum over the casting nodes of `sun_shadow_fingerprint(u64(u32(level)), u64(u32(x)), u64(u32(y)), u64(u32(z)), render.terrain_hash)`; a sum, so the selection's order does not count (the depth does not either).
- `sun_shadow_tree_fingerprint :: proc(cache: ^Field_Tree_Cache, cascade: Sun_Shadow_Cascade, extent_metres: f32) -> u64`: the wrapping sum over the tiles `select_shadow_trees` scans (instances, the box widened by the extent casts) of `sun_shadow_fingerprint(x, y, z, tile.revision)`.
- `sun_shadow_entity_moves :: proc(kind: Entity_Kind, machine: Machine) -> bool`: true for `.Inserter`, `.Drill`, `.Launch_Pad`, a machine of kind `.Hatch`, and a `machine.motion.kind` other than `.None` and `.Glow`.
- `model_frame_draws_entity`: with casters whose part is not `.All`, an entity whose `sun_shadow_entity_moves(common.handle.kind, casters.machines.machines[common.machine])` differs from `part == .Moving` is left out, before the box test.
- `load_sun_shadow_target :: proc(texels: int) -> (framebuffer, depth_texture: u32, problem: string)`: `load_sun_shadow_map`'s framebuffer, depth texture, attach and completeness steps with its three problems, freeing what it made; `load_sun_shadow_map` calls it after its shaders and unloads the shader on a problem.
- `load_sun_shadow_kept :: proc(kept: ^Sun_Shadow_Kept, texels: int) -> bool`: the target, Ready; on a problem one line `error: a viewport's kept sun shadow map did not load (<problem>), its shadow is drawn whole every frame` and `state = .Failed`.
- `release_sun_shadow_kept :: proc(kept: ^Sun_Shadow_Kept)`: `rlgl.UnloadFramebuffer` when Ready (it deletes the attached depth texture, as for the shared map), then `kept^ = {}`. `release_sun_shadow_map` releases every kept map first.
- `serve_sun_shadows :: proc(shadows: ^Sun_Shadow_Renderer, receivers: []rl.Material, on: bool, texels, viewport_count: int)`: a stale map releases everything; otherwise the kept maps from `viewport_count` up are released.
- `usable_sun_shadow_kept :: proc(shadows: ^Sun_Shadow_Renderer, slot: int, moving_frame: bool) -> ^Sun_Shadow_Kept`: nil for a slot outside 0 to `SUN_SHADOW_KEPT_MAPS − 1`; with `moving_frame` the slot's `drawn` set false and nil; an Unloaded one loaded at `shadows.texels`; nil unless Ready.
- `begin_sun_shadow_target :: proc(framebuffer, depth_texture: u32, texels: int, cascade: Sun_Shadow_Cascade, clear: bool)`: `begin_sun_shadow_map`'s body, the clear only when asked; replaces it.
- `copy_sun_shadow_depth :: proc(source, destination: u32, texels: int)`: `rlgl.DrawRenderBatchActive()`, `rlgl.DisableScissorTest()`, `rlgl.EnableDepthMask()`, `rl.rlBindFramebuffer(rl.RL_READ_FRAMEBUFFER, source)`, `rl.rlBindFramebuffer(rl.RL_DRAW_FRAMEBUFFER, destination)`, `rlgl.BlitFramebuffer(0, 0, side, side, 0, 0, side, side, SUN_SHADOW_DEPTH_BUFFER_BIT)`, `rlgl.DisableFramebuffer()`.
- The header comment: the snap sentence rewritten (decision 3) and a paragraph on the kept maps (decisions 1, 2, 4).

### Tests (no GL context)
