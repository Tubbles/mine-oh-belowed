## Bodies that share a spill saddle (0334)

Tags: water, bodies, flood, lakes, 0334, 0312, 0300, 0336

From 0312's second review: lakes 0, 5 and 6 at 8 km never settled when streamed in. The design tried three narrower rules before the one that landed. A wall at the fringe partitions the basin the fringe lies in, which cut 0300's second cave into separate bodies. A take without the cell's volume share raised the owner's level until its own cells read as wholly under it. The seed alone as the search's target would walk a deep lake's whole water at every merge, so a sample wholly under the level ends the search too. The main agent added the cap and the memo: water thinner than a slab over a flat holds no sample wholly under its level, so the search would run to the seed, and a fringe of n samples met sample by sample would cost about n²/2 visits a call.

The review streamed every lake at 4, 8 and 16 km in both orders (120 runs) with counters in a scratch copy: the water summed exactly across every take, no remembered answer disagreed with a fresh search, a world saved and loaded every tick decided alike, and saves driven into the flip under the old rule settled 12 to 92 ticks after loading. It also showed where the memo's premise bends: a take holds the other body's level except across a flat stretch of its curve, where the level once rose 47 fill units (the comment says so now). Its two open ends are in `SUGGESTIONS.md`. The reversed 8 km lake 27 stays awake as on `main`, which is 0336's.

## raylib 6.2 (0319)

Tags: raylib, upgrade, android, windows, cursor, precision, pinning, 0319, 0315, 0236, 0126, 0133

raylib stays pinned at a tag while Odin and the NDK float (0315): the three committed libraries and the copied binding are built from one source, so a floating tag would change nothing until someone rebuilt them. The upgrade is an item each time, with the diff of `src/` between the tags read against what the game calls.

The design read that diff and found the three changes the game meets (the capsule's counts, the sphere's bands, `SetMousePosition`). The review found two more the table lacked, both in the window code and neither reachable from a headless check: on Windows the borderless toggle now goes through `glfwSetWindowMonitor`, and Borderless is the game's default mode, so every Windows start takes the new path; and a windowed `InitWindow` needs a primary monitor, so a start with none ends at the game's "could not open a window" message where 6.0 opened one.

The cursor fix is not reproduced headless: a command-line start under Xvfb reads yaw 27.5 and pitch 0 on the 6.0 and the 6.2 build alike, since no pointer moves there. The couch decides it.

The review compiled the narrowed precision patch's shader with Mesa's `glsl_compiler` and `glslangValidator`: both define `GL_FRAGMENT_PRECISION_HIGH` at `#version 300 es`, against the letter of the specification, so there the patch changes nothing. It stays for a driver that follows the letter; the phone's is not known.

The Android archive now holds NDK 30's native app glue. The leading fields of `android_app` and `ANativeActivity` the game declares are the same in 27.3 and 30.

## A flood passes a saddle under a generated lake (0336)

Tags: water, bodies, flood, lakes, generation, old saves, 0336, 0334, 0312, 0300, 0326

The item asked for lakes generated at or under their pour point, on the reading that lake 27 at 8 km stood 0.69 samples over it. The design measured every lake at 4, 8 and 16 km with a flood on the samples and found the generation sound: the main water stands at least 0.07 samples under its pour point, and what stands over is three small cases a few fill above their rims (pockets of 8 km lakes 4 and 5 by 13 and 3 fill, 4 km lake 4 at 500 mm by 10, a notch the 1 m lattice misses), which the weir's dead band and one hand-over to the front settle. The 0.69 was an inner saddle, a ridge under the lake's own flat water, so the change went to the flood and the generation's bytes stay.

The rule had to count woken water as well as still water: chunks arriving together wake each pocket's lowest sample, and with still water only lake 27 together took 288 to 1,802 ticks. Filling to the inner saddle instead would have parted 22 lakes into ponds 0312 never found, and joining the claimed water asleep (13 ticks instead of 41 for lake 27 reversed) would reopen what 0312's review rejected.

An old save keeps the same lakes. One that holds a lake settled into several bodies at rest (lake 27 streamed or reversed before this: 3) keeps them until a flood reaches the lake (a dig, a pour).

The review turned the rule off in a copy: both behavioural tests then fail as on `main`, so they test it. It also pumped a ridge lake under its ridge: the lake parted in two with the water conserved to the unit, so the saddle kept in `joined_sill` still works as 0300's does. Its two notes are in `SUGGESTIONS.md`.

## The relief bounded by boxes (0314)

Tags: generation, relief, cost, bound, determinism, 0314, 0306, 0337, 0326, 0327

The design's counts chose the seam (the item holds them and the rejected candidates): what grew with 0306 was the band of samples that need the relief, not only the evaluation, so the change decides samples instead of making a column cheaper, and it changes no byte, which spared a record flag and a second path kept forever.

The specification rested every noise range on the fades being monotone and named a test to prove it. The implementer wrote that test first and it failed: in integers `noise_fade` falls by at most 1 and `quintic_fade` by at most 3 between neighbouring fractions. The margin that replaced the assumption was the review's main subject. It computed the falls exhaustively, followed the margin through both callers of `fade_range` and the mesa's two ramps, and checked the landform curve's range against every value of the curve for 33 knot sets. Then it turned the bound off by a flag in a copy and compared the generation byte for byte: 593 chunks and 2,268 grids over the 4, 8 and 16 km planets, four seeds, three spacings, the crater baked and not, the carving sites and the lakes, at tier edges, gorge walls, crests, the sea level and the calm circle's edge, every array equal, and 9.0 million columns inside their box's range. Every product stays under 2.1e18 up to the 100 km radius limit.

No timing is in this landing: the bar (each shipped view within 1.3 times its pre-0306 cost) is read from `test_report_the_relief_cost` in the night window, and the couch's and the phone's start and walk are the user's. If the night's figures fall short, the design's next lever is a per worker cache of each noise's lattice corners.

## Waters over the whole planet (0326)

Tags: generation, waters, tiles, determinism, seams, cache, cost, 0326, 0312, 0336, 0327, 0337

The design made every rule local to a tile and its ring of neighbours (the catchment capped at 64 cells, the escape flood inside the window), which is what lets a tile be found in any order on any thread. The price is that a new world's springs and lakes are not 0312's, round the home either: the 8 km home's 3 by 3 tiles hold 20 lakes and 9 springs.

Two things in the specification did not survive the implementation. Its cache protocol (copy all keys once every one is found, evict freely) could send a reader round forever, since a final's batch of up to 26 analyses can exceed what a small cache keeps, so readers copy key by key and a publish never evicts its own key. And decision 4's premise, that the home's finals cost no more than 0312's finder, was wrong by 2 to 3.4 times in relief reads. That was accepted (decision 7) with the main thread's readers, which can now wait for a tile a worker is finding: the night's waters report times the start, and a stall felt in play becomes an item of its own.

The first review found lakes lost at the cube's seams, 7 of 62 border candidates at 8 km. The fix dropped the faces' ownership of cells altogether, because a basin's lowest owned cell is no pit when a lower neighbour lies over the seam, so no rule of ownership finds it. Springs were lost the same way (2 of 12). A second review of that round alone hashed 14 interior tiles at the three radii against the snapshot (equal), covered every border tile at 4 and 8 km (each of the 436 lakes an analysis finds has a kept lake over it, none twice) and asked two cube corners in opposite orders, also from two threads through a cache of 4 tiles. Its one finding moved the reach limit from a quarter of the radius to 21 percent: the face test by corners skipped tiles from 22 percent, on radii no shipped preset has. It also looked for the documented gap (a basin past both faces' tiles) and found none at 4 km: the 3 uncovered lakes in the ring past the faces' tiles are ones the owning grid rejects by its rules.

Not run: the relief map's planet net end to end. Its first whole run is the night's `--relief-map=planet,8000`, which goes to the user.

## Input injected through the command socket (0341)

Tags: input, command-socket, headless, captures, lockstep, 0341, 0183, 0342

The user's three sentences set the scope: a headless run reads no physical controller, no keyboard or mouse either, and the game gets a way to inject so xdotool goes. Until the landing the capture scripts kept every controller out with `SDL_GAMECONTROLLER_IGNORE_DEVICES_EXCEPT=0x0000/0x0000`; they now start with `--input=none`.

The seam is raw device state, not actions: a control stands behind every injected press, so the glyphs, the readers of raw input (typed text, the pointer) and a button that carries two actions behave as with a device, with no second path. The design wanted the keyboard's glyphs at the start, since that is the UI's default; the main agent turned that to the gamepad's, as the couch shows.

The review traced every device reader under `--input=none` and found none left. Its one real finding was not in the feature but beside it: a record's command lines run inside the tick on every machine, and `query ui` there read a UI that a tick's context does not have. A peer on the same build never sends such a line, so it took a malformed record, but it would have taken every machine in the session down at that tick. It also showed by state hash that `hold`, `tick 10`, `release` under a pause equals ten frames of holding, and one tick less does not.

The first capture taught the script something the specification had not said: the inventory does not open during the arrival's fall, about 2400 ticks, so a script ticks until `seat stand` answers ok.

## A view distance setting (0343)

Tags: presentation, settings, lod, view-distance, lockstep, performance, 0343, 0342, 0354

The user's bar for 60 frames a second is judged at a 512 m view, and the view became a setting to play with. The design's choice was how a shorter view uses the levels: packing them inside the view keeps the near ground as sharp as at 1024 m and halves the nodes at 512 m (about 49 against 96 on flat ground, an estimate, not a measurement), where the two simpler ways each lose one of those.

The implementer's own hash test meshed 32 nodes a session in six frames, which proved little, so the review was asked for long sessions instead: three per scenario (256 m, 1024 m, and the view changing every frame) with the real streaming every two or three ticks, digging, a torch, foundations, water bodies awake at the spring with two pumps, a tree felled over 366 ticks. Every tick's hash and the saved bytes were equal, and after fifty changes of the view the live meshes matched a session that never changed it.

It also found what the specification's "keeps the 2:1 balance" did not cover: the balance holds across faces, and the packed distances let nodes two levels apart touch along edges and corners. With the haze not fading shaded ground, that is 0354, written at the landing after the 256 m capture showed a far hilltop unfaded. The setting ships at 1024 m, where neither shows.
