# 2026-09-28 Tree species and felling

Tags: #generation #trees #felling #save #decision

## Work item 0059 implemented

- Six tree species in the new content file `data/trees.sjson` (oak, birch, pine, acacia, palm, dead tree), picked per tree by weight from each biome's `trees` list; a biome with trees and no list is refused. Crowns are round, conical, flat or none, trunks 4 to 16, roots on tall oaks and pines. Forest and plains get clearings from a new appended `Clearings` sub seed (wavelength 48), plains a higher density so its trees stand in groves.
- `LEAF_REACH` is 4 and `FEATURE_MAXIMUM_HEIGHT` 20, from the species limits (`MAXIMUM_CROWN_RADIUS`, `MAXIMUM_TRUNK_HEIGHT`) the trees file is validated against. `GENERATOR_VERSION` is 4, so saves from 0058 show "terrain changed".
- Felling: mining a log drops the logs above it as loose items; the leaves around the column are queued and decay after 1 to 4 seconds unless a log lies within 4 blocks (Manhattan). The queue is the second later table of `entities.bin`.
- Decision: a conical crown over roots starts at least 6 blocks above the root, not at a third of the trunk, so no needle lies within the support distance of a root and a felled pine leaves no crown hanging on its roots.
- Decision: trees keep 4 blocks off the landing pad, since the pad clears only 10 blocks above itself and trees now grow to 20.
- Decision: the sapling item is named in `data/trees.sjson` (`sapling_item`) and checked against the items at load, rather than named in code.
- Decision: the simulation reads the tree blocks from the session's generator (`simulation_tree_felling`), so `tick_world` takes them as a parameter (empty in tests without trees) and `src/loop.odin` passes them.

## Work item 0060 implemented: block textures and item icons

Tags: #rendering #textures #icons #ui #hot-reload #decision

- Block tiles come from `data/textures/blocks/<id>.png` with `_top`, `_side` and `_bottom` overrides, decoded with `core:image/png` (no raylib, so tests read the committed files); a missing, undecodable or wrongly sized file falls back to the colour tile, the last two with a log line. The atlas layout and the mesher are unchanged.
- Item icons: an item atlas with a tile per item (tile index = `Item_Id`), `Item_Icon_Kind.Item_Tile` and an `Item_Tile` draw command. `Item_Tile` is appended to both enums so a zeroed `Item_Icon` is still `Block_Tile` as before.
- Decision: `item_icon` keeps its signature, so the registry learns which items have a tile through `Item_Registry.icon_loaded`, a slice owned by the item atlas and reassigned whenever the atlas is rebuilt (start, content reload, texture change). Callers outside the icon code need no change.
- Decision: the item atlas reuses `Atlas_Layout` (`item_atlas_layout_for_item_count`) instead of a separate layout type, so the origin and size helpers serve both atlases.
- Decision: belt and loose item icons are drawn without depth writes, flushed around the batch: raylib's default shader does not discard transparent texels, so with depth writes a quad's clear corners would hide whatever is drawn behind them later. An icon may then show over an item drawn later behind it; acceptable at 0.4 blocks.
- Decision: an item's average colour counts texels with alpha 128 and up; it is kept with the atlas for the map, which does not use it yet.
- Items held by machines (`render_entities.odin`) stay cubes; that file was outside the item's scope.
- The Textures category's toast needed a new string, `reload_textures_done`, in `data/strings/en.sjson`.

## Work item 0061 implemented: block shapes

Tags: #blocks #meshing #collision #raycast #lighting #rendering #decision

- `Block_Definition.shape` (cube, slab, stairs, post, cross) with pure geometry in `src/block_shape.odin`: collision boxes, bounds, quads. `parse_blocks_file` expands oriented shapes, so every caller (the loader, the test registry) sees the variants right after their listed block, and `oriented_block` finds a variant by offset.
- Only solid cubes are opaque (`block_is_opaque`); the mesher's vertex light and occlusion now read opacity too, so a slab or stairs cell holds light and does not black out the cube face behind it. Cubes are unchanged.
- Decision: variants drop their listed block's item through the item's `mined_from` list in `data/items.sjson`, since item resolution lives in `src/item.odin`, which this item did not touch. A new slab or stairs block must list its variants there; a missing one fails loading with "can be mined but yields no item".
- Decision: variants are drawn with their listed block's tiles (`block_shape_base`), since the texture files are named by the listed id. The upper slab variant is `<id>_upper`, not `<id>_top`: `concrete_slab_top.png` is the listed slab's top face, and a variant id ending in a face group suffix would collide with it.
- Decision: a cube face against a slab or stairs is always drawn, also the part the shape covers (a stone top under a bottom slab); exact coverage checks were left out for simplicity.
- Decision: a post's target box is 8 texels wide (half a block, centred, the drawn height) while it is drawn 2 texels wide, so a torch can be aimed at with a gamepad; `shape_bounds` (the outline) is the target box.
- The player has no step up logic, so stairs are jumped like before; entities keep the whole cell rule.
- Pipes drawn thinner (core 0.3, stubs 0.22).
- Meshing time per chunk (test_report_generation_and_meshing_time, three runs each on the same machine): 21.6 to 27.4 ms before, 22.8 to 27.0 ms after.

## Work item 0082 implemented: ground cover per biome

Tags: #generation #biomes #blocks #placement #textures #decision

- Six cross blocks (`grass_tuft`, `tall_grass`, `flower_red`, `flower_yellow`, `dead_bush`, `reeds`), each with an item that places it back. `Biome_Definition.ground_cover` lists them per biome with a chance and the top blocks they stand on; `apply_ground_cover` runs after `clear_above_outcrops` and before the pad stamp. `GENERATOR_VERSION` is 5.
- Decision: the running sum runs over every entry in order, and the picked entry must stand on the column's top block, so an entry's chance is its share of the columns whatever the other entries stand on. Validation also refuses chances that add up to more than 1, which the running sum would silently cut.
- Decision: "dry land" is the cell above the surface being air after the features. Water fills that cell on flooded columns, so no separate height test is needed, and wetland columns at sea level get reeds.
- Decision: the top block is computed from the column (the biome's top block, or its pit block in a low spot), not read from the chunk, so the chunk that holds the cover cell decides alone when the surface is the top layer of the chunk below.
- Decision: the ray stops at cover (its bounds fill the cell), so a block or machine aimed at cover takes the cover's cell (`placement_target`), otherwise a machine aimed at covered ground would float one cell up. The replaced cover drops nothing.
- Decision: `commit_placement` has no block registry and the developer `place` command calls it too, so `placement_at` records `clears_cover` and `commit_placement` clears the non air cells of the footprint; a valid placement's footprint holds only air and cover.
- Follow up in the same item: the chunk shader discards texels with alpha below 0.5 (only the cover tiles have any, so every other tile draws as before); belts (placement, drag, the developer command) and pipes use `cell_takes_machine` and `placement_target` and clear the cover they replace; `mine_block` calls `drop_unsupported_cover` after the felling hook, which turns cover above the mined block to air and spills its item.
- Decision: `drag_target_column` gets the placement target, so a drag aimed at the side of a cover cell follows the cover's column.
- Not done: the bore drill ghost's vein line (`bore_drill_ghost_vein`) still reads the raw target, since passing the block registry changes its test in `deep_mining_test.odin` too. Only the column counts there, so it differs only when aiming at the side of a cover cell.

## Work item 0064 implemented: sky and atmosphere

Tags: #rendering #sky #fog #lighting #day-cycle #decision

- `render_day.odin` derives a `Day_Sky` (day fraction, day number, blend, `Sky_Colors`) from the tick and the day length; `sun_direction`, `moon_direction` and `sun_path_axis` give the tilted path. `render_sky.odin` draws the dome, the stars, the sun and the moon first inside the 3D mode.
- Decision: the sky renderer lives in `Chunk_Renderer.sky`, created and destroyed with it, so `Frame_State` in `loop.odin` needed no new field.
- Decision: the dawn and dusk colours peak with the sun on the horizon (sun height 0) and fade out 0.2 of sun height above and below (`TWILIGHT_GLOW_HALF_WIDTH`), so mid dusk is fraction 0.5 exactly. Dome vertex colours blend horizon to zenith by the sine of the elevation.
- Decision: the sun disc is its colour multiplied by the sun tint, so it turns orange at dawn and dusk. The moon's phase cycle is 8 days (`MOON_CYCLE_DAYS`); the day number is the tick over the day length, so the phase changes just after sunrise with the moon under the horizon. The shadow disc is skipped when it no longer overlaps (full moon).
- Decision: models take a colour tint now: `model_light_tint` and `emissive_brightness` return `[3]f32`, so an idle machine's emissive layer matches its lit layer at dusk.
- Fog distances for the current radius (6 chunks) stay 96 and 160 blocks.
- The dome covers the upper hemisphere only: below the horizon, where no chunk is drawn, the clear colour shows, so the main agent made the clear colour the horizon colour (the fog colour) rather than the zenith the item asked for; the world's edge then fades into the same colour whether seen at the horizon or from high up.

## Work item 0063 implemented: weather

Tags: #rendering #weather #fog #wind #shaders #settings #commands #decision

- `weather.odin` holds the pure schedule (`weather_at`), `render_weather.odin` what it looks like; the chunk shaders gain wind sway (vertex) and cloud shadows (fragment); a `weather` setting and a `weather <kind|auto>` command.
- Decision: the schedule reads the day ticks (`simulation_day_ticks`), the same clock as the sky, so a weather hour is a game hour of the visible day; the `time` command therefore also moves the weather.
- Decision: particles are fixed in the world and wrapped into a square column of 24 by 24 by 16 blocks around the camera, and only those inside the 12 block cylinder are drawn, so walking does not drag the rain along. At full rain about 470 of the 600 are drawn: that many `DrawLine3D` streaks (or snow billboards) in one batch per frame.
- Decision: the particle count also scales with the sky light at the camera cell, so no rain falls in caves or under roofs, and it fades under trees.
- Decision: overcast wind 0.5 and fog wind 0.1; fog casts no cloud shadows (strength 0 at full fog); overcast darkens the sky colours at 0.6 of rain's gloom. Weather at full intensity when forced by the command.
- Decision: the weather command sets `Session.weather_override` through a pointer in `Command_Context`, not a developer request, since it is render state and never reaches the simulation; `developer.odin` is unchanged.
- The cloud texture is bound as the material's second map (`MAP_METALNESS`, pointed at the `cloud_texture` sampler by `use_chunk_shader`), so `DrawMesh` binds it and `UnloadMaterial` frees it.
- Not done: leaves do not sway. The mesher knows a block only by its registry entry, and leaves are ordinary solid cubes there; the tree species name them in `trees.sjson`, which the mesher does not see. Marking them needs a data flag on the block (`blocks.sjson` and `Block_Definition` in `world_block.odin`), outside this item's files. Leaves are also greedy merged, so their quads would need cell by cell meshing to sway per block.

## Work item 0065 implemented: water rendering

Tags: #rendering #water #meshing #shaders #fog #decision

- The mesher routes water faces into `Chunk_Mesh_Data.water_parts` with a tangent per vertex (flow x, flow z, shore, 0); `render_water.odin` draws them in a transparent pass with the `water.vs` and `water.fs` pair, and closes the fog in under water.
- Decision: the flow sum and the four shore values are part of `Face_Key`, and the shore values count in `corners_uniform`, so a merged water quad carries one flow and one shore value. Open water still merges; cells at a shore stay single quads.
- Decision: the shore value reads the three cells around the vertex's vertical edge in the water cell's own layer (the fourth is the water cell), not the layer in front of the face that `vertex_light` reads: for the top face that layer lies above the water, where a bank level with the surface has no block. Side and bottom faces use the same rule, so their top vertices match the surface.
- Decision: the fragment shader turns the flow (world x and z) into the face's texcoords by the face normal from the derivatives of the world position, since the texcoords run along each face's own axes. The time uniform wraps every 600 seconds; an axis aligned flow and both ripples continue seamlessly, a diagonal flow jumps once per wrap.
- Decision: the water pass draws after the torch flames too (the item named the loose items and the weather particles as its neighbours), so flames behind water show through it. Foam lightens the colour but keeps the water's alpha; the water has no cloud shadows.
- Decision: under water is decided at the camera position of the frame, which in third person is the camera behind the player, so the view is fogged when the camera is in water.
- Decision: the game does not start when the water shader does not load, like the chunk shader; a hot reload of a broken pair keeps the old shader and the other pair still reloads.
- Not done: `take_mesh_results` (`world_streaming.odin`, outside this item's files) counts a mesh against the per frame upload limit only when it has opaque parts, so a chunk with water faces alone uploads without counting.

## Work item 0067 implemented: particles and feedback

Tags: #rendering #particles #feedback #decision

- `particles.odin` holds the pool (2048 slots, the next index wraps and replaces the oldest) and the per kind gravity, drag, launch speed, lifetime, size and growth; `render_particles.odin` the emitters, the last frame's memory (`Particle_Memory` in `Frame_State`, reset in `enter_session`) and the drawing.
- Decision: the steam engine emits in `Producing` and the combustion generator in `Generating`, the states `steam_engine_state` and `combustion_generator_state` set (the item said `Producing` for both).
- Decision: the flare stack flames in `Flaring`, not whenever `flare_stack_is_relieving` holds: an unpowered stack is relieving but burns nothing, and the relief check reads a buffer the tick has just drawn down, so it would flicker at the 90 percent line. `Flaring` is set only when it relieves with power, and it is what the stack's glowing top already reads.
- Decision: the launch exhaust starts under the rising rocket (`rocket_ascent`) rather than on the pad, so the flame follows it up; the smoke stays on the platform over a 3 block radius. Both rates are three times higher while the ascent is under a quarter.
- Decision: debris starts just off the face the player digs at (the target's face), so the cubes are not hidden inside the block. The break puff reads the local player only (`players[0]`).
- Decision: the descending capsule lands on the top of the drop capsule entity (the landing pad's capsule cell); the body is a cube with two cords drawn with depth writes, the parachute a disc billboard in the particle pass.
- Decision: smoke, steam, puffs and debris are darkened by the sky light like the rain; sparks, flames and exhaust glow and are not. `disc_pixels` and `upload_disc_texture` (`render_sky.odin`) take the size and edge width, so the particles share the generator with a 16 texel disc and a 3 texel edge.

## Work item 0066 implemented: the player has hands

Tags: #rendering #player #animation #models #settings #decision

- `player_animation.odin` holds the pure phases (walk from the walked distance, the chop, the place swing, the head bob, the step detector) and `Player_Animation_Memory`; `render_player_model.odin` loads the six limb files, reads the pivots from the voxel bounds and builds the transforms; `render_player.odin` draws the body, the first person arm and held item, and spawns the footstep dust.
- Decision: every limb file is the whole 10 by 29 by 10 frame with only its limb filled, so the six stand in place at one transform and no offsets are kept per limb. The pivots come from each limb's voxel bounds (an arm one voxel under its top, a leg at its top, the head at its bottom), so a replaced model moves its joints with it. The limbs mesh with the footprint set to the voxel size and scale to blocks in the transform, since `mesh_voxel_model` takes a footprint in whole blocks.
- Decision: whether the player walks is decided when a new tick arrived, not per frame (the item said since the last frame): on a display faster than 60 Hz every other frame has no new tick, and the walk would stop on those frames.
- Decision: the walked distance and the placed counters are the world's statistics, summed over players; with one player that is the local player. The walk phase moves per tick, not interpolated.
- Decision: mining raises the third person right arm to 90 degrees and chops 50 degrees down and back; the first person arm is held 80 degrees forward and follows half of the chop and place swings, so the hand stays mostly in view. The place swing is 40 degrees. The third person head follows the look up to 60 degrees.
- Decision: the head bob is two sines per walk cycle, one per step. The first person arm and held item are drawn only with the player model loaded, like the capsule stood in for the body; an item without an icon is a small cube in its colour. The Settings panel's row count stays as it is, like the Weather toggle: the Display tab scrolls.
- `tools/make_placeholder_models.py` takes an optional voxel size for the player frame; running it rewrote no existing model file.

## Work item 0068 implemented: sound, sparse

Tags: #audio #sound #settings #data #decision

- `audio.odin` holds the sound table, the pure mixer state (the gap rule, the loop volume approach) and the raylib half (device, loading, playing); `sound_events.odin` the selection, the detectors, `Sound_Memory` in `Frame_State` and the frame's triggers. 28 placeholder files from `tools/make_placeholder_sounds.py`, 1.8 MB.
- Decision: effects scale with the effects volume and loops with the ambience volume, so the machine hum, being a loop, follows the ambience slider. The table has no channel field.
- Decision: the three volumes are clamped to 0 to 1 where the mixer reads them; the configuration does not range check them, since `configuration.odin` was outside the item's files.
- Decision: the Audio tab comes second, after Display. `SETTINGS_ROW_COUNT` stays: the Audio tab's three rows are fewer than the Display tab's.
- Decision: a third UI sound, `ui_back`, plays on Back over a screen other than the title. The move and confirm events are recorded in `ui_resolve` (`ui_core.odin`) from the same conditions `ui_interact` activates on, since `ui_widgets.odin` was outside the item's files; the fallback focus of a newly opened screen is not a move.
- Decision: the step detector keeps its own distance in `Sound_Memory` and calls `footstep_due` like the animation, instead of reading the animation's step flag, which is local to `render_player.odin` (outside the item's files). Wading takes the `sound_material` of the water block at the feet, so no material name is written in code.
- Decision: mining hits play when the dig crosses a quarter into 1, 2 or 3; the fourth quarter is the break, which the `blocks_mined` counter already announces.
- Decision: the landing thud plays when the particles' descent was active last frame and is not now. Snow is silent; the rain loop plays at the intensity times the open sky at the eye, like the rain particles' count.
- Decision: the footsteps and the ambience are checked against the sound table when the mixer loads the table (at start and on a sounds reload), not when the blocks load, since `data_load.odin` was outside the item's files. A table that fails leaves the game silent at start, or keeps the old sounds on a reload. A content reload does not check them again: an unlisted id plays nothing.
- Decision: the hum family comes from the machine data: a fluid machine hums as fluid, another machine with an electric draw as electric, the rest as burner. The distance is measured from the player's eye, also in third person.
- Decision: biome ambience: wind over the mountains, cold barrens, highland, badlands, desert and coastal dunes; birds in the hills, forest and plains; insects on the steppe and in the wetland; water at the lake and the beach; the tar flats have none.
