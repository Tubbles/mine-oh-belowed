## 0370: water leaves at the loaded set's edge

Tags: water, performance, sixty-fps, teleport, bodies, lakes, lockstep, save, review, 0370, 0367

The design made the edge a sink for everything, bodies included, and the implementation showed what that does to a generated lake at a set's edge: a pocket holding part of the lake's own shore split off as a body, water left by arrival order, and a film appeared from water one body's level wrote and then released. The implementer stopped twice rather than adapt the lake's test, and the main agent changed the rule: a body seeded in a generated lake's mask keeps the edge as a wall, and only other bodies and moving water leave. The item's target was the pool moving water builds, and a lake at a set's edge is a question of what is loaded, not of water.

What the rule leaves, found by the review under bodies and kept below the bar: the lake's own front water still crosses the edge once while its chunks arrive, and a lake can end as a few bodies by arrival order (`SUGGESTIONS.md`). The review's first question was whether two machines can have different loaded sets, since the sink would then make their water differ; they cannot, the set coming from the players' feet alone.

Two save faults older than this item surfaced because its tests hash at the load: an ended flood's stale fields, and a size check on a body table from before 0300.
