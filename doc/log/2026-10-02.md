# 2026-10-02

## The vision: one star system of smooth voxel planets

Tags: vision, design, world, sphere, voxels, water, light, building, belts, arms, survival, space, rockets, station, multiplayer, look, time, rebuild

The user spent the morning writing ideas into `TODO.md` (Bigger picture) and asked for them to be rewritten, researched and fleshed out in a back and forth, with no implementation. The draft lived in `work/vision-2026-10-02.md` (untracked, twelve drafts) with three research reports under `work/research/` (eight inspiration games, asset workflows, voxel planets). Forty decisions came out of it and were merged into `DESIGN.md` and `PLAN.md` the same day. The decisions, grouped, with the user's as "user" and the agent's proposals the user accepted as "accepted":

World:

- The planet is a true sphere with gravity at the centre and every entity's up following it; gravity and shape are constant however much is mined (user). Planet to space and back is always a rocket, not steered: up to low orbit, down by a return pod to a spot picked on the map, no orbital mechanics (user).
- The world is smooth voxels, a density field with a material per sample meshed into Astroneer's soft sculpted surface (user, after the research showed no quad grid keeps square cells on a sphere: an axis aligned grid gives a staircase everywhere but the six axis points, a cube sphere leaves 42 to 75 percent of each face more than 10 percent off square with rows bending 31 to 37 degrees across an edge, latitude bands put a seam around the planet; `work/research/voxel-planets-2026-10-02.md`). Cubes were dropped; the earlier idea of tapered blocks ("healing faces") was dropped with them.
- The sample spacing is a world setting between a third of a metre and a metre; performance is not a worry yet (user). The material unit stays a cubic metre per item.
- Bedrock depth is a planet value in data, 256 m the starting guess (user).
- Water is a second field with a fill fraction per sample, a cellular rule towards the centre, meshed by the same mesher (accepted, over static basin levels and a Timberborn sheet that cannot do cave water). Volume is conserved, with the sea, springs and rain as infinite sources (accepted, over Minecraft's infinite source cells with a limited run). Precedents: Astroneer has no liquids at all, Timberborn's depth sheet on a tile grid, From Dust's fluids on a smooth heightmap.
- Light propagates so lamps matter; sources are generous since the torch's 5 block feel is cramped (user); the falloff is gentle near the source and steep at the edge, values in data (accepted).
- Tint: natural materials carry the tint of their place, tints never stack, every recipe takes all tints as one (user); filters carry an "accept all tints" toggle, on by default (user).
- One day length for every planet and one shared clock; when all players sleep, eight hours pass (user). Long nights as a role trait dropped.
- Every location carries at least one material found nowhere else, moons and belts included (user). Planet roles (home, first hop, ore world, gas source, second living world, deep world, ice world, belts) as the agent listed them (accepted); "two biome families" means two groups of related biomes on the home world.

Building:

- Foundation frames: a foundation defines a local frame, up along gravity and any yaw, and everything snaps to it; belts and pipes may run free between islands (user). A frame never re-tangents (user).
- Grid pitch 0.5 m, arm reach 2 m, both in data (user).
- Inserters are big industrial arms with an elbow and a folded resting pose, the look Factorio's inserter reaches for; the current model reads as a miniature crane and goes (user).
- Free belts work as Satisfactory's: poles placed freely, the curve between two poles derived by the game from positions and facings, never drawn (user, after a table of four ways: grid only, straight segments, derived curves, hand drawn splines).
- Machines are meshes at real sizes on frames; the idea of machines as collections of blocks fell with the blocks.

Player and survival:

- Three modes: survival (health, hunger, oxygen, suit battery, death allowed), peaceful (no stats at all, not even oxygen), creative (user).
- Slopes slow and slide past a walkable angle of about 40 degrees in data, a step of one sample, a jump of a metre with a mantle to one and a half, a tool reach of a few metres (accepted).
- Oxygen follows Planet Crafter: the starter planet is not breathable, oxygen is a stat from the first minute, generators run electrolysis, the pod brings an infinite water reserve, panels and batteries for the night, and a bed (user). No oxygen tethers (user). Terraforming lowers consumption until the atmosphere is breathable, a branch and not the main loop, breathable late, the curve from playtesting (user). Sealed rooms and greenhouse domes are breathable early with a simple farming mechanic (user).
- Death is never gory: a tinted screen with a word like "perished", immediate respawn at home; a respawn action in the menu so nobody is stuck; keep inventory on death is a per save setting; a wreck or a death site keeps the inventories for a salvage trip that recovers everything (user).

Space:

- Low orbit is empty on arrival; the first capsule is the station's seed (user). Return pods are built and fuelled (user).
- The station's four stages, capsule, outpost, dock (the cage sets the ship size, the small ship is the gate out of home orbit), yard (plates made in orbit from belt ore), and mass as the currency of orbit (accepted, "I love the station draft and the weight rocket lift idea"). The station is built by hand from floors and walls on the frame grid, airtight when enclosed (user); mag boots hold the player to any surface and switch off to float; the jet pack stops the player smoothly when no input is given; loose items float (user). "One up" for all modules dropped.
- Rockets follow the rocket equation: a cost to orbit per body and an exhaust speed per fuel give tonnes of fuel per tonne lifted (user: "take inspiration from the rocket formula"). Tiers as proposed: solid fuel, rocket fuel, hydrogen, staging with the dock, a transit drive apart from lift (accepted).
- Ships are models with walkable interiors; the inside is a foundation frame (accepted, revised from "the inside is blocks").
- Free flight is pinned; every parking place is a playable scene with the ship still and the player on EVA, a belt stop holding several asteroids (user).
- Comms work as Kerbal's network: relays with a maximum range, a handful per planet, ground stations; not simulated in real time (user). Coverage gates remote information only (accepted).
- The player's own machines break seldom and need a wrench trip; repair drones later; derelicts are rare; no port puzzle (user).
- The main quest line ends after the first third; contracts carry the rest (user). The survey satellite replaces the purchased survey (user). Fauna stays scenery (user).

Multiplayer:

- About 32 concurrent players as a guess, no cap; up to four in local split screen mixed freely with remote players (user). Both a dedicated server and a player's machine host; one home, everyone starts at the pod, new players spawn there (user). The phone joins as a remote player (user). Permissions are a host role (user).
- The model is Factorio's lockstep on the deterministic tick, split screen as viewports over one simulation (agent, recorded as the design).

Look and time:

- Everything built stays industrial and Factorio like; the soft terrain is the only thing that changes; no toy look (user).
- Weather per planet, one texture and at most one mechanic per kind, never damage (accepted).

The rebuild:

- The first playable slice is built on `main`, no branch, and includes multiplayer from the start, "bolting it on later is a worse proposition" (user). No compatibility path, saves break as needed (user).
- Down the line: a signal network like Factorio's circuit network shared between planets over comms, and a processor programmed in a Scratch like block language (user).
- The engine cut of 0146 reads as the agent wrote it: the engine owns the fields, their edits, light, meshing, collision, frames and curves; the plugin never sees a block grid (user: "your call").

## What it does to the plan

Tags: plan, milestones, architecture, plugins

The block world is retired and its content carries over; `PLAN.md` gained the rebuild, a new alpha 1 definition (phases 1 to 7 on the smooth world with multiplayer) and milestones M13 to M20. Architecture questions that survive the decisions stay high priority: the queued writes (0166) become mandatory for lockstep and move into M13 with the per tick event list; the occupant index (0164) and the arrival list (0165) are re-read against the field world. The plugin system of 0146 goes on the back burner (user: "the plugin system can be put on the back burner, and we could have it in mind as we go but I want to focus on these new things now"); its cut remains the guide for where code goes. The technical brief for the smooth world engine with multiplayer is `doc/work/0167-smooth-world-engine-brief.md`, written in the shape of 0146 and fleshed out with the user before any item implements it.
