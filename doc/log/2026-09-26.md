## Decisions, later the same day

- License: AGPL-3.0-only for now.
- Camera: first person by default, third person as a toggle.
- The simulation is designed for several players from the start.
- Water flows (Minecraft style). Depth is unbounded. Hand mining is fast. Machine footprints may be odd shapes, they are part of the puzzle.
- Direction: the factory is the game. Surface first, at Factorio scale (veins yielding tens of thousands of units), with underground play at most a tenth of the time. The depth-as-progression idea from earlier the same day is dropped, including drills that carve or deplete blocks. The design revision follows the genre research in `work/genre-research.md`.
- Veins are finite or infinite as a world setting (difficulty option), not a fixed rule.
- Adopted for the revision: foundations and platforms are placed blocks, dug terrain feeds the factory (sand, clay, gravel), byproducts come back as building blocks, veins are reservoirs with a surface outcrop that any drill on it taps, the underground is optional content (caves with rare deposits) plus bore drills that reach deep veins from the surface, recipe depth lives in data.
- Core content, not optional extras: pipes for liquids and gases, pumps and tanks, a plastics chain, byproduct handling and recycling.
- No "kid mode". Peaceful means no violence. Nothing else is simplified.
- Power is alpha content and part of the logistics puzzle: fuel logistics for boilers, pole coverage, separate networks, brownouts. Rails and minecarts are post alpha.
- No mandatory building. Rockets ship goods for trade and for quest completion. The rocket program is post alpha content, but its framing (a colony supplying an orbital station that sends requests and rewards) is the quest narrative from the start.
- A data driven quest system guides the player through the whole game and replaces a tutorial. Quests guide and reward, the alpha decides whether they also gate.
- The early game is deliberately manual (hand fuelled machines, carrying ore, hand crafting) so that automation arrives as relief, not as a lecture.

## Decisions after the genre research

Research report: `work/genre-research.md` (untracked). Distillate with sources: `doc/inspiration.md`.

- No teleporting logistics, ever: no logistic bots, no storage network with autocrafting. Belts, ramps, lifts, inserters, pipes, later rails.
- Vein model: reservoirs with a surface outcrop, Factory0-Resources style, mixed ores plus spoils, sized on the Manufactio scale. Finite by default with a late game revival route at a cost, infinite as a world setting.
- Quests are chapters over eight gameplay phases. Alpha 1 is phases 1 to 4. Post alpha milestones M6 to M9 are phases 5 to 8.
- Built in from the start instead of leaning on mods: recipe graph browser, rate readout per machine, bottleneck overlay, production statistics, distribute gesture, selection assist.
- Day one engine requirements: recipes with any number of inputs and outputs mixing items and fluids, fluid phases, vein entities, statistics counters, quest runtime.
- Pace rule: depth comes from byproducts and alternative routes, not from recipe step count.
- Pillar "Down is forward" replaced by "The factory is the game" and "Depth of recipes, not depth of tunnels".
