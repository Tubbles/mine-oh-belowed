## Water bodies on the field (0293)

Tags: field, water, bodies, flood, codec, save, tech-demo, 0293

The tech demo's second water model, the browser demo `bodies-waves` carried into the field: still water as bodies with an integer volume and a level, the cellular rule only on the wet front. The implementation left the specification in seven places, each weighed sound by the review. Front water landing in a body's dry air stays awake and is absorbed later, because owned samples that slept and took every inflow left no stream in the trough and broke the seeding step. Placed ground keeps a lake's volume and raises its level, as displacement does; the specification subtracted it. A pending flood is saved whole (its heap, pushed bits and record) rather than replayed, since a replay reads other bodies' levels at save time instead of at each visit; it costs about 4 KiB per touched chunk in the hashed entities file. The level curve's base lowers instead of folding low cells into band 0, so a cave fills to the right level. Frozen parts carry an owner tag, so the thaw finds a merged body's samples. The review reproduced two spring bugs: a sender planned before a spring promised water against its tick-start fill, so the byte wrapped past full (springs now refill in a pre-pass), and a spring under a lake added its rate plus its own byte every tick (settle skips springs, the rate goes through the inflow). A pool seeded against a lake's side stayed a second body at its own level; a seeding flood now merges when its sill reaches the other body's level. The reader bounds the base, level, band index (four times one flood's span, so a merged body's lower base fits), owner and every list count.
