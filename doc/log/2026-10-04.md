## The placement editor and the tools radial (0215)

Tags: placement, editor, input, bindings, touch, hud, lockstep, m14

- The centre rule (folding 0213): every footprint Place puts on the field is centred on the aimed cell, offset `(side - 1) / 2` cells below it on each horizontal axis, as `foundation_block_first_offset` already centres a foundation block, so an even side's centre corner is the aimed cell's high corner and a 1 by 1 or 2 by 2 footprint keeps its origin. The aim is a cell, not a point: choosing the corner nearest the hit point would make the outline jump between four positions while the reticle moves inside one cell. The height is never offset. The block world keeps its corner placement.
- A machine on bare ground is centred on cell (0, 0, 0) of its new frame, and its origin is the simulation's own (`field_footprint_origin({}, ...)`), never a cell taken from a command; the frame keeps standing on its hit, so a nudge on bare ground re-stands the frame on the ground under the moved centre.
- The commit is a `Machine_Placement_Command` (the machine, the rotation, the frame and origin, or the new frame's ground point and heading) rather than input in the record: the editor's state is presentation per viewport, never saved, hashed or sent, so the commit carries its result and the simulation sees one command as Place's queued placement. It is queued into the field's placements at the tick's start and drained with them, so the drain checks it like any placement. No save layout changes (the command list is not saved); its network tag is appended last, so no older tag moves.
- Pipette has no effect anywhere today; the tools radial routes one Pipette press to the world on the release of a tap, so the pipette item (0216) finds it there. Once the radial has shown, its dead centre selects nothing.
- Touch: the editor's controls are HUD touch buttons pressing the gamepad controls of their actions, not a placement layout file, so they follow rebinding and every user layout. While the editor is anchored the free screen's taps and holds press nothing, since a stray tap would commit and a resting thumb cancel a 5 m machine. On narrow screens, where the touch row wraps into a stack, the grid stands left of that stack above the hotbar.
- The threshold is data: `direct_placement_limit` in `data/game.sjson`, 2 by 2 by 3 shipped.

Tags: input, bindings, keyboard

## The world's hints, the raised outline and a 6 m reach (0219)

Tags: hud, glyphs, placement, field

- The world's glyph bar names the held item's controls only in a field session (`Hud_Context.field_session`): in a block session the field player's tool is zero (Material) and would show a false Place, and Pipette does nothing outside the field's tools radial. Place shows for every tool but the hand, Tools (Pipette's binding, Q and D-pad Up) for any item, then Turn for a machine (Next_Brush turns only a machine) or Brush for a material or the hand, where the same control cycles the brush. No binding changes.
- The Open hint of a machine with a panel counts as an aimed hint, and the trailing Inventory is then left out, being the same binding. Elsewhere the trailing Inventory and Pause are what the bar sheds first, the opposite of the menus' bar, which keeps Back: both are learnt in the first minute.
- The bar stands beside the hotbar when its kept hints fit there in two rows, else above it. The room beside the hotbar, measured in UI units, runs from 1346 (1920 by 540) down to 136 (1280 by 800 at scale 1.5), where one keyboard hint ("Right mouse" Place at the largest text, 289) is twice the room, so the item's "the editor's four stay whole beside the hotbar" could not hold. Above the hotbar the bar is as wide as from the hotbar's left edge to the safe right edge and never rises into the target lines' band (main agent, at the approval): the band ends at H/2 + 194 units and the bar's first row tops at 0.95H - 216 on a screen H units tall, so wherever H is under 911 (UI scale 1.2 and 1.5, whatever the pixels) the world's bar keeps one row and that row already lies in the band. The placement editor's bar is the exception (main agent): it takes two rows above the hotbar even into the band, since a missing Cancel is worse than the tool line crossing the bar. Both are accepted as a limit of the large UI scales (main agent, after the review); lifting the target lines or a HUD layout for the large scales is left to the user (SUGGESTIONS.md). The bar moving with what is held at the largest scales is accepted.
- The footprint outline's walls are a cell tall (500 mm at the shipped pitch). A bare placement's corners read within 250 mm of its centre, where the outline's floor stands, so at least 260 mm of wall and the whole rim show at the worst corner: the margin is half the wall. A placement refused Too steep can bury a wall, but its outline is red.
- `tool_reach_millimetres` is 6000. The field aim's quarter sample raycast takes 24 steps instead of 16 at 1 m spacing and 72 instead of 48 at 333 mm, the frame raycast 12 cells instead of 8 at the 500 mm pitch, in the tick and the prediction.
