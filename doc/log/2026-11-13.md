## 0349: the globe after the ground, a node culled by its meshes

Tags: performance, presentation, culling, shadows, review, 0349, 0348

The specification of 2026-11-11 was brought up to `main` before the implementation, since eight render items had landed over it. The one design change: two boxes a node, not one. The view's test reads a box round everything the node draws, the sun's casters a box round the terrain alone, because 0348's kept shadow map is keyed on the terrain's mesh: with one joined box a wave or a cover remesh could flip a caster at the edge of the casters' box and redraw the map for nothing, and the spring remeshes its water about every other frame.

One change of the picture, found by the review and accepted: the shadow pass pins a caster nearer the sun than its near plane onto that plane, so terrain past `caster_reach_metres` still cast whenever its widened cube reached into the band. With the terrain's own box that node is dropped. The old reach was longer by the cube's empty space along the sun, about 5 to 16 m and up to 35 m more under a sun 15 degrees up. The new reach is the documented one; a ridge shadow missing at dusk is answered by raising the reach in the data.

Captures of the profile's six views at 1024 m, at noon and at dusk, on both builds: eleven equal pixel for pixel, the sea at noon 4 pixels apart. No table yet: the before and after run is the next night window's, the before from the commit under this one.

The probe's node counts in the specification predate the caves and were not taken again.
