# HUD

What the world view draws over the world (`hud.odin`, through the UI draw list under any open screen). Widget and screen rules are in [ui.md](ui.md); the touch buttons' input is in [touch_overlay.md](touch_overlay.md).

- The toolbars sit bottom centre, the quest objective top right, toasts and Mission Control top left, notices top centre, the glyph bar bottom right. There is no compass strip.
- Under a screen `draw_hud` draws only the crosshair or the mining ring, the toolbars and the craft queue; everything else waits for the world.

It offers Sprint while the player walks on the ground without sprinting (`sprint_hint_shown`), and "Pick up" with the Mine glyph while the field player aims at a frame cell other than the pod's (`field_pick_up_hint_shown`, 0195), "Broken down, tear it down" instead on a broken machine (`field_target_is_broken`, 0201). Beside a machine with a panel the Inventory glyph says "Open", since that binding opens it (`inventory_hint_key`, 0194); a power switch adds the Interact glyph with "Turn", a full schematic crate "Take". Where the aimed thing takes Interact and Interact's glyph is the Inventory's (the gamepad's X, 0233) the bar shows that hint alone, with no Open or Inventory, since the press is Interact's there (`hud_target_takes_interact`); with the keyboard F and E both show. The pod's hatch offers nothing (0231: its doors follow the players). The pod's chair (0223) and its charging cradle (0358, `field_pod_seat_hint_key`) come first with the Interact glyph: "Unbuckle" strapped in after touchdown, "Stand" seated, "Stop waiting" cradled, "Sit" standing aimed at the chair, "Wait" standing aimed at the cradle, nothing while the world falls; while strapped in, seated or cradled the held hints do not show. While the placement editor is anchored (0215) it shows Move (the nudge, D-pad Up's glyph), Turn (Rotate), Place (commit) and Cancel (Mine), and nothing else (`placement_editor_glyph_hints`), kept whole at every size |

- **Touch buttons** (`hud_touch_button_rectangles`): backpack, map, pause, tools (the pickaxe, the tools radial, 0215), then rotate, a slot's size each on the hotbar's baseline, so rotate coming and going moves no other; rows going up where the room is narrower (1280 by 800 at UI scale 1.5). Rotate shows while a machine or stairs is selected (turned before placing) or a belt, splitter, inserter or drill is targeted (turned in place). While the placement editor is anchored tools goes (Pipette is unavailable there) and the editor's buttons show as a 3 by 3 grid of slots above the row, its middle column two steps left of the safe area's right edge: commit (a check), nudge away and cancel (a cross) on top, as L2 and R2 sit on the pad, nudge left and right in the middle, nudge towards at the bottom; where the row's stack leaves no room above it the grid stands left of the row's column, above the hotbar.
- **Crafting queue**: one box per run, newest nearest the hotbar, up to 4 rows going up; when the runs outnumber the boxes the front run and the newest show. Each box has the product's icon and the run's crafts as its count; a progress bar over the front run, and above it "Crafting waits: inventory full" while the finished craft's outputs do not fit, or "Waiting for Log" while the front craft lacks an ingredient.

## Toolbars

Toolbars of eight shortcuts over the inventory's 45 slots (0278, `toolbar.odin`, after Techtonica). A slot names an item kind (`Player.toolbars`), never a stack; the selected slot of the active toolbar (`active_toolbar`, `selected_hotbar_slot`) holds the kind's first stack in slot order (`selected_stack_index`), wherever it lies, and Place, Use_Item, Drop_Stack, the hand and the field tool all take that one stack. An absent kind stays named and holds nothing: the hand is empty and the tool the hand's until the kind arrives again. A kind may stand on several slots.

- `toolbars` in `data/game.sjson` sets how many (1 to 4, [content.md](content.md)); one ships.
- A slot draws the kind's icon and the inventory's count of it over every stack above 1, fitted (`draw_toolbar_slot`); an absent kind its icon under `HUD_EMPTY_KIND_SHADE`; a slot naming nothing only its frame.
- The rows (`draw_hud_toolbars`): the active toolbar lowest as the hotbar, the next ones above it in order, wrapping (`toolbar_at_row`), each a gap above the last, of plain slots from the hotbar's left edge under `HUD_INACTIVE_ROW_SHADE`. The selected outline is row 0's. The selected kind's name stands a gap above the stack, dim while the inventory lacks it.
- The radial, the slot keys, L1 and R1, the wheel and the touch taps select on the active toolbar ([input.md](input.md)); a higher row is no touch target.
- Y in the crafting view names a kind on the selected slot ([ui.md](ui.md), Crafting view); Y held there opens the toolbar edit mode, which moves, swaps and clears shortcuts on every toolbar, and whose carousel past its lowest row sets the active toolbar (`Active_Toolbar_Command`, 0279, [ui.md](ui.md), Toolbar edit). The starter kit, the tech demo's kit, the chapter kits and `give` name what they give on the first empty slots in order, and a tool or machine that arrives by a pick up or a hand craft names itself on the first empty slot unless some toolbar names it already ([logistics.md](logistics.md), Routing).

## Targeting

- Target lines under the crosshair (0052), stacked without gaps: the block's name dim, or an entity's name and state; the pickaxe the block needs (0051); the vein line. Nothing when the ray hits nothing.
- On the field, with a machine other than a foundation held over bare ground (`field_tool_line`, `bare_ground_line`, 0201), the tool line says "On bare ground, wears out in 60 min" (the minutes that apply to the held machine, [content.md](content.md), Machines on bare ground) where the ground under the footprint is flat enough, and "Too steep, needs a foundation" where it is not, which Place there toasts; a machine that stands on the ground for good (a junction box) shows neither. The ghost is the machine's footprint centred on cell (0, 0, 0) of the frame it would stand on (0215), red where the drain would refuse it.
- The placement editor (0215, [input.md](input.md), The placement editor): with it on and a machine over the direct placement limit held, the tool line says "Place to anchor the Stone furnace"; anchored, "Placing the Stone furnace: walk round it"; either is replaced by the refusal's text in red (`HUD_REFUSED_TEXT_COLOR`) while the placement would be refused (`placement_editor_tool_line`). The ghost is low walls a cell tall round the footprint's lowest row with an arrow for the front, then, anchored, the machine's model see through ([presentation.md](presentation.md), The field session).
- With a foundation held the tool line names the block Place puts down, "Foundation 5x5, 2 high" (`foundation_block_line`, 0193), chosen in the configure pop-up that Context_Action opens on a foundation highlighted in the inventory view ([ui.md](ui.md), Configure pop-up); the ghost draws every cell of the block, red when the drain would refuse it.
- During a cable run (0368, [input.md](input.md), Bindings) the tool line says the span left from the run's first port to the hand, "Light cable: 12 m of the span left", or past it, "Light cable: 3 m past the span" (`cable_run_line`); the carried cable draws red then ([presentation.md](presentation.md), Cables). A cable's take up draws its progress as a pick up's does.
- A discoverable ore reads "Unknown ore" until its drop was obtained once; the vein line calls its type "Unknown ore" until one of its discoverable ores is. A vein type with no discoverable output (a quarry, the deep bauxite vein) is always named.
- A bar above the crosshair fills while a block is dug, and on the field while Mine is held on a frame cell to pick its entity up (0195, `PICK_UP_SECONDS`, the confirmed tick's progress, never the prediction's); a foundation something stands on gets no bar and toasts "Something stands on it". In the touch tap scheme (0118), which draws no crosshair, a ring around the mined block fills clockwise instead (`draw_mining_ring`, placed through the camera the world was drawn with; on the field at the mined frame cell's centre on its frame, `mining_ring_point`).
- On the field (0296, [input.md](input.md), The jetpack and cheat movement) a thin bar under the crosshair, inside the gap above the first target line (`draw_jet_bar`, `HUD_JET_BAR_HEIGHT`), shows the jetpack's budget while it is below full, read from the predicted body (`hud_field_player`), so the target lines never move; "CHEAT" in the accent colour right of the crosshair on its line (`draw_cheat_tag`) while cheat movement is on. Both draw under a screen too, like the crosshair.
- Placing: no preview cube; the outline shows the target's shape (a slab's half, the half block box a torch aims at, 0061). A slab goes into the upper half against a block's underside or high on a side face, else the lower. Stairs rise away from the player, a quarter turn more per Rotate.
- Ground cover (0082: tufts, flowers, reeds) stops the ray and fills its cell. A block placed at cover replaces it; a machine, belt or pipe aimed at cover stands on the ground under it and clears the cover in its footprint; a belt drag lays over cover. Mining the block under cover drops the cover as a loose item.
- Ghosts (0081): a machine's own model tinted translucent green or red at full brightness, its part at rest, turned by Rotate; a translucent box without a model. A belt ghost is its surface with a white chevron along the flow, following a ramp; an inserter ghost is its arm folded at rest (0175) and outlines its pickup cell dim and its drop cell bright with a chevron over the base; drills, splitters and fluid machines draw their arrows and ports over the model.

## Notices

- **Mission Control** (0069): notices whose key starts with `mc_` go to a panel at the top left, the toasts moving below it. The panel colour with an accent border on the left, the venture's mark (a stepped chevron of fills) and "MISSION CONTROL", then the line typing at 40 characters a second with a blinking cursor, held 4 seconds, faded over half a second.
- Touchdown (0223): the landing gives each player strapped into the pod's chair the Mission Control line "Touchdown confirmed, Contractor. Unbuckle when the dust settles." (`mc_touchdown_confirmed`, from the simulation's `Touchdown_Confirmed` event in `show_simulation_events`, with the chime), not a quest message, so the journal does not keep it.
- The wait (0358): when every connected unit waited in the cradle and the day moved on, each of them gets the Mission Control line "Eight hours waited out in the cradle, Contractor. Back to work." (`mc_wait_finished`, from the `Wait_Finished` event), as the touchdown's.
- Mission Control's lines queue (at most 8, the oldest waiting gives way); a rising chime plays as each starts (`mission_control_chime`). No button acts on a line, since in the world Confirm is also Jump and Mine; its time runs on under a screen, and the journal keeps it.
- **Discovery card** (0052): `item_discovered` shows a card at the top centre for 3 seconds instead of a toast: the icon, "Discovered" dim and the name, with the discovery chime. A second discovery replaces it; under the touch overlay it sits below the layout's top centre buttons. Finished research, the capsule landing, a machine on bare ground that broke down ("Stone furnace broke down", 0201, `log_machine_breakdowns`, told once) and the contract and trade notes stay toasts.
- **Biome banner** (0058): when the biome under the player has changed and stayed changed for 2 seconds, its name fades in below the brownout warning, shows 3 seconds in all and fades out. A world's first biome is announced too; a short visit announces nothing; it pauses under a screen. Not on a field world, whose block column under the block body means nothing (0262).
- **Descent and survey** (0067): the map draws a parachute over the landing pad while a capsule descends. After an orbital survey a satellite crosses the map west to east along the pad's row in 6 seconds, over the pad halfway, and a bright quad crosses the sky along the sun's path in the same time. Both are render state kept with the particles, never read by the simulation; a loaded world starts neither.

## The player's hands

In first person the right arm reaches in from the lower right with the selected stack (0066, 0092): a cube shaped block as a 0.35 block cube with its top, side and bottom tiles, shaded per face and lit like the arm; any other item (torch, slab, stairs, ground cover) as its icon, a small cube in its colour without one; nothing for an empty slot. It chops while mining, swings once on a placement, and draws over the world, so a wall never hides it. In third person the whole body walks, sprints, chops and places, and the head follows the look.
